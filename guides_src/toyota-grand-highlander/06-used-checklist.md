A used Grand Highlander is at most 3 years old, so many are still under Toyota's 3-year, 36,000-mile basic warranty, and every hybrid is young enough for its 10-year battery warranty. That makes it one of the safer used buys in the class. But the Grand Highlander has had more than its share of recalls for a young model, and several of them are serious. Your job is to make sure every one has been done.

## Pick the job first

If you need to tow more than 1,588 kg (3,500 lb), choose the gas model or the Hybrid MAX. If you don't, the Hybrid saves the most fuel. If you want speed, the Hybrid MAX is the only answer. For the lowest price, a 2024 XLE with the airbag recall done is the cheapest way in, and for the newest car with the most choice, look at a 2025 or 2026.

## The years at a glance

| Model year | What it is | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Launch year, in XLE, Limited and Platinum | Curtain airbag recall (24V-461). Instrument panel and camera software recalls (25V-595, 25V-744). Load label recall |
| 2025 | Adds the LE and Hybrid Nightshade | Instrument panel and camera software recalls. Load label recall |
| 2026 | Carries over | Camera software recall. Rear axle carrier recall on cars built April 10–27, 2026 (26V-437). Steering knuckle recall on cars built June 19–26, 2026 (26V-598) |

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **Curtain airbags (24V-461), 2024:** In June 2024, Toyota recalled about 111,700 2024 Grand Highlanders and Grand Highlander Hybrids, along with the Lexus TX, and stopped sales. If a front window is down, the curtain airbag may not deploy as intended in certain crashes, so the vehicle doesn't meet a federal ejection-mitigation standard. Toyota first covered the driver's side and expanded the recall to the passenger side in August 2024. Dealers replace both curtain airbags. This is the most important recall to confirm on any 2024.
- **Instrument panel software (25V-595), 2024–2025:** The instrument panel may start up without showing the speed and some warning lights. The fix is a free software update.
- **Rearview camera software (25V-744), 2024–2026:** About 67,700 gas models and 55,300 Hybrids with the Panoramic View Monitor can show a frozen or blank rear camera image in reverse. The fix is a free software update.
- **Load capacity label (Toyota 26TA07 and 26TB07):** On certain Grand Highlanders, the load carrying capacity modification label may show inaccurate added-weight values. Owners were notified in May 2026.
- **Rear axle carriers (26V-437), 2026:** About 1,510 gas Grand Highlanders and 2,449 Hybrids built from April 10 to 27, 2026, may have forged aluminum rear axle carriers made with too little strength. A carrier can break where it attaches to the toe control arm, which can upset the rear of the vehicle. Dealers check the part codes and replace the carriers if needed.
- **Steering knuckles (26V-598), 2026:** A small number of Grand Highlanders built from June 19 to 26, 2026, part of a 617-vehicle Toyota and Lexus recall, have front steering knuckles from a batch that missed a heat-treatment step. They can crack and break, especially after hitting a pothole or curb, which can cause a loss of steering control.
- **Every year:** New recalls can appear at any time. Run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask a Toyota dealer to check for open campaigns. Ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Body, chassis and cabin

Run a vehicle history report, and look for uneven panel gaps, paint that doesn't match and overspray on rubber seals. Check under the cargo floor for water. On the test drive, turn the steering to full lock in both directions at low speed and listen for clicking or clunking from the front, since some owners have reported noisy CV axles. Make sure the car tracks straight and the steering feels the same in both directions.

Inside, fold and raise every seat, try every USB-C port and pair your phone. Check that the backup camera image doesn't freeze and that the instrument panel shows the speed every time you start the car. On a hybrid, make sure it starts in Ready mode quietly and that no warning lights stay on.

## What is it worth?

We don't publish average prices. They go stale quickly and hide the differences that matter. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same year, grade, powertrain, drive and mileage, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what cars actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you, comparing a listing with similar ones nearby. Expect the Hybrid and Hybrid MAX to carry a premium. A pre-purchase inspection is worth more than any seller's speech about Toyota reliability.

Buying from a private seller, or keeping the car past its factory warranty? Service plan providers to compare include [Chaiz](https://www.chaiz.com), [Endurance](https://www.endurancewarranty.com) and [Olive](https://www.olive.com). Carveyance doesn't rank or recommend them. Read what each one covers and excludes, and see the [FTC's guidance on auto service contracts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/what-know-about-auto-service-contracts-extended-warranty-scams) first.

## Our shortlist

- **Best value:** a 2024–2025 gas XLE with the airbag recall done.
- **Lowest running costs:** a 2025–2026 Hybrid XLE with front-wheel drive.
- **Quickest:** a Hybrid MAX Limited, the cheaper way into 362 hp.
- **Best for towing with hybrid economy:** a Hybrid MAX, rated for 2,268 kg (5,000 lb).
- **Newest:** a 2026, with the axle and knuckle recalls checked by VIN.
