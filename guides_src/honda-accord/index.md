The Accord has been Honda's midsize sedan since 1976, and between 2017 and 2026 Honda sold it in 3 generations with 6 different powertrains. The 2017 car was the last of the ninth generation and the last Accord with a V6 or a coupe body. The tenth generation, sold for 2018–2022, replaced the V6 with a 252-hp turbocharged 2.0-liter and offered a 6-speed manual until 2020. The eleventh generation, from 2023, dropped the 2.0T and made the hybrid the heart of the range, rated at up to 48 mpg.

For a used buyer, the choices are the generation, the engine and how much of the car's history you can prove. This guide has 4 chapters: one for each generation, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2023 or later Accord Hybrid EX-L, with 204 hp and an EPA rating of 48 mpg combined.
- **Best value:** a 2021–2022 Accord Sport or Sport SE with the 1.5-liter turbo. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto are standard, and it's rated at 32 mpg.
- **Most fun:** a 2018–2022 Sport 2.0T or Touring 2.0T, with 252 hp. Car and Driver ran one from 0 to 60 mph in 5.5 seconds.
- **For high-mileage drivers:** a 2019–2022 Accord Hybrid, rated at 47 or 48 mpg combined in most trims.
- **The last V6:** a 2017 Accord V6 sedan or coupe, with 278 hp. The coupe was also sold with a 6-speed manual.
- **Think twice if** you need all-wheel drive. No Accord in this period offers it.
