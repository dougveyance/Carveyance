The 2017 Odyssey was the last of a generation that began in 2010, and Honda changed almost nothing for its final year beyond extending the acoustic windshield to the SE and EX-L. That makes it a known quantity. It has a 248-hp V6 with Variable Cylinder Management, which shuts down cylinders on the highway, and a 6-speed automatic that every Odyssey got from the 2014 refresh. It seats 8, rides comfortably and handles more like a big car than most vans its size.

## The trims

There were 6: LX, EX, SE, EX-L, Touring and Touring Elite, with navigation and rear entertainment available separately on the EX-L. Every one has the same engine and gearbox, and the EPA rated them all at 22 mpg combined. The SE and Touring Elite have HondaVAC, a built-in vacuum cleaner, and the EX-L and above have a cool box chilled by the air conditioning. The Touring Elite adds HID headlights, a 650-watt audio system and a 16.2-inch split-screen rear entertainment system. Prices started at $29,850 for the LX ($41,000 in 2026 dollars) and ran to $45,325 for the Touring Elite ($62,200).

## How it drives

Car and Driver ran a 2015 Touring Elite, the same van, to 60 mph in 7.7 seconds. It's not as quick as the 2018 Odyssey, but it's smooth, and the 6-speed is a conventional automatic without the newer gearboxes' software quirks. The 60/40 third row folds into the floor, and a 2017 can tow 1,588 kg (3,500 lb) when properly equipped.

## What to check before you buy

The important recall is 17V725, which covers 2011–2017 Odysseys: if a second-row outboard seat is reinstalled between its 2 side-to-side positions, it may not latch and can tip forward under braking. Dealers fit a bracket to the floor strikers. A 2017 is also covered by 23V858, the fuel pump campaign, by VIN. The V6's timing belt is due every 7 years or 105,000 miles, so on a 2017 it's due now unless the records say it's done. Test both power sliding doors, the tailgate and the vacuum if it has one. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2017 EX or SE is a good used minivan for less money than a newer Odyssey, provided the timing belt is done, the seat bracket is fitted and the doors work properly. Skip the Touring Elite unless you'll use its entertainment system. If you want Honda Sensing and the newer seats, look at [the 2018–2026 van](../2018-2026/).

::: curiosity A Vacuum in the Van
HondaVAC, in the 2017 SE and Touring Elite, is a vacuum cleaner built into the van itself, so the cabin can be cleaned anywhere, without a trip to the car wash. It's one of the few car features designed entirely around crumbs.
:::
