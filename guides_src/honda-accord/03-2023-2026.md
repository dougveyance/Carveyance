The eleventh-generation Accord went on sale in January 2023, still long, low and unmistakably a sedan while most of its rivals were giving up. Honda didn't try to make it more dramatic. It made the hybrid the center of the range, gave it a calmer, more grown-up cabin and kept building it in Marysville, Ohio.

## Hybrid or turbo

There are 2 powertrains. The LX and EX, and later the SE, use a turbocharged 1.5-liter 4-cylinder with 192 hp and a CVT, rated at 32 mpg combined. Every other trim is a hybrid: a 2.0-liter engine with 2 electric motors and 204 hp, with 247 lb-ft of torque available from a standstill. Car and Driver ran a Hybrid Touring from 0 to 60 mph in 6.5 seconds, about a second slower than the old 2.0T. In everyday driving the hybrid feels quicker than that, because the electric motor answers the throttle at once. The 2.0T, the manual and all-wheel drive are gone, and none of them came back.

The wheels decide the hybrid's fuel economy. The EX-L, on 17-inch wheels, is rated at 48 mpg combined. The Sport, Sport-L and Touring, on 19-inch wheels, are rated at 44. That 4-mpg gap is about 28 gallons a year at 15,000 miles, and the bigger tires cost more to replace.

## The cabin

The LX and EX have a 7-inch touchscreen. The hybrids have a 12.3-inch screen, the largest Honda had used, with a volume knob. The Touring adds Google built-in, with Google Maps, Google Assistant and apps from Google Play, the first Honda to have it, plus a head-up display and a 12-speaker Bose system. The rear seat is generous and the trunk holds 16.7 cubic feet. For 2026, Honda gave the LX and SE a 9-inch touchscreen, wireless Apple CarPlay, Android Auto and phone charging, and the SE moved to 19-inch wheels, which lowered its EPA rating to 31 mpg.

## What to check before you buy

This generation has several recalls, so run the VIN and ask for proof of each repair. The integrated control module on 2023–2025 Hybrids can reset while driving and cut drive power; the fix is a software update (25V785). The high-pressure fuel pump on 2023–2024 cars may crack and leak fuel (24V763). On some 2023–2024 cars, the driver's seat cushion frame may not be tightened (24V859 and 26V054), and the front seat belt pretensioners may be missing a rivet (23V782). On 2023–2026 Hybrids, the tire repair kit's sealant bottle can build pressure and blow its cap off if the nozzle isn't attached properly (26V366); some cars that were repaired got faulty replacement parts and need the fix again (26V663). The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

The Hybrid EX-L is the pick: the best fuel economy, the smaller 17-inch wheels and most of the equipment that matters. A Touring is the one to buy if you want Google built-in and the head-up display. A 1.5-liter LX or EX makes sense only if it's clearly cheaper and you don't drive much. If you want speed more than efficiency, a well-kept [2018–2022 Accord 2.0T](../2018-2022/) is still the quicker car.

::: curiosity The Engine as a Generator
Most of the time, the hybrid Accord's engine doesn't drive the wheels at all. It turns a generator, and an electric motor moves the car. Only at steady highway speeds does a clutch connect the engine directly to the wheels, because that's when driving them directly is more efficient.
:::
