The Insight name has been on 3 very different Hondas. The first, in December 1999, was the first hybrid sold in America: a 2-seat aluminum hatchback with a 3-cylinder engine. The second, for 2010 to 2014, was a 5-door hatchback priced to undercut the Toyota Prius. The third, the one this guide covers, is a compact sedan sold from 2019 to 2022.

The 2019–2022 Insight is built on the same platform as the Civic of the same years, with a 1.5-liter engine and Honda's 2-motor hybrid system making 151 hp, and an EPA rating of up to 52 mpg combined. Honda built it alongside the Civic and CR-V in Greensburg, Indiana, and ended production in June 2022, to concentrate on hybrid versions of its best-selling models.

This guide has 2 chapters: the 2019–2022 Insight, and the checklist to use before you buy, with the recalls and the hybrid checks.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2019–2022 EX, rated at 52 mpg, with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Most comfortable:** a Touring, with leather, a power driver's seat and navigation, rated at 48 mpg.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2019–2021 LX with its recalls done, priced below an EX.
- **Think twice if** you carry bulky things often. The LX and EX trunk holds 15.1 cubic feet, but a hatchback, like a Fit or a Prius, has a bigger opening.
