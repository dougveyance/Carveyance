The 2025 Prologue went on sale in March 2025 with a new front drive unit and the same 85-kWh battery. It made the front-wheel-drive car a little more powerful and every version a little more efficient, and Honda didn't raise the price. The 2026 changed almost nothing mechanically. What changed was the price, and then the Prologue's future.

## More power and range

[[chart:range]]

The front-wheel-drive Prologue now makes 220 hp and 243 lb-ft, up from 212 hp and 236 lb-ft. With all-wheel drive, the total is 300 hp and 355 lb-ft, up from 288 hp. The EPA range rose to 308 miles for the front-drive EX and Touring, 294 for the all-wheel-drive EX and Touring and 283 for the Elite, gains of 10 to 13 miles. Charging is unchanged: up to 150 kW at a DC fast charger, and about 35 minutes from 20 to 80%. The 2026 ratings are the same as the 2025's.

## The prices

The 2025 kept the 2024's prices, from $47,400 for the EX ($49,500 in 2026 dollars) to $57,900 for the Elite ($60,400). The 2026 arrived at dealers in December 2025 at the same $47,400 to start, now with a portable charging kit as standard and a choice of a home charging station with a $1,250 installation credit or a $500 credit toward the car.

On April 1, 2026, Honda cut the price of every 2026 Prologue by $7,500, including cars already in dealer stock. The federal $7,500 tax credit for electric cars had ended on September 30, 2025. The 2026 EX now starts at $39,900, the all-wheel-drive EX at $42,000, the Touring at $44,200 and the Elite at $50,400, plus a $1,495 destination charge. That cut also sets a ceiling on what a used 2024 or 2025 is worth.

## The end of the line

In July 2026, Honda confirmed that Prologue production would end with the 2026 model year, with sales continuing into early 2027 from dealer stock, and said owners would continue to get service, parts and warranty coverage through its dealers. The Prologue's Acura sibling, the ZDX, had already been dropped. Honda sold 33,017 Prologues in the US in 2024 and 39,194 in 2025, but sales fell to 14,530 in the first 10 months of 2026.

## What to check before you buy

2024–2025 Prologues are part of a recall for a rearview camera that can show a distorted or blank image; Honda replaces the camera (26V306). Honda covers the high-voltage battery for 8 years or 100,000 miles. See [the used checklist](../used-checklist/) for the battery and charging checks.

## The verdict

A 2025 or 2026 EX with front-wheel drive is the Prologue to buy: the longest range and the essentials, without the Elite's big wheels. A new 2026 at the April 2026 price is the benchmark for any used one, so a 2024 or 2025 needs to be priced well below it. Choose all-wheel drive if you need the traction, and expect about 14 miles less range.
