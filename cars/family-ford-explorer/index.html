The Ford Explorer family

7 generations and variants in the Index, 1991–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1991–1994

    Ford Explorer (first generation)

    The truck that invented the family SUV, March 1990: Bronco II bones, 4 real doors, an Eddie Bauer badge — and 1.15 million sold in 4 years.

    160 hp9.8 sec 0-6099 mph top1,154,639 built
    4.0 V6, 1991–92 · 4.0 V6, 1993–94 · Explorer Sport
  2. 1995–2001

    Ford Explorer (second generation)

    The best-selling SUV in America every year it existed, peaking at 445,157 in 2000 — and the truck at the center of the Firestone crisis that changed tire law.

    215 hp8.8 sec 0-60105 mph top2,903,080 built
    4.0 OHV V6 · 4.0 SOHC V6 · 5.0 V8 · Explorer Sport 3-door
  3. 2001–2010

    Ford Explorer Sport Trac

    The Explorer with a bed, 2001–10: 4 doors, 4 feet of composite cargo box, and a decade head start on the Honda Ridgeline idea.

    292 hp7.9 sec 0-60108 mph top
    Sport Trac, 2001–05 · Sport Trac, 2007–10
  4. 2002–2005

    Ford Explorer (third generation)

    The ground-up answer to the hard years: independent rear suspension, a third row, a modern V8 — still selling 433,847 in its first year.

    239 hp8.4 sec 0-60105 mph top1,386,086 built
    4.0 SOHC V6 · 4.6 Modular V8
  5. 2006–2010

    Ford Explorer (fourth generation)

    The last body-on-frame Explorer, 2006–10: its best engine and its worst timing, with sales falling from 179,229 to 52,190 as gas and the economy broke the truck-SUV era.

    292 hp7.8 sec 0-60110 mph top508,362 built
    4.0 SOHC V6 · 4.6 Modular V8, 3-valve
  6. 2011–2019

    Ford Explorer (fifth generation)

    The bet that saved the badge, 2011: unibody, front-drive bones, EcoBoost fours — heresy that put the Explorer back over 200,000 sales a year.

    365 hp5.7 sec 0-60115 mph top1,735,873 built
    3.5 Duratec V6 · 2.0 EcoBoost · 2.3 EcoBoost · Explorer Sport, 3.5 EcoBoost
  7. 2020–present

    Ford Explorer (sixth generation)

    Back on rear-drive bones, 2020: a 400-hp ST, a hybrid, a 2025 refresh — and still, 35 years in, over 200,000 Americans a year buy an Explorer.

    400 hp5.5 sec 0-60143 mph top1,257,360 built
    2.3 EcoBoost · 3.3 Hybrid · 3.0 EcoBoost, Platinum · Explorer ST
