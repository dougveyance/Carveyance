The Pilot is Honda's largest SUV, a 3-row family car with a V6 in every version and room for up to 8. It rides on a unibody rather than a truck frame, so it drives more like a big car than a pickup, and since 2007 every one has been built in Lincoln, Alabama. Between 2017 and 2026, Honda sold it in 2 generations. The third generation, through 2022, has a 280-hp V6 and either a 6-speed or a 9-speed automatic, depending on the trim and year. The fourth, from 2023, is bigger, with a new 285-hp V6, a 10-speed automatic and an off-road TrailSport.

For a used buyer, the choices are the generation, the trim, the seating layout and front- or all-wheel drive. This guide has 4 chapters: one for each generation, one on trims and towing, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2023 or later EX-L or Touring with all-wheel drive. More room, a smoother 10-speed and the newest safety equipment.
- **Best value:** a 2019–2022 EX-L, with Honda Sensing standard and the 2019 refresh's updates.
- **Best for 7 or 8 passengers who change their minds:** a 2023 or later Touring or Elite, whose second-row middle seat lifts out and stores under the cargo floor.
- **Best for dirt roads:** a 2023 or later TrailSport, with 8.3 inches of ground clearance and all-terrain tires.
- **Think twice if** you're looking at a 2016–2019 Pilot without proof that its recalls are done, especially the hood latch and, on some engines, the connecting rod bearings.
