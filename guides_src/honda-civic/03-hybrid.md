Honda last sold a Civic Hybrid in 2015, and for its return in 2025 it built a very different car. The old one was slow and frugal. The new one is frugal and quick, with a 2-motor system borrowed from the Accord and CR-V, and Honda calls it the most powerful Civic other than the Type R. It comes as a sedan or a hatchback, in Sport Hybrid and Sport Touring Hybrid grades, and it doesn't plug in.

## How it drives

A 2.0-liter 4-cylinder and 2 electric motors make 200 hp combined and 232 lb-ft of torque, all of it available from a standstill. Most of the time an electric motor drives the front wheels while the engine runs a generator; at steady highway speeds, a clutch connects the engine to the wheels directly. Car and Driver ran a Sport Touring Hybrid from 0 to 60 mph in 6.2 seconds, 0.6 second quicker than the last Civic Si it had tested. It pulls hard from low speeds, and the 2.0-liter gas Civic, at 150 hp, feels slow beside it.

The fuel economy is just as strong. The EPA rates the hybrid sedan at 49 mpg combined (50 city, 47 highway) and the hatchback at 48. The gas-only 2025 Civic LX sedan is rated at 36.

## Sport or Sport Touring

The Sport Hybrid has the hybrid powertrain, 18-inch wheels, heated front seats and a sunroof. The Sport Touring adds leather, power front seats, a 12-speaker Bose stereo, and parking sensors. In 2025 the sedan was priced from $28,750 for the Sport Hybrid ($30,000 in 2026 dollars) and $31,750 for the Sport Touring Hybrid ($33,100), before delivery. On the used market, the difference between them is mostly equipment, so pick the grade by the features you'll use every day.

## What to check before you buy

The hybrid is too new for a long reliability record, so judge each car by its paperwork and an inspection. Check that its recalls are done: the steering gearbox on 2025 Civic Hybrids and Hatchback Hybrids (24V744), and the high-pressure fuel pump on some 2025 Civics and Civic Hybrids (24V763). Have a Honda dealer scan the hybrid system and confirm how much of the hybrid component warranty is left for that VIN. Check the 12-volt battery, because a weak one can stop a healthy hybrid from starting. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

For most people, this is the Civic to buy. It's the quickest everyday Civic, the most efficient and, as a Sport Hybrid, not much more expensive than a well-equipped gas car. Choose the hatchback if you carry bulky things, and the sedan for the extra mpg. If you want a manual and a sharper chassis instead, read [the Si chapter](../si/).

::: curiosity Quicker Than the Prelude
The 2026 Prelude uses the same hybrid powertrain as the Civic Hybrid, in a coupe that costs about $8,500 more than a Civic Sport Touring Hybrid hatchback. In Car and Driver's tests, the Civic Sport Touring Hybrid reached 60 mph in 6.2 seconds, and the Prelude in 6.5.
:::
