The Ridgeline is a midsize pickup unlike any other in America: a unibody, built on the same platform as the Pilot, with a transverse V6, independent suspension at all 4 corners, a lockable trunk under the bed floor and a tailgate that folds down or swings open like a door. It rides and handles more like an SUV than a truck, and it carries and tows enough for most owners, if not for those who tow heavy trailers or go rock crawling.

Between 2017 and 2026, Honda sold one generation, built in Lincoln, Alabama, with a 280-hp V6. The 2017–2020 trucks could be had with front-wheel drive; the 2021 restyle made all-wheel drive standard and gave the truck a squarer, tougher face, and a TrailSport arrived for 2024. In July 2026, Honda confirmed a third-generation Ridgeline within 2 years.

This guide has 3 chapters: the 2017–2020 truck, the 2021–2026 truck, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2024 or later RTL, with the 9-inch touchscreen, wireless phone mirroring and standard all-wheel drive.
- **Best value:** a 2021–2023 Sport or RTL, with the 9-speed and standard all-wheel drive.
- **Best for dirt roads:** a 2024 or later TrailSport, with all-terrain tires and steel underbody protection.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2017–2019 RT or RTL with front-wheel drive, if you don't need traction in snow or mud.
- **Think twice if** you tow more than 2,268 kg (5,000 lb) or need a long bed. The Ridgeline has neither.
