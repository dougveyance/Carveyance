The Toyota C-HR family

2 generations and variants in the Index, 2018–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 2018–2022

    Toyota C-HR (AX10)

    The gas C-HR, sold in the US for 2018–2022: a sharply styled, front-drive small crossover with a 144-hp 2.0-liter and a CVT, rated at 29 mpg. It was meant to be a Scion.

    Toyota C-HR (electric)
