The Honda Civic family

19 generations and variants in the Index, 1973–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1973–1979

    Honda Civic (first generation)

    The first Civic, 1973–1979: a small front-wheel-drive hatchback that reached America just before the 1973 oil crisis and became Honda's first real success as a carmaker.

    50 hp12.9 sec 0-60767,583 built
    1.2-liter · 1.5-liter CVCC
  2. 1980–1983

    Honda Civic (second generation)

    The second-generation Civic, 1980–1983: bigger, squarer and closer to the Accord, with a 4-door sedan in America for the first time from 1981.

    67 hp10.9 sec 0-60563,654 built
  3. 1984–1987

    Honda Civic (third generation)

    The third-generation Civic, 1984–1987: a flat-roofed hatchback, a sedan, a tall wagon you could get with 4-wheel drive, and from 1986 the first Civic Si hatchback sold in America.

    76 hp11.6 sec 0-60849,930 built
    1.5-liter · Si
  4. 1988–1991

    Honda Civic (fourth generation)

    The fourth-generation Civic, 1988–1991: lower and glassier, with double wishbones up front, fuel injection in every US car and the return of the Si.

    92 hp9.6 sec 0-60955,187 built
    1.5-liter · 1.6-liter
  5. 1992–1995

    Honda Civic (fifth generation)

    The fifth-generation Civic, 1992–1995: rounder and lighter, with VTEC in the Si and EX, a new coupe for 1993, and the VX hatchback that topped 40 mpg.

    125 hp7.5 sec 0-601,031,265 built
    1.6-liter VTEC · 1.5-liter · VX
  6. 1996–2000

    Honda Civic (sixth generation)

    The sixth-generation Civic, 1996–2000: the last with double-wishbone front suspension, a CVT in the thrifty HX coupe, and from 1999 a 160-hp Si.

    106 hp8.5 sec 0-601,585,441 built
    1.6-liter · 1.6-liter VTEC · Si
  7. 1997–2000

    Honda Civic Type R (EK9)

    The first Civic Type R, 1997–2000: a stripped, seam-welded Japanese-market hatchback whose hand-finished 1.6-liter made 185 PS (182 hp) and revved past 8,000 rpm.

    182 hp6.7 sec 0-60140 mph top16,000 built
  8. 2001–2005

    Honda Civic (seventh generation)

    The seventh-generation Civic, 2001–2005: roomier inside, simpler underneath, the first Civic Hybrid, and an Si hatchback built in England.

    115 hp9.3 sec 0-601,562,222 built
    1.7-liter · 1.7-liter VTEC · Si · Hybrid
  9. 2001–2005

    Honda Civic Type R (EP3)

    The second Civic Type R, 2001–2005: built in Swindon, England, with a 2.0-liter i-VTEC and a 6-speed, sold in Europe and shipped to Japan in a sharper form.

    197 hp6.5 sec 0-60146 mph top25,798 built
    Europe · Japan
  10. 2006–2011

    Honda Civic (eighth generation)

    The eighth-generation Civic, 2006–2011: a 2-tier dashboard, a 197-hp Si with a limited-slip differential, and the 2006 North American Car of the Year.

    140 hp7.7 sec 0-601,728,197 built
    1.8-liter · Si · Hybrid
  11. 2007–2011

    Honda Civic Type R (FD2 and FN2)

    The third Civic Type R, 2007–2011: 2 different cars, a high-revving sedan for Japan and a heavier hatchback for Europe.

    222 hp6.3 sec 0-6043,101 built
    FD2 sedan · FN2 hatchback
  12. 2012–2015

    Honda Civic (ninth generation)

    The ninth-generation Civic, 2012–2015: planned during the 2008 recession, criticized at launch, and reworked by Honda within a year.

    140 hp9.0 sec 0-601,315,454 built
    1.8-liter · Si · Hybrid · Natural Gas
  13. 2015–2017

    Honda Civic Type R (FK2)

    The fourth Civic Type R, 2015–2017: the first with a turbocharger, 310 PS (306 hp) from a 2.0-liter, and a Nürburgring record for front-wheel-drive cars.

    306 hp5.7 sec 0-6015,978 built
  14. 2016–2021

    Honda Civic (tenth generation)

    The tenth-generation Civic, 2016–2021: a sedan, a coupe through 2020 and, from 2017, an England-built hatchback, with a 158-hp 2.0-liter or a 174- to 180-hp 1.5-liter turbo.

    174 hp6.9 sec 0-60126 mph top1,920,635 built
    1.5-liter turbo · 2.0-liter
  15. 2017–2020

    Honda Civic Si (tenth generation)

    The 2017–2020 Civic Si: a 205-hp turbo sedan or coupe with a 6-speed manual, a limited-slip differential and adaptive dampers, priced from $23,900 ($32,800 in 2026 dollars).

    205 hp6.8 sec 0-60
  16. 2017–2021

    Honda Civic Type R (FK8)

    The first Civic Type R sold new in the US: a 306-hp, manual-only hatchback built in Swindon, England, that Car and Driver ran from 0 to 60 mph in 4.9 seconds.

    306 hp4.9 sec 0-60170 mph top
  17. 2022–present

    Honda Civic (eleventh generation)

    The eleventh-generation Civic, from 2022: a calmer sedan and a US-built hatchback, with a 2.0-liter or 1.5-liter turbo at first and, from 2025, a 200-hp hybrid rated at up to 49 mpg.

    180 hp7.2 sec 0-601,028,074 built
    1.5-liter turbo · 2.0-liter · Hybrid
  18. 2022–present

    Honda Civic Si (eleventh generation)

    The 2022 and later Civic Si: a 200-hp turbo sedan with a rev-matching 6-speed manual and a limited-slip differential, but no adaptive dampers.

    200 hp6.8 sec 0-60
  19. 2023–present

    Honda Civic Type R (FL5)

    The 2023 and later Civic Type R: 315 hp, a 6-speed manual and a calmer body on the eleventh-generation Civic hatchback, built in Yorii, Japan.

    315 hp4.9 sec 0-60171 mph top
