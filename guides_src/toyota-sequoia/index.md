The Sequoia is Toyota's biggest SUV, a 3-row family hauler built on the same bones as the Tundra pickup. Between 2017 and 2026, it came in 2 very different forms. The second generation, sold through 2022, has a 5.7-liter V8, a 6-speed automatic and independent rear suspension that leaves room for a huge cargo hold. The third generation, from 2023, has the Tundra's twin-turbo V6 hybrid, a 10-speed automatic, a solid rear axle and much more torque, but noticeably less space.

Which one is better depends on what you carry. This guide has 6 chapters. Start with the generation you're shopping, read the space chapter if your third row is often full, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Most space:** a 2019–2022 V8 SR5 or Limited. With the seats folded, it holds about 38% more than the new one.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2024–2026 hybrid SR5 or Limited, with its recalls done.
- **Best for towing:** any 2023–2026 hybrid, rated for up to 4,318 kg (9,520 lb).
- **Lowest running costs:** the hybrid, at 20 to 22 mpg combined against the V8's 14 or 15.
- **Think twice if** you rarely tow and mostly drive in town. A [Grand Highlander](/fitdrive/guides/toyota-grand-highlander/) has an adult-size third row, nearly as much space behind it and uses far less fuel.

## At a glance

|  | 2017–2022 V8 | 2023–2026 hybrid |
|---|---|---|
| Engine | 5.7-liter V8 | 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 with an electric motor |
| Transmission | 6-speed automatic | 10-speed automatic |
| Power | 381 hp | 437 hp |
| Torque | 401 lb-ft | 583 lb-ft |
| Rear suspension | Independent | Solid axle on coil springs, air springs available |
| Cargo, behind 3rd / 2nd / 1st row | 18.9 / 66.6 / 120.1 cu ft | 11.5–22.3 / 49.0 / 86.9 cu ft |
| EPA combined, 4x4 | 14 mpg | 20 mpg |
| Tows up to | 3,357 kg (7,400 lb) | 4,318 kg (9,520 lb) |
