The Toyota Prius family

10 generations and variants in the Index, 2001–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 2001–2003

    Toyota Prius (XW10)

    The first Prius, sold in America from 2001 to 2003: the world's first mass-produced hybrid car, launched in Japan in 1997.

    70 hp13.0 sec 0-60123,000 built
  2. 2004–2009

    Toyota Prius (XW20)

    The second Prius, 2004–2009: the liftback that made the hybrid mainstream, Motor Trend's 2004 Car of the Year and European Car of the Year for 2005.

    110 hp10.1 sec 0-601,192,000 built
  3. 2010–2015

    Toyota Prius (XW30)

    The third Prius, 2010–2015: a bigger 1.8-liter engine that used less fuel, plant-based plastics inside, and 50 mpg on the EPA's combined scale.

    134 hp10.0 sec 0-601,688,000 built
  4. 2012–2015

    Toyota Prius Plug-in Hybrid (XW35)

    The Prius Plug-in Hybrid, 2012–2015: a Prius with a larger battery you could charge from the wall, good for about 11 miles on electricity.

    134 hp9.8 sec 0-6042,293 built
  5. 2012–2019

    Toyota Prius c

    The smallest Prius: a subcompact hybrid hatchback with 99 hp and an EPA rating of 46 mpg combined, sold in the US for 2012–2019 and in Japan as the Toyota Aqua.

    99 hp11.2 sec 0-60105 mph top189,633 built
  6. 2012–2017

    Toyota Prius v

    The roomiest Prius: a tall hybrid wagon with a sliding, reclining rear seat and up to 67.3 cubic feet of cargo space, rated at 41 mpg combined. Sold in the US for 2012–2017.

    134 hp10.9 sec 0-60148,079 built
  7. 2016–2022

    Toyota Prius (XW50)

    The fourth-generation Prius, sold in the US for 2016–2022: a 121-hp hybrid hatchback rated at 52 mpg combined, or 56 in the Eco grades, with Toyota Safety Sense P standard from 2017 and electric all-wheel drive from 2019.

    121 hp10.5 sec 0-60114 mph top542,052 built
  8. 2017–2022

    Toyota Prius Prime (XW52)

    The first Prius Prime: a plug-in Prius with its own nose and tail, 25 miles of EPA electric range, 133 MPGe and 54 mpg as a hybrid. It seated only 4 until a fifth seat arrived for 2020.

    121 hp10.3 sec 0-60101 mph top100,000 built
  9. 2023–present

    Toyota Prius (XW60)

    The fifth-generation Prius, from 2023: low, sleek and much quicker than before, with a 194-hp 2.0-liter hybrid, available all-wheel drive and an EPA rating of up to 57 mpg combined.

    194 hp7.1 sec 0-60171,981 built
  10. 2023–present

    Toyota Prius Prime / Plug-in Hybrid (XW60)

    The second plug-in Prius, from 2023: 220 hp, up to 44 miles of electric range and 52 mpg in hybrid mode. Sold as the Prius Prime until 2024 and as the Prius Plug-in Hybrid from 2025.

    220 hp6.6 sec 0-6035,732 built
