The newest Fit is now 6 years old, and many have spent their lives as first cars, student cars and city runabouts, with curb scrapes, short trips and missed services to match. The design is simple and well proven, so a Fit with a clean history and records is a sound buy. The work is in finding that one.

## First, know which one it is

Decode the VIN to confirm the year, trim and gearbox, and check the equipment against the original window sticker if the seller has it. Honda Sensing came on 2018–2020 EX and EX-L models and, for 2018 and 2019 only, as an option on the LX and Sport with the CVT. Apple CarPlay and Android Auto came on the 2018–2020 Sport, EX and EX-L. A seller's listing that says a car has either one is worth checking on the dashboard.

## The years at a glance

| Model year | What it is | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 2017 | LX, EX, EX-L; no Honda Sensing, no CarPlay | Check by VIN |
| 2018 | Update: Sport trim, Honda Sensing, CarPlay and Android Auto, quieter cabin | Fuel pump (23V858). Rearview camera on key-start cars (23V046, 24V384). Passenger seat sensor (26V332) |
| 2019 | Carryover | Fuel pump (20V314, 21V215, 23V858). Rearview camera (23V046, 24V384). Passenger seat sensor (26V332) |
| 2020 | Last year; manual on LX and Sport only; Honda Sensing on EX and EX-L only | Rearview camera (23V046, 24V384). Passenger seat sensor (24V064, 26V332) |

## The Magic Seat

Try every seat position. Flip both rear cushions up and back down, fold both seat backs flat and raise them again, and make sure each part moves freely and latches firmly. A seat that binds or won't latch may have something trapped in the mechanism or a broken part. Look under the cushions and in the footwells for damage from heavy cargo.

## The body and the rest

Look for curb damage to the wheels and the lower bumpers, uneven tire wear, and paint that doesn't match from panel to panel. Check the cargo floor and the spare-wheel well for water, and the hatch and door seals for damage. Drive over rough pavement and listen for knocks from the suspension, and run the air conditioning at idle as well as on the move.

## The gearbox

On a manual, check the clutch: it should take up smoothly, without slipping or juddering, and shifts should be clean. On a CVT, ask for records of fluid changes and check that it pulls away smoothly from cold and when warm. Chapter 2 has the full checks for each.

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **2018–2019:** 23V858, a fuel pump impeller that can deform and stop the pump, which can make the engine stall.
- **2019:** 20V314 and 21V215, low-pressure fuel pumps that may fail, with the same risk of stalling.
- **2018–2020:** 23V046, re-recalled as 24V384, a rearview camera image that may not appear on cars with a conventional key ignition.
- **2020:** 24V064, and **2018–2020:** 26V332, a front passenger seat sensor that may not detect an occupant correctly, which can affect the passenger air bag.
- **Every year:** run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls), ask a Honda dealer for any open campaigns, and ask the seller for proof that each repair was done.

## What it costs to run

The Fit is cheap to fuel: at 12,000 miles a year, an LX with the CVT at 36 mpg uses about 333 gallons, and a manual at 31 mpg about 387. The LX has 15-inch wheels and the other trims 16-inch wheels, so tires for an LX usually cost less. Follow the Maintenance Minder for oil, fluid and filter changes, and keep the receipts.

## Walk away if

The car has a branded title, flood or fire damage, a slipping clutch or a CVT that shudders, a warning light the seller can't explain, a Magic Seat that won't latch, or open safety recalls with no repair date. Walk away from any seller who won't let a mechanic inspect it.

## What is it worth?

[[chart:price]]

We don't publish average prices, because they go stale quickly. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same year, trim and gearbox, with similar mileage and the same title history, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what cars actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you. Cross-shop a Civic, an HR-V or a Toyota Yaris of the same age: if a Fit costs about the same as a better-equipped Civic, decide how much the Magic Seat is worth to you.

Buying from a private seller, or keeping the Fit past its factory warranty? Service plan providers to compare include [Chaiz](https://www.chaiz.com), [Endurance](https://www.endurancewarranty.com) and [Olive](https://www.olive.com). Carveyance doesn't rank or recommend them. Read what each one covers and excludes, especially for the gearbox, and see the [FTC's guidance on auto service contracts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/what-know-about-auto-service-contracts-extended-warranty-scams) first.

## Our shortlist

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2018–2020 EX with the CVT and Honda Sensing.
- **Best to drive:** a 2018 or 2019 Sport or EX manual.
- **Most economical:** an LX with the CVT, at 36 mpg combined.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2017 LX with service records.
