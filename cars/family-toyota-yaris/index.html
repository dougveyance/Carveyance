The Toyota Yaris and Echo family

4 generations and variants in the Index, 2000–2020. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 2000–2005

    Toyota Echo (XP10)

    The Echo, 2000–2005: a tall little Toyota sedan and coupe with its gauges in the middle of the dash, the North American forerunner of the Yaris.

    108 hp8.5 sec 0-60164,299 built
  2. 2007–2011

    Toyota Yaris (XP90)

    The first car sold in America as a Yaris, 2007–2011: a small liftback and sedan with a 106-hp 1.5-liter that replaced the Echo.

    106 hp8.9 sec 0-60393,958 built
  3. 2012–2018

    Toyota Yaris (XP130)

    The last Toyota-designed Yaris sold in the US: a small 3-door or 5-door hatchback with a 106-hp 1.5-liter, a 5-speed manual or a 4-speed automatic and, from 2017, standard Toyota Safety Sense C.

    106 hp10.2 sec 0-60103,450 built
  4. 2016–2020

    Toyota Yaris iA / Yaris (Mazda-built)

    A Mazda2 with Toyota badges: sold as the Scion iA for 2016, the Yaris iA for 2017–2018 and the Yaris Sedan for 2019–2020, joined by a 5-door Yaris Hatchback for 2020. Built by Mazda in Mexico, with a 106-hp 1.5-liter and up to 40 mpg on the highway.

    106 hp8.7 sec 0-60120 mph top96,584 built
