The Honda Passport family

4 generations and variants in the Index, 1994–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

    Honda Passport (first generation)

    The first Passport, 1994–1997: an Isuzu Rodeo with Honda badges, and the first SUV Honda sold in America.
