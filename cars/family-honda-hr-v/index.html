The Honda HR-V family

3 generations and variants in the Index, 1999–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1999–2006

    Honda HR-V (first generation)

    The first HR-V, 1999–2006: a tall, Logo-based hatchback with optional 4-wheel drive, sold in Japan, Europe and Asia but never in North America.

    124 hp10.8 sec 0-60106 mph top
  2. 2016–2022

    Honda HR-V (second generation)

    The second HR-V, 2016–2022: a small crossover built on the Fit's platform, with the Fit's Magic Seat and a 1.8-liter engine, and the first HR-V sold in the US.

    141 hp8.6 sec 0-60738,905 built
    1.8-liter, CVT · 1.8-liter, 6-speed manual
  3. 2023–present

    Honda HR-V (third generation)

    The third HR-V, 2023 on: a North American model built on the 11th-generation Civic, bigger and quieter than before but without the Magic Seat.
