Free to list

Any car with a Carveyance sticker can go on The Lot. The owner sets the price and writes the notes; Carveyance checks each listing before it shows.

Private messages

Buyers message the owner from the car's page. The owner's email and phone stay private until they choose to reply.

Buy smart

See the car in person, check the title and VIN, and never send a deposit to someone you haven't met. Carveyance doesn't handle money between buyers and sellers.

Selling an enthusiast car?

Put a Carveyance sticker on it, fill in its page, then switch on “List this car for sale” in your editor. No sticker yet? Email info@carveyance.com.
