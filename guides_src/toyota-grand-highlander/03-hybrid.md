The Grand Highlander Hybrid is the efficiency pick of the range, and in its class it's a remarkable one. A vehicle this size, with room for 7 or 8 and their luggage, usually returns low-20s mpg. The Hybrid is rated at up to 36. For a family that drives a lot and doesn't tow anything heavy, it's the Grand Highlander to buy.

## What you get

The Hybrid pairs a 2.5-liter 4-cylinder, which makes 187 hp and 177 lb-ft on its own, with electric motors for 245 hp in total. It uses an electronically controlled CVT, and you can have front-wheel drive or all-wheel drive, which adds a third electric motor to drive the rear wheels. Toyota quoted 0 to 60 mph in 7.8 seconds for an all-wheel-drive model.

The Hybrid launched for 2024 in XLE, Limited and Platinum grades. For 2025, Toyota added an LE grade and a Hybrid Nightshade Edition. For 2026, the Hybrid starts at $44,710 for an LE, before Toyota's destination charge, $3,350 more than the gas LE.

## The numbers

[[chart:fuelcost]]

The EPA rates the Hybrid XLE at 36 mpg combined with front-wheel drive and 34 with all-wheel drive, and the heavier Limited with all-wheel drive at 33. Over 12,000 miles a year at $4 a gallon, a front-drive Hybrid uses about $670 less fuel than a front-drive gas model, which pays back the 2026 price gap in about 5 years. The catch is towing. The Hybrid is rated for 1,588 kg (3,500 lb), against 2,268 kg (5,000 lb) for the gas model and the Hybrid MAX.

## How it drives

The Hybrid is the calmest Grand Highlander around town, moving away quietly on electric power. It's also the slowest, and the engine drones when you ask for full power on a highway on-ramp. Some reviewers found it the least engaging version to drive, but for a family hauler, smoothness and economy count for more than speed. If you want hybrid economy and real performance, the Hybrid MAX costs more and uses more fuel, but it's much quicker.

## What to check

- **Recalls shared with the rest of the range:** The 2024 curtain airbag recall covered about 35,000 2024 Grand Highlander Hybrids. About 55,300 2024–2026 Hybrids with the Panoramic View Monitor need a camera software update, and about 2,449 2026 Hybrids built in April 2026 were part of the rear axle carrier recall. The checklist chapter covers each one.
- **Hybrid battery warranty:** Toyota covers the hybrid battery for 10 years or 150,000 miles. Every Grand Highlander Hybrid is young enough to still be covered, mileage permitting.
- **The hybrid system:** On any used hybrid, ask a shop that knows Toyota hybrids to scan for stored codes, and check that the battery cooling intake isn't blocked.

Run the VIN on [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) before you buy.

## Which one to buy

- **Hybrid XLE FWD:** the most efficient Grand Highlander, at 36 mpg.
- **Hybrid XLE AWD:** the all-weather family pick, at 34 mpg.
- **Hybrid Nightshade (2025–2026):** the darker look, with the same economy.
- **Hybrid Limited or Platinum:** more comfort, at a cost of about 1 mpg.
- **Skip it if:** you tow more than 1,588 kg (3,500 lb). Choose the gas model or the Hybrid MAX instead.

## The verdict

The Grand Highlander Hybrid is the best way to carry a big family economically in a Toyota crossover. It isn't fast, and it can't tow as much as the others, but it's quiet, roomy and frugal. For most families, that's exactly the point.
