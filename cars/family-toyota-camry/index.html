The Toyota Camry family

10 generations and variants in the Index, 1980–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1980–1982

    Toyota Celica Camry

    The Celica Camry, 1980–1982: a rear-wheel-drive Japanese sedan dressed to look like a Celica, and the car that gave the Camry its name.

    94 hp12.0 sec 0-60100,000 built
  2. 1983–1986

    Toyota Camry (V10)

    The first Camry, 1983–1986: Toyota's first front-wheel-drive car with a sideways-mounted engine, built to take on the Honda Accord.

    92 hp11.8 sec 0-60426,286 built
  3. 1987–1991

    Toyota Camry (V20)

    The second Camry, 1987–1991: softer, sleeker and better built, the first Camry made in Kentucky, with a V6 and All-Trac all-wheel drive from 1988.

    115 hp9.2 sec 0-601,212,770 built
    2.0-liter · 2.5-liter V6
  4. 1992–1996

    Toyota Camry (XV10)

    The third Camry, 1992–1996: the first wide-body, American-sized Camry, reshaped at the urging of Toyota's US executives and twice on Car and Driver's 10Best list.

    130 hp12.0 sec 0-601,586,296 built
    2.2-liter · 3.0-liter V6
  5. 1997–2001

    Toyota Camry (XV20)

    The fourth Camry, 1997–2001: a sedan-only Camry in America, with a 133-hp 4-cylinder or a 194-hp V6, and the country's best-selling car from 1997 to 2000.

    194 hp7.7 sec 0-602,078,874 built
    3.0-liter V6 · 2.2-liter
  6. 2002–2006

    Toyota Camry (XV30)

    The fifth Camry, 2002–2006: bigger and taller, sedan only, with a sporty SE back in the lineup, and the start of the Camry's long run as America's best-selling car.

    157 hp8.3 sec 0-602,154,579 built
    2.4-liter · 3.0-liter V6
  7. 2007–2011

    Toyota Camry (XV40)

    The sixth Camry, 2007–2011: the first Camry Hybrid, a 268-hp V6 that ran to 60 in under 6 seconds, and the Camry's best US sales year, 2007.

    158 hp8.7 sec 0-601,900,312 built
    2.4-liter · 2.5-liter · 3.5-liter V6 · Hybrid
  8. 2012–2017

    Toyota Camry (XV50)

    The last of the older Camrys: a roomy, quiet front-drive sedan with a choice of 4-cylinder, V6 or hybrid power. Comfortable and sensible rather than exciting, and a generation behind the 2018 car in cabin and safety tech.

    178 hp8.0 sec 0-60112 mph top2,447,027 built
    2.5-liter · 3.5-liter V6 · Hybrid
  9. 2018–2024

    Toyota Camry (XV70)

    The Camry that shook off its dull image: lower, sharper and better to drive, with a 203-hp 4-cylinder, a 301-hp V6 and a 52-mpg hybrid. All-wheel drive arrived for 2020, and so did a TRD version with a rear wing.

    203 hp7.9 sec 0-60133 mph top2,184,285 built
    2.5-liter · 3.5-liter V6 · Hybrid
  10. 2025–present

    Toyota Camry (XV80)

    The first Camry sold only as a hybrid: 225 hp with front-wheel drive or 232 hp with all-wheel drive, which is available on every grade, and up to 51 mpg. Built in Kentucky, it's also the most powerful base Camry yet.

    232 hp6.8 sec 0-60603,627 built
