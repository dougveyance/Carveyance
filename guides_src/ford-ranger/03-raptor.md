Ford had sold a Ranger Raptor in other countries for years. The 2024 redesign finally brought one to America, built alongside the other Rangers in Wayne, Michigan. It isn't a Ranger with bigger tires. Its suspension, engine, ride height and off-road hardware are designed to absorb rough terrain quickly and with control, and the trade-offs are real.

## What the Raptor adds

The Raptor has a 3.0-liter twin-turbo V6 rated at 405 hp and 430 lb-ft, with the 10-speed automatic and 4-wheel drive. It rides on FOX Live Valve internal-bypass shocks, 2.5 inches in diameter, and Ford reinforced the front frame rails, the front shock towers, the rear shock brackets and the suspension mounting points to take the punishment. It has its own body with a "FORD" grille and flared fenders, and its own interior with sport seats and orange accents. Trail Control, an off-road cruise control, is standard.

## Performance

Car and Driver ran a 2024 Ranger Raptor to 60 mph in 5.3 seconds and a 2026 in 5.2, the quickest Rangers on record. The EPA rates it at 17 mpg combined.

## What it gives up

The Raptor tows less than the regular truck: 2,499 kg (5,510 lb), against 3,402 kg (7,500 lb) for a properly equipped 2.3-liter or 2.7-liter Ranger, and it has less payload. Buy it for the experience it was built to deliver. If your main pastime is hauling a loaded trailer or stacking building supplies, the plainer truck is objectively better.

## What it cost

The 2024 Ranger Raptor started at $55,365 before delivery, or $59,400 in 2026 dollars, $22,800 more than an XL.

## Raptor checks

Look hardest at what fast off-road driving punishes: the shocks for leaks and damage, the wheels for bends, the tires for cuts, and the skid plates, frame and suspension mounts for impacts. Check whether it has been lifted or re-geared, and ask for records of shock service. Run the VIN for the recalls that cover 2024–2026 Rangers, including the side curtain air bags (25V541) and the trailer module (26V104).
