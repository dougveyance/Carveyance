Honda revealed the fourth-generation Passport in November 2024, and it went on sale on February 11, 2025, as a 2026 model. It was designed in Southern California, developed in Ohio and is built in Lincoln, Alabama, alongside the Pilot it shares its platform with. The styling is upright and boxy, with a short front overhang and PASSPORT stamped into the nose and the steel tailgate, and Honda aimed it squarely at buyers who actually leave the pavement.

## The drivetrain

[[chart:accel]]

The V6 is a new double-overhead-cam 3.5-liter, rated at 285 hp and 262 lb-ft, with a 10-speed automatic. Every 2026 Passport has i-VTM4 torque-vectoring all-wheel drive, with a stronger rear drive unit than before. Towing is 2,268 kg (5,000 lb) with the tow package. The EPA rates the RTL at 21 mpg combined and the TrailSport at 20 on its all-terrain tires.

The new one is heavier and, on test, slower. Car and Driver ran a 2026 TrailSport Elite, at 2,133 kg (4,702 lb), to 60 mph in 7.1 seconds, where a 2019 Elite took 5.8. That's the cost of a bigger body, off-road tires and extra hardware.

## The trims

The range is RTL, TrailSport and TrailSport Elite, each available with a Blackout package. At launch, the RTL cost $44,750, the TrailSport $48,450 and the TrailSport Elite $52,450, before a $1,450 destination charge. Every version has a 10.2-inch digital instrument panel and a 12.3-inch touchscreen with Google built-in and wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. The cargo area is the biggest a Passport has had: 44 cubic feet behind the rear seat and 83.5 with it folded.

The TrailSport adds all-terrain tires, an off-road suspension tune, steel skid plates and orange recovery hooks. The TrailSport Elite adds TrailWatch, a 360-degree camera system for placing the wheels on rough ground, and TrailSport models show roll, pitch and elevation readouts. The Insurance Institute for Highway Safety named the 2026 Passport a Top Safety Pick+.

## The 2027 TrailSport

The refreshed 2027 Passport began arriving at dealers on September 29, 2026. The TrailSport and TrailSport Elite get a reengineered suspension that raises ground clearance by 1.38 inches, to 9.65 inches, with better approach, departure and breakover angles and more suspension travel, plus bigger fender flares and amber lights in the hood scoop. The TrailSport also gains TrailWatch and a heated steering wheel. Prices start at $45,195 for the RTL and $49,995 for the TrailSport. Honda says TrailSport trims make up more than 80% of Passport sales.

## What to check before you buy

Some 2026 Passports were recalled because the rear subframe bolts may not have been tightened properly (26V424). It's a new design with little long-term history, so ask for the service records and the software updates, and on a TrailSport look underneath for scrapes, dents and bent recovery hooks. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2026 RTL is the best Passport for most buyers: all-wheel drive, the new V6 and the big screen, for the least money. Buy a TrailSport if you'll use the tires and skid plates, and a 2027 TrailSport if you want the lift and TrailWatch. The TrailSport Elite is for people who want every camera and comfort feature.
