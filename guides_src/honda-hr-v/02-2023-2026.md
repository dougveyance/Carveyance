For 2023, Honda gave North America a different HR-V from the rest of the world. Ours is built on the 11th-generation Civic's platform, and elsewhere it's sold as the ZR-V. It went on sale in June 2022 with a new independent rear suspension, a stiffer body and a more upscale cabin than the Fit-based car it replaced. It feels like a small CR-V rather than a tall Fit.

## The engine

[[chart:power]]

Every one has a 2.0-liter 4-cylinder with 158 hp and 138 lb-ft, 17 hp and 11 lb-ft more than before, and a CVT. All-wheel drive cost $1,500 on any trim in 2023 ($1,700 in 2026 dollars). It's still not quick: Car and Driver ran an all-wheel-drive EX-L to 60 mph in 9.4 seconds. The bigger car also uses more fuel than the old one. The EPA rates it at 28 mpg combined with front-wheel drive and 27 with all-wheel drive, against 30 and 29 for a 2022.

## Space

[[chart:cargo]]

There's 24.4 cubic feet behind the rear seat, about the same as before, but only 55.1 with it folded, against 58.8 for a 2016–2022 LX, and there's no Magic Seat. If you carry tall things often, the older car is the more useful one.

## The trims

The range is simple: LX, Sport and EX-L. The Sport adds 18-inch black wheels, black exterior trim and heated front seats; the EX-L adds leather and a larger touchscreen. Prices started at $23,650 for the 2023 LX ($26,100 in 2026 dollars) and $27,450 for the EX-L ($30,300). Honda has adjusted the equipment and prices most years since, so check the window sticker or the VIN for the exact car.

## What to check before you buy

The most important recall is 24V744, which covers 2023–2025 HR-Vs along with many other Hondas: friction inside the steering gearbox can make the steering heavy or sticky, and dealers replace a spring and regrease the gearbox. Some 2023–2024 cars have front seat belt pretensioners missing a rivet (23V782), and some 2024 cars are recalled for driver's seat fasteners (24V859, 26V054). The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2023 or later Sport or EX-L is the better car to drive and to sit in, and the better choice for most buyers if the steering recall is done. Choose the older HR-V if you value the Magic Seat and the extra cargo space with the seat folded, or want to spend less. Either way, take all-wheel drive only if you need it.
