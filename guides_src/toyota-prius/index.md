The Prius is the car that made hybrids ordinary in America, and between 2017 and 2026 Toyota sold it in 6 forms. The regular Prius covers 2 generations: the fourth generation, sold through 2022 and rated at up to 56 mpg, and the redesigned 2023 car, which went from 121 hp to 194 hp and is rated at up to 57 mpg. The plug-in Prius Prime could run 25 miles on electricity for 2017 and 44 miles for 2023, and Toyota renamed it the Prius Plug-in Hybrid for 2025. Toyota also ended 2 spinoffs in this period: the small Prius c after 2019 and the wagon-shaped Prius v after 2017.

For a used buyer, the choices are the generation, whether to plug in, and how much hybrid battery warranty is left. This guide has 5 chapters: one for each generation of the regular Prius, one for the plug-ins, one for the Prius c and Prius v, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2023 or later Prius LE, rated at 57 mpg combined, with 194 hp and 10 years of hybrid battery coverage.
- **Best value:** a 2020–2022 Prius LE or XLE with records. Apple CarPlay is standard, and the hybrid battery is covered for 10 years or 150,000 miles.
- **Best for short commutes:** a 2023 or later Prius Prime SE or Plug-in Hybrid SE, with up to 44 miles of electric range.
- **Best in snow:** a 2019–2022 Prius AWD-e, or a 2023 or later Prius with all-wheel drive.
- **Most cargo space:** a 2017 Prius v, with up to 67.3 cubic feet behind the front seats.
- **Think twice if** you carry tall adults in the back. The 2023 Prius has less rear headroom than the car it replaced, and a 2017–2019 Prius Prime seats only 4.
