The Porsche 911 family

12 generations and variants in the Index, 1973–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1973–1974

    Porsche 911 Carrera RSR (1973–1974)

    The race car the Carrera RS 2.7 was built to make legal. Porsche had to sell 500 road cars to race it, sold 1,580, and the RSR went on winning in America and Europe for years.

    300 hp5.6 sec 0-60174 mph top55 built
  2. 1975–1989

    Porsche 911 Turbo (930)

    The first 911 Turbo: wide fenders, a whale tail and a reputation. Porsche built it so it could race a turbocharged 911, and ended up selling 21,589.

    234 hp4.9 sec 0-60165 mph top21,589 built
    3.0-liter · 3.3-liter, US market · 3.3-liter, 5-speed
  3. 1978–1983

    Porsche 911 SC

    The 3.0-liter air-cooled 911 that carried Porsche through years when its own leadership expected the line to end. Light, unassisted and honest, it is the classic 911 that still asks to be driven rather than displayed, and a rented, privately entered 3.0-liter 911 won the 1978 Monte Carlo Rally.

    178 hp6.3 sec 0-60141 mph top58,914 built
  4. 1986–1988

    Porsche 959

    Porsche's 1980s technology demonstrator: twin turbos, all-wheel drive, adjustable suspension and 197 mph. Porsche lost money on every one of the 292 it sold.

    444 hp3.6 sec 0-60197 mph top292 built
  5. 1989–1994

    Porsche 911 (964)

    The 911 rebuilt underneath for the 1990s: all-wheel drive, power steering and ABS under a body that still looked like the 1960s original. It also brought the RS America, the light, plain 911 Porsche made for American drivers.

    247 hp4.8 sec 0-60162 mph top63,762 built
    Carrera 2 · Carrera 4 · Turbo 3.3 · Turbo 3.6 · Carrera RS · Carrera RS 3.8
  6. 1994–1998

    Porsche 911 (993)

    The last air-cooled 911, and for many people the best looking of all of them. It was faster and smoother than the 964, and the twin-turbo, all-wheel-drive Turbo made 400 hp.

    270 hp4.7 sec 0-60168 mph top68,029 built
    Carrera · Carrera 4S · Carrera S · Turbo
  7. 1997–2005

    Porsche 911 (996)

    The first water-cooled 911, and for years the one purists loved to mock for its Boxster parts and teardrop headlights. It was also the car that rebuilt the 911 for the modern era and set the template every later generation has followed.

    296 hp5.2 sec 0-60174 mph top175,262 built
    3.4-liter · 3.6-liter
  8. 1999–2005

    Porsche 911 GT3 (996)

    The first GT3 Porsche sold in America and the blueprint for every GT3 since: a naturally aspirated flat-6 that revs to 8,200 rpm, a 6-speed manual and an engine hung out behind the rear axle. More than 20 years later, it still runs with sports cars a generation younger.

    380 hp4.0 sec 0-60190 mph top4,863 built
  9. 2016

    Porsche 911 R (991)

    A GT3 RS with no wing, no roll cage and a 6-speed manual: the 911 Porsche built for people who wanted to change gear themselves. It cost $184,900, and 991 were made.

    500 hp3.4 sec 0-60200 mph top991 built
  10. 2021–present

    Porsche 911 GT3 (992)

    The 992-generation GT3: a 502-hp, 9,000-rpm flat-6, front suspension from Porsche's race cars and a choice of wing or no wing. It started at $161,100.

    502 hp2.7 sec 0-60197 mph top
    GT3, PDK · GT3 Touring · GT3, 6-speed manual
  11. 2023–2024

    Porsche 911 Dakar

    A 911 raised 50 mm and fitted with all-terrain tires, built to honor the 911 that won the 1984 Paris–Dakar. Porsche made 2,500.

    473 hp2.9 sec 0-60150 mph top2,500 built
  12. 2023–present

    Porsche 911 GT3 RS (992)

    The most extreme road-legal 911 Porsche had made, with a wing taller than its roof, a drag-reduction system like a Formula One car's and a 6:49 lap of the Nürburgring.

    518 hp2.7 sec 0-60184 mph top
