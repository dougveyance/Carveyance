The Camry is the car people buy when they want to stop thinking about cars. By 2020 it had been America's best-selling passenger car for 17 years running, and the reputation behind that streak is mostly deserved. But "a Camry" covers a lot of ground. Between 2017 and 2026 Toyota sold 3 generations, a 4-cylinder, a 301-hp V6, 2 generations of hybrid, a TRD model with a rear wing and, from 2025, a Camry that's a hybrid whether you asked for one or not.

For a shopper, the story splits 3 ways. The 2017 car is the last of the seventh generation. The 2018–2024 car is the modern one, with a choice of 4-cylinder, V6 or hybrid. And the 2025–2026 car is hybrid-only, with all-wheel drive available on every grade. This guide has 6 chapters. Start with the generation you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a documented 2021–2023 LE or SE 4-cylinder. The 2021 update brought Toyota Safety Sense 2.5+ and a new screen to a car that already had Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Best for high mileage:** a 2021–2024 Hybrid LE, rated at 52 mpg, if the asking price isn't far above a comparable 4-cylinder.
- **Most fun:** a 2018–2024 V6, especially the 2020–2024 TRD. Toyota doesn't make a Camry like it anymore.
- **Newest:** a 2025–2026 LE or XLE. Choose AWD only if your weather needs it.
- **Think twice if** you haul tall, bulky things every week. A sedan's trunk is still a sedan's trunk, and a RAV4 Hybrid trades some mileage for a hatch.

## At a glance

| | 2017 | 2018–2024 gas | 2018–2024 hybrid | 2025–2026 hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Engine | 2.5-liter 4-cylinder (3.5-liter V6 and hybrid also sold) | 2.5-liter 4-cylinder or 3.5-liter V6 | 2.5-liter 4-cylinder with electric motors | 2.5-liter 4-cylinder with electric motors |
| Transmission | 6-speed automatic | 8-speed automatic | Electronically controlled CVT | Electronically controlled CVT |
| Power | 178 hp | 203 hp (206 XSE), V6 301 hp | 208 hp | 225 hp, 232 hp AWD |
| Drive | Front | Front; AWD on 4-cylinders, 2020–2024 | Front | Front or AWD |
| EPA combined, best version | 27 mpg | 32 mpg (V6 26) | 52 mpg | 51 mpg |
