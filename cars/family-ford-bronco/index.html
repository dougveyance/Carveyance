The Ford Bronco family

8 generations and variants in the Index, 1966–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1966–1977

    Ford Bronco (first generation)

    The original Bronco, 1966–1977: Ford's own small 4x4, built on a chassis of its own as a wagon, a half-cab pickup or an open roadster.

    105 hp225,585 built
  2. 1978–1979

    Ford Bronco (second generation)

    The big Bronco, 1978–1979: a shortened F-100 with V8 power, held back by the fuel crisis and sold for just 2 model years.

    161 hp13.7 sec 0-60181,955 built
  3. 1980–1986

    Ford Bronco (third generation)

    The third Bronco, 1980–1986: smaller and lighter, on the new F-Series, with independent front suspension and a 6-cylinder as standard.

    160 hp14.1 sec 0-60322,429 built
    5.8-liter V8 · 4.9-liter 6-cylinder, manual · 5.0-liter V8, automatic
  4. 1987–1991

    Ford Bronco (fourth generation)

    The fourth Bronco, 1987–1991: a smoother nose, fuel injection and rear anti-lock brakes on the same proven platform.

    185 hp10.0 sec 0-60235,451 built
    5.0-liter V8, automatic · 4.9-liter 6-cylinder, 5-speed manual · 5.8-liter V8, automatic
  5. 1992–1996

    Ford Bronco (fifth generation)

    The last of the original line, 1992–1996: a rounder Bronco on the 1992 F-150, with a driver's air bag from 1994, replaced by the Expedition.

    162,703 built
    5.0-liter V8, automatic · 5.8-liter V8, automatic
  6. 2021–present

    Ford Bronco (sixth generation)

    The Bronco's return, 2021 on: a body-on-frame 4x4 with removable doors and roof, 2 or 4 doors and, for those who row their own, a 7-speed manual.

    330 hp6.2 sec 0-60512,924 built
    2.7-liter V6, 10-speed automatic · 2.3-liter, 10-speed automatic · 2.3-liter, 7-speed manual
  7. 2021–present

    Ford Bronco Sport

    The smaller Bronco, 2021 on: an Escape-based crossover with an upright body, standard 4-wheel drive and, in the Badlands, real trail hardware.

    181 hp8.2 sec 0-60594,386 built
    1.5-liter turbo 3-cylinder · 2.0-liter turbo 4-cylinder
  8. 2022–present

    Ford Bronco Raptor

    The desert Bronco, 2022 on: 418 hp, FOX shocks, 37-inch tires and 85.7 inches of width, and the quickest Bronco Car and Driver has tested.

    418 hp5.6 sec 0-60
