Most Ridgelines are bought by people who want a pickup bed without a pickup's ride, and many have spent their lives carrying bikes, groceries and camping gear. Some have towed boats and campers to their limit. The way to tell is not to take it through mud, but to check the bed, the hitch, the records and the recalls.

## First, know which one it is

Decode the VIN to confirm the year, trim and drivetrain. A 2017–2020 Ridgeline may have front-wheel drive, and from 2017 to 2019 it has the 6-speed; a 2020 has the 9-speed. Every 2021 and later Ridgeline has all-wheel drive and the 9-speed. A 2024 or later has the 9-inch screen, and a TrailSport has all-terrain tires and a steel oil-pan guard.

## The bed and tailgate

Open the Dual-Action Tailgate both ways, several times, and check that it latches firmly each time. Open the In-Bed Trunk, look for water or a blocked drain, and check that the spare tire and jack are there. Back up and watch that the rearview camera works with the tailgate both open and closed; broken camera wiring at the tailgate is a common recall item. Look at the bed floor for heavy damage and at the tie-down cleats.

## Towing and hauling checks

On a truck with a hitch, ask what it towed and how often. Check the hitch and wiring, and look for records of transmission fluid changes; towing works the gearbox hard. On a front-drive 2017–2020 truck, remember the rating is 1,588 kg (3,500 lb), not 2,268 kg (5,000 lb). Check the rear tires and suspension for signs of overloading.

## Engine checks

The V6 has a timing belt, due every 7 years or 105,000 miles, usually with the water pump; ask for proof it was done. Listen from cold for a knock, especially on a 2017 or 2019 truck under the connecting rod bearing recall. Look for oil leaks, and on an all-wheel-drive truck, ask for the rear differential fluid records and make a few tight, slow turns to listen for groaning.

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **2017:** 16V888. Water can get into the rear wiring on some all-wheel-drive trucks.
- **2017–2019:** 19V053. Acidic car-wash soap can crack the fuel pump's feed port and leak fuel. Dealers replace the pump if needed and fit a cover. 22V867: opening and closing the tailgate can break the rearview camera wiring.
- **2017 and 2019 (some engines):** 23V751. A connecting rod bearing may wear and seize.
- **2017–2020:** 21V932. The hood latch striker can separate, letting the hood open while driving. Also 23V858, the fuel pump campaign, by VIN.
- **2017–2023, sold in 22 salt-belt states and DC:** 26V365. The rear subframe can corrode at the suspension mounts; Honda installs a reinforcement kit.
- **2018:** 18V221, the power front seats. **2019:** 18V664 (seat belt pretensioner), 19V298 and 18V848.
- **2020–2021:** 23V174, heated mirror pads, and 24V064, the passenger seat weight sensor. 26V332 covers the same sensor on 2017–2021, 2023 and 2025 trucks.
- **2020–2023:** 23V458. A brake master cylinder fastener may not be secured.
- **2020–2024:** 24V321. The rearview camera's tailgate wiring can fatigue and break.
- **2021:** 21V900, a rear seat belt retractor. **2023:** 24V184, a steering gearbox ball joint that may loosen.
- **2017–2020 accessory tonneau covers:** 20E068, Honda's genuine tonneau cover.
- **Every year:** run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Walk away if

The truck has a branded title, flood damage or a musty smell you can't explain, or a bent or rusted rear subframe. Walk away from a transmission that slips or flares, an engine knock the seller can't explain, a tailgate that won't latch, and any seller who won't allow an independent inspection.

## What is it worth?

We don't publish average prices. They go stale quickly, and an average that mixes a 2017 RT with a 2025 TrailSport tells you very little. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same years, trim, drive and mileage, with the same title history, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what trucks actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you. Cross-shop a Toyota Tacoma or Chevrolet Colorado of the same age, and take the cost of a due timing belt, tires and brakes off the price.

## Our shortlist

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2024 or later RTL.
- **Best value:** a 2021–2023 Sport or RTL.
- **Best for dirt roads:** a 2024 or later TrailSport.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2017–2019 RTL with its fuel pump and camera recalls done.
