The Chevrolet Corvette family

4 generations and variants in the Index, 1953–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1953–1962

    Chevrolet Corvette (C1)

    America's sports car, attempt one: 300 hand-laid fiberglass roadsters in 1953, all Polo White, all 6-cylinder automatics — saved by the V8 in 1955 and finished as a 360-hp fuel-injected weapon.

    270 hp69,015 built
    Blue Flame six · 265 and 283 V8 · Fuel injection · 327
  2. 1990–1995

    Chevrolet Corvette ZR-1 (C4)

    King of the Hill: a Lotus-designed, Mercury Marine-built 32-valve V8 in a Corvette, 4.6 seconds to 60 in 1990. The $27,016 option nearly doubled the car's price, and 6,939 found buyers.

    375 hp4.6 sec 0-60176 mph top6,939 built
    ZR-1, 375 hp · ZR-1, 405 hp
  3. 2001–2004

    Chevrolet Corvette Z06 (C5)

    The C5 Z06 was the lightest, sharpest Corvette of its generation, and for 2002 its LS6 V8 climbed to 405 hp. Balanced, talkative and easy to drive at the limit, it showed that a Corvette could win with finesse rather than brute force.

    405 hp4.2 sec 0-6028,388 built
  4. 2027–present

    Chevrolet Corvette Grand Sport

    Chevrolet brings back its most storied badge on the mid-engine Corvette, pairing a naturally aspirated 6.7-liter V8 and 535 hp with the wide-body look. It is built for the driver who wants one car for back roads, long weekends and the occasional track day, and it makes a strong case that anything more would be excess.

    535 hp3.3 sec 0-60
