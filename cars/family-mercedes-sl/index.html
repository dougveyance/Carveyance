The Mercedes-Benz SL family

3 generations and variants in the Index, 1974–2011. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1974–1985

    Mercedes-Benz 280 SL (R107)

    The 6-cylinder European version of Mercedes-Benz's long-lived R107 roadster, never sold new in America. Free of federal bumpers and lighter on its feet than the big V8 SLs, it trades straight-line shove for a sweeter, more alert drive.

    182 hp10.1 sec 0-60127 mph top25,436 built
  2. 1989–2001

    Mercedes-Benz SL500 (R129)

    Mercedes-Benz's 1989 SL turned a luxury roadster into an engineering showcase, with a pop-up roll bar, seat-mounted belts, a power top and, in the SL500, a quad-cam 5.0-liter V8. Heavy, quiet and almost impossible to ruffle at speed, it has aged into one of the most admired open cars of its decade.

    315 hp6.3 sec 0-60155 mph top213,089 built
  3. 2001–2011

    Mercedes-Benz SL500 (R230)

    The SL that folded its steel roof into its trunk: shown at Frankfurt in 2001, sold for a decade, and offered all the way up to a 661-hp V12 Black Series. The SL 55 AMG was the fastest Mercedes of its day.

    302 hp6.1 sec 0-60155 mph top
    SL500 · SL 55 AMG · SL600 · SL 65 AMG
