Toyota hadn't sold a Supra in America since the 1990s when it brought the name back for 2020. The fifth-generation car was developed alongside the BMW Z4, built by Magna Steyr in Graz, Austria, and powered by BMW engines. It ran from the 2020 to the 2026 model year, and production ended in spring 2026.

For a shopper, the story splits 3 ways. The 2020 3.0 has 335 hp. The 2021–2026 3.0 has 382 hp and a retuned chassis, and from 2023 it could be ordered with a 6-speed manual. And for 2021 through 2024, Toyota also sold the 2.0, a lighter, cheaper 4-cylinder that's easy to overlook. This guide has 3 chapters. Start with the review, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a documented 2021–2022 3.0 automatic. It has the 382-hp engine without the premium a manual or a last-year car tends to carry.
- **Best to drive:** a 2023–2026 3.0 manual. Not the quickest Supra, but the most involving.
- **Lightest and cheapest to run:** the 2021–2024 2.0, as long as the price is clearly lower than a comparable 3.0.
- **Think twice if** you need good visibility, cargo space or an easy cabin to climb into every day. A GR86 is more fun at legal speeds, and a Nissan Z gives you more room.
