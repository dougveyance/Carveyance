Full-size trucks are sold like luxury goods now, with leather, wood and big screens at the top of the range. None of that helps a Tundra haul mulch. Decide what the truck has to do, then pick the grade, and remember that every luxury feature adds weight that comes straight out of the payload.

## The grades

| Grade | Years | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| SR | 2017–2026 | Work truck. Fewest features, lightest weight |
| SR5 | 2017–2026 | The volume seller, and usually the best value |
| TRD Sport | From 2018, second generation | Street-focused styling and suspension |
| Limited | 2017–2026 | Comfort. Leather from 2026 |
| Platinum | 2017–2026 | Luxury, in a modern style |
| 1794 Edition | 2017–2026 | Luxury, in a western-ranch style with saddle-brown leather and wood |
| TRD Pro | 2017, 2019–2026 | Off-road suspension (Fox shocks from 2019), skid plate. Hybrid only from 2022 |
| Capstone | 2022–2026 | The flagship. Hybrid only |

## TRD Pro or 1794 Edition?

They're aimed at different owners. The TRD Pro is for rough roads and trails, with Fox shocks, off-road tires and underbody protection, and from 2026 it can have the Tacoma's IsoDynamic seats. Those tires and shocks cost some quiet and some fuel on the freeway. The 1794 Edition is a long-distance cruiser with a western-themed cabin. For a contractor or a boat owner, a well-specified SR5 or Limited with the right tow equipment is often a better buy than either.

## Towing: the headline is only the start

[[chart:tow]]

Toyota rated the 2017 Tundra at up to 4,763 kg (10,500 lb), on a 4x2 Regular Cab with the 5.7-liter V8. For 2020, under the SAE J2807 standard, it quoted up to 4,627 kg (10,200 lb). The 2022–2026 truck is rated at up to 5,443 kg (12,000 lb), with a maximum payload of 880 kg (1,940 lb).

Those peaks belong to specific, lightly equipped trucks. A loaded CrewMax with leather, a sunroof and a hybrid battery will be rated well below them. Before you buy for towing:

- Read the door-jamb label for the truck's payload, and the owner's manual or Toyota's towing guide for its exact tow rating.
- Remember that the trailer's tongue weight, usually 10% to 15% of the trailer's weight, comes out of the payload, along with passengers, a bed full of gear and any accessories.
- Check the hitch class, the axle ratio and the gross combined weight rating, not just the trailer number.
- Test the trailer brake controller, the wiring and, on newer trucks, the towing cameras.

A weight-distributing hitch improves stability. It doesn't raise the truck's ratings.

## Our picks

- **Work truck:** an SR or SR5 with the tow package.
- **Best value:** an SR5 or Limited, either generation.
- **Towing near the limit:** a 2022–2026 gas V6 with the right axle ratio, checked against the door label.
- **Comfort:** a Platinum or 1794 Edition. The 2022–2026 versions ride far better.
- **Off-road:** a TRD Pro, if you'll use it.

::: curiosity What 1794 Means
The 1794 Edition is named after a ranch, not a battle or an engine. Toyota built its San Antonio truck plant on land bought from what's described as one of the oldest working cattle ranches in Texas, founded in 1794 by the Spanish colonist Juan Ignacio de Casanova. The ranch theme carries into the cabin, with saddle-brown leather and wood trim. The Sequoia borrowed the name for 2025.
:::
