The RAV4 Prime does 2 jobs. Plug it in at night and it can cover a typical day's driving on electricity alone. Run the battery down and it carries on as a 38-mpg hybrid. And when you press the accelerator hard, it's the quickest RAV4 of its generation by a wide margin. For the right owner, it's the best RAV4 Toyota has built.

## What you get

The Prime arrived for 2021, at dealers in the summer of 2020. It uses a retuned version of the RAV4 Hybrid's 2.5-liter engine, still making 176 hp on its own, with more powerful electric motors and an 18.1-kWh battery under the floor. Together they make 302 hp. Toyota said it reached 60 mph in 5.7 seconds, which made it the quickest 4-door model Toyota sold at the time, and Edmunds later measured 5.9 seconds. All-wheel drive is standard, with a rear electric motor, as on the hybrid.

The EPA rated it at 42 miles on electricity, 94 MPGe combined and 38 mpg once the battery is depleted. A heat pump climate control system, adapted from the Prius Prime, helps the battery last longer in cold weather. The Prime came as an SE, starting at $38,100, about $47,400 in 2026 dollars, or an XSE at $41,425. For 2025, Toyota renamed it the RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid. The car itself was largely unchanged.

## How it drives

The Prime feels like a different animal from the other RAV4s. In electric mode it's silent and smooth, with strong pull from a standstill. With the engine running and the battery charged, it surges forward in a way no other compact Toyota SUV of the time could match. It's also the most expensive RAV4 of its generation by a long way.

## What to check

- **Charging and battery health:** Ask how the car was charged and test the charging cable that comes with it. Check the electric range shown on a full charge. Toyota covers the high-voltage battery for 10 years or 150,000 miles, and the coverage transfers to the next owner.
- **Hybrid system software:** In early 2023, Toyota recalled 16,679 2021 RAV4 Primes built between November 25, 2019, and June 24, 2021. If the car is driven in electric mode in cold weather, the battery could drop below a set limit and the hybrid system could shut down, cutting drive power. The fix is a free software update.
- **Rear motor cable corrosion (programs 22TE09 and 25TD01):** The same cable issue as the RAV4 Hybrid applies to the Prime. Program 22TE09 covers 2021–2022 Primes, and limited service campaign 25TD01 covers about 86,650 2021–2024 Primes with an inspection and a protector seal. Owners who have the seal fitted get 10 years of coverage on the cable with no mileage limit.
- **Roof rail leaks (program 22TE05):** About 2,000 2021 Primes are included in the roof rail clip seal program. Check for water stains on the headliner and damp carpets.
- **Instrument panel and camera software (25V-595 and 25V-744):** Certain 2023–2024 Primes and 2025 Plug-in Hybrids may start up without the speedometer and some warning lights showing, and about 17,300 2023–2025 cars with the Panoramic View Monitor can show a frozen or blank rear camera image. Both fixes are free software updates.

Run the VIN on [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask a Toyota dealer to check for open campaigns too.

## Which one to buy

- **SE:** the value pick, with the same powertrain as the XSE.
- **XSE:** more equipment and a nicer cabin.
- **2023 or newer:** worth finding for the newer multimedia screen and digital cluster.
- **Skip it if:** you can't charge at home or at work. A RAV4 Hybrid costs less and does almost as well on gas alone.

## The verdict

The RAV4 Prime is the RAV4 for the buyer who wants electric driving without range worries and doesn't mind paying for it. Plug it in every night and it will reward you every morning.

::: curiosity Toyota's Quickest Family Car
When the RAV4 Prime went on sale, Toyota said it was the quickest 4-door vehicle in its US lineup, which put it ahead of every Camry and Avalon. Its successor goes further still. Edmunds measured the 2026 RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid GR Sport at 5.6 seconds to 60 mph, and with the GR Supra gone, it called the GR Sport the quickest gas-powered Toyota with an automatic transmission.
