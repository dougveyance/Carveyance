The C-HR was supposed to be Scion's first crossover. Scion showed it as a concept at the 2015 Los Angeles Auto Show, then closed in 2016, and the production car arrived for 2018 wearing a Toyota badge. Toned down a little from the concept, it still looked like no other Toyota. It shares its platform with the 2016 Prius, and Toyota tuned its handling on the Nürburgring. The 2018–2020 cars sold in North America were built in Turkey.

## How it drives

Every US C-HR has a 2.0-liter 4-cylinder with 144 hp and 139 lb-ft, a CVT and front-wheel drive; all-wheel drive was never offered here. The chassis is the strong point, and Edmunds called it a dynamically capable little crossover. The engine is the weak point. Car and Driver needed 11.0 seconds to reach 60 mph, and Consumer Reports called it among the slowest vehicles in its ratings, so plan freeway merges with care.

The EPA rates it at 29 mpg combined (27 city, 31 highway). Car and Driver lists 19 cubic feet of cargo space behind the rear seat.

[[chart:zero60]]

## The years and grades

The 2018 came in 2 grades, the XLE at $22,500 ($30,100 in 2026 dollars) and the XLE Premium at $24,350, both with Toyota Safety Sense P, including automatic emergency braking and full-speed adaptive cruise control. For 2019, Toyota reorganized the range into LE, XLE and Limited grades, cut the starting price to $20,995 ($27,600 in 2026 dollars) for the LE, and made an 8-inch touchscreen with Apple CarPlay standard. The 2020 refresh brought a new front end, standard LED headlights and Android Auto, and the Limited gained adaptive headlights and an 8-way power driver's seat. A blacked-out Nightshade Edition arrived for 2021, and Toyota ended US sales after 2022, leaving the Corolla Cross as its small crossover.

## What to check before you buy

Run the VIN for the recalls: the electronic parking brake on 2018 cars, rear hub bolts on some 2019 cars, rear center seat belts on some 2019–2020 cars, and the forward collision radar on some 2021 cars. Check that the electronic parking brake applies and releases cleanly. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A gas C-HR makes sense as a stylish, efficient city car for 1 or 2 people, and it's one of the cheapest ways into a late-model Toyota crossover. A 2020–2022 XLE is the best of them, with the LED headlights and both phone systems. If you need all-wheel drive, more power or a roomier back seat, look at a Corolla Cross, or at [the electric C-HR](../2026-electric/) if your budget stretches.

::: curiosity Coupe High-Rider
Toyota says C-HR stands for Coupe High-Rider. The name was ready before the brand was: the car was first shown as a Scion, and only became a Toyota in the US after Scion closed.
:::
