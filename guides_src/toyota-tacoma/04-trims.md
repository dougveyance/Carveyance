Tacoma grade names read like a ladder, but they're really a menu. A TRD Sport is built for pavement, a TRD Off-Road for trails and a TRD Pro for owners who will actually use expensive shocks. Equipment moved between grades over the years, so trust the window sticker or the build sheet, not the decal on the bed.

## The 2017–2023 grades

| Grade | What it's for | Key hardware |
|---|---|---|
| SR | Work truck, lowest price | 4-cylinder standard, V6 available. 7-inch screen from 2020 |
| SR5 | The volume seller | More equipment. 10-way power seat with the V6 from 2020 |
| TRD Sport | Street and gravel | Sport-tuned shocks, 17-inch wheels |
| TRD Off-Road | Trails | Bilstein shocks, locking rear differential, Crawl Control and Multi-Terrain Select with the automatic |
| Limited | Comfort | The most comfort equipment, Panoramic View Monitor from 2020 |
| TRD Pro | Serious off-road | Fox internal-bypass shocks, front skid plate, 1-inch lift. V6 only |

## The 2024–2026 grades

| Grade | What it's for | Key hardware |
|---|---|---|
| SR | Work truck | 228-hp turbo, leaf springs, 8-inch screen |
| SR5 | The volume seller | 278-hp turbo. XtraCab or Double Cab |
| TRD PreRunner | 2-wheel-drive desert style | Leaf springs, 2-wheel drive only |
| TRD Sport | Street | Red sport-tuned shocks. Hybrid available |
| TRD Off-Road | Trails | Bilstein remote-reservoir shocks. Hybrid available |
| Limited | Comfort | Adaptive Variable Suspension. Hybrid available |
| TRD Pro | Fast off-road | Fox QS3 shocks, IsoDynamic seats, 33-inch tires. Hybrid only |
| Trailhunter | Overlanding | Old Man Emu shocks, 33-inch tires, rock rails, air compressor. Hybrid only |

## Hardware beats branding

On a third-generation truck, the 2 things worth paying for off-road are the locking rear differential and the right tires. The TRD Off-Road has the locker, and with the automatic it also gets Crawl Control and Multi-Terrain Select, which adjust the throttle and brakes for sand, mud and rock. The TRD Pro adds Fox shocks and more clearance, but costs much more for gains most drivers never reach.

On a 2024–2026 truck, check 3 things: the rear suspension, the engine and the shocks. Leaf springs ride harder than coils with an empty bed. The hybrid is optional on the TRD Sport, TRD Off-Road and Limited, so 2 trucks with the same badge can drive very differently. And the TRD Off-Road's Bilstein shocks are the subject of the August 2026 reservoir recall, so make sure the fix is done.

## Choosing one

- **Commuting and weekend errands:** an SR5 or TRD Sport.
- **Regular trails at a fair price:** a TRD Off-Road. It's the best balance of cost and capability in both generations.
- **Comfort and features:** a Limited, ideally a 2024–2026 with the hybrid.
- **Fast desert roads or rock crawling:** a TRD Pro, if you'll use it.
- **Long trips off the grid:** a Trailhunter, with an optional ARB bed rack for a tent or gear.
- **Towing:** a 2017–2023 V6 with the Tow Package, or a 2024–2026 SR5 or TRD PreRunner XtraCab.

Off-road grades hold their value strongly, which helps sellers more than buyers. A clean stock TRD Off-Road gives most weekend explorers nearly everything a TRD Pro does for much less money. Equally, buying a basic truck and adding aftermarket suspension often costs more and works worse than buying the right grade in the first place.
