The fourth-generation Pilot went on sale in December 2022 as a 2023 model, and it's the largest and most powerful SUV Honda has built. It sits on a new light-truck platform, 3.4 inches longer than before with a wheelbase 2.8 inches longer, and the styling is squarer and tougher. The cabin is simpler and better finished, with a 9-inch touchscreen on every trim above the Sport. It still drives like a big, calm car, just a more solid one.

## A new V6 and a 10-speed

The engine is still a 3.5-liter V6, but it's a new twin-cam design making 285 hp and 262 lb-ft, and it's paired with Honda's own 10-speed automatic in every Pilot. All-wheel-drive models get the second generation of i-VTM4, with a rear differential that handles 40% more torque and responds 30% faster than before. Car and Driver ran a TrailSport to 60 mph in 6.9 seconds. Fuel economy is about what it was: 22 mpg combined with front-wheel drive, 21 with all-wheel drive and 20 for the TrailSport, with its all-terrain tires.

## The seat that hides under the floor

On the Touring and Elite, the second-row middle seat lifts out and stows in a well under the cargo floor. With it in place, the Pilot seats 8; with it removed, there's a walkway to the third row between 2 captain's-style seats. The EX-L comes as an 8-seater or with second-row captain's chairs, and the TrailSport always has captain's chairs and seats 7. Second-row legroom is up 2.4 inches, and the third row gains legroom too. Behind the third row there's 22.4 cubic feet, by Honda's measure, which counts the space under the floor.

## The years

The 2023 range ran Sport, EX-L, TrailSport, Touring and Elite, and a cheaper LX joined it. For 2025, the Black Edition returned and the LX was dropped. The 2026 refresh brought a larger grille, a 12.3-inch touchscreen and a 10.2-inch digital instrument display on every trim, wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and more insulation to cut noise.

## What to check before you buy

This generation has collected a long list of recalls for a young car. On 2023–2024 Pilots, a few steering racks may contain a loose ball bearing (23V735). On 2023–2025 Pilots, the fuel filler neck can separate and leak (24V900), fuel injection software may need updating (25V031), the brake pedal pivot pin may not be secured (25V391), and the electric power steering needs new software (26V721). Some 2025 Pilots have a third-row seat belt routed incorrectly (25V056), and some 2026 Pilots have rear subframe bolts that weren't tightened properly (26V424). Each one is a free repair, but confirm each was done. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

For most families, a 2023 or later EX-L or Touring with all-wheel drive is the Pilot to buy: more room and refinement than the older car, with the removable middle seat if you choose the Touring. Pay for the TrailSport only if you'll use its ground clearance and tires; on pavement, its all-terrain tires cost 1 mpg against an all-wheel-drive EX-L. A 2026 brings the bigger screen and a quieter cabin, if the price gap is small.

::: curiosity A Spare Seat, Stowed
Honda shaped the underfloor well behind the third row to hold the removable middle seat, so it travels with the car instead of being left in the garage. With it out, the outboard seats double as captain's chairs, and a child seat in either one no longer blocks the way to the back.
:::
