The CR-V Hybrid's best trick isn't its fuel economy. It's the way it moves a family SUV away from a stop on electric power, smoothly and quietly, before the engine joins in. Honda has sold it in the US since 2020, across 2 generations, and it uses the same 2-motor system as the Accord Hybrid: most of the time the engine runs a generator and an electric motor drives the wheels.

## 2020–2022

The first CR-V Hybrid arrived in the 2020 refresh, with a 2.0-liter engine, 2 motors and 212 hp. All-wheel drive was standard, and the EPA rated it at 38 mpg combined. It was priced from $27,750 for the LX ($36,000 in 2026 dollars). The battery sits under the cargo floor, so the hybrid has 33.2 cubic feet behind the rear seat and 68.7 with it folded, against 39.2 and 75.8 in the gas CR-V, and it has no spare tire.

## 2023–2026

The sixth-generation hybrid has 204 hp and 247 lb-ft, and it's the volume CR-V: the Sport, Sport-L and Sport Touring are all hybrids. For the first time, it's available with front-wheel drive, which the EPA rates at 40 mpg combined, against 37 with all-wheel drive. The Sport Touring is all-wheel drive only, and Car and Driver ran one from 0 to 60 mph in 7.9 seconds. The battery still takes the lower cargo floor, but the bigger body means 36.3 cubic feet behind the rear seat, 3.1 more than before. The hybrid is rated to tow 1,000 pounds, against 1,500 for the turbo. The 2026 TrailSport Hybrid is rated at 35 mpg.

## What to check before you buy

Have a Honda dealer scan the hybrid system for stored codes, and confirm how much of the hybrid component warranty is left for the VIN. The engine should start and stop without shudders, and there should be no hybrid warnings on the dash. Check the tire repair kit, since there's no spare, and check the recalls: the fuel pump on 2020 Hybrids (23V858) and the front passenger seat weight sensor on 2020–2022 cars (26V332); on 2023–2025 cars, the steering gearbox (24V744) and the high-pressure fuel pump (24V763); and on 2023–2026 Hybrids, the tire repair kit's sealant bottle (26V366), plus 26V663 for cars that got faulty replacement parts. Some 2023 hybrids had a faulty transmission replaced under 23V588. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2023 or later Sport Hybrid with front-wheel drive is the best CR-V for most people: 40 mpg, the newer cabin and stronger acceleration than the turbo. A 2020–2022 Hybrid is the bargain if you want standard all-wheel drive and can live with the smaller cargo area. If you need the spare tire, the lower floor or more towing, buy [a gas CR-V](../2023-2026/) instead.

::: curiosity "A Clutch, Not a Gearbox"
The CR-V Hybrid has no conventional transmission. Below highway speeds, an electric motor drives the wheels. At a steady cruise, a single clutch locks the engine directly to the wheels, because at that speed it's more efficient than turning a generator.
:::
