After 2021, the Land Cruiser disappeared from America for 2 model years. When the name came back for 2024, it wasn't on the big V8 200 Series or on its global replacement, the 300 Series, which went to the Lexus LX 600 instead. It was on the 250 Series, known as the Land Cruiser Prado in many other countries. It's narrower in mission, more affordable and built around off-road ability and modern technology rather than luxury.

That matters on the used market. A 2021 and a 2024 Land Cruiser aren't 2 years of the same truck. They're 2 different ideas of what a Land Cruiser should be.

## How it drives

Every US 250 Series has the same powertrain: Toyota's 2.4-liter turbocharged 4-cylinder i-FORCE MAX hybrid, with an electric motor built into the 8-speed automatic. It makes 326 hp and 465 lb-ft, 64 lb-ft more than the old V8 but 55 hp less. The electric motor fills in before the turbo builds boost, so it feels strong pulling away and climbing. It's not quick: Car and Driver timed a 2024 First Edition to 60 mph in 7.7 seconds, 1.0 second slower than the old V8.

[[chart:torque]]

The off-road hardware is serious and standard. Every version has full-time 4-wheel drive with a 2-speed transfer case and locking center and rear differentials. The Land Cruiser grade and the First Edition add a front anti-roll bar that disconnects at the push of a button, letting the front wheels move more freely on rough ground. It rides on Toyota's TNGA-F frame, shared with the Tacoma, Tundra, Sequoia and 4Runner, and tows up to 2,722 kg (6,000 lb).

## Living with it

The 250 Series has 2 rows and 5 seats in the US. Car and Driver points to the hybrid battery as the reason the third row was dropped, and the battery takes some of the cargo area too. If you need 7 seats, this isn't your Land Cruiser.

The cabin is thoroughly modern. Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 is standard, the 1958 has an 8-inch touchscreen and the Land Cruiser grade a 12.3-inch screen, both with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto. The EPA rates every version at 23 mpg combined (22 city, 25 highway), better than any Land Cruiser before it. The square body still pays for itself at highway speed, and Car and Driver saw 20 mpg in its test.

Changes since launch are small. The First Edition was sold for 2024 only, leaving 2 grades from 2025. For 2026, nothing changed but the price. For 2027, Toyota added an optional high-mounted air intake, heated and ventilated rear seats in the Premium Package and a new color, Inked.

## What to check before you buy

This generation is too new for a long-term reliability record, but it already has recalls. The big one, announced in October 2026, covers about 106,000 2024–2027 Land Cruisers whose fuel tube connection near the tank can come apart and leak fuel, raising the risk of a fire (26V-673). Dealers will inspect it and fit an extra clamp or replace the tube, and Toyota planned to notify owners by mid-December 2026. Ask whether the work has been done, and if the remedy isn't available yet, ask the dealer what to do in the meantime.

Certain 2024–2025 models are also recalled because part of the 12.3-inch instrument display can go blank at startup, hiding warnings (26V-341). The fix is a software update. Then test it: it should start quietly in Ready mode with no warnings, shift smoothly and engage 4-low and both lockers where it's safe to try. Look underneath for trail damage. ## The verdict

The best value is a 2024–2026 Land Cruiser 1958 with its recalls done. If you'll go off-road often, the Land Cruiser grade's disconnecting anti-roll bar is worth having. A First Edition is the collectible one, but you pay for the badge.

Buy the 250 Series if you want a capable, efficient, easy-to-park 4x4 with modern safety equipment, and you don't need a third row or heavy towing. If you do, read [the 200 Series chapter](../2017-2021-200-series/).

::: curiosity The Ones With Round Eyes
The 1958 and the First Edition have round LED headlights. The Land Cruiser grade has slim rectangular ones, which Toyota says are reminiscent of the 60 Series of the 1980s. The 1958 is named for the year the Land Cruiser first went on sale in the US, and Toyota calls its round lights retro-inspired, a nod to the Land Cruisers of old.
:::
