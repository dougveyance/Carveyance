The Ford Taurus family

7 generations and variants in the Index, 1986–2019. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1986–1991

    Ford Taurus (first generation)

    The jelly bean that saved Ford: Motor Trend's 1986 Car of the Year rewrote American sedan design, passed 2 million built in 6 years — and hid a 220-hp Yamaha V6 in the SHO.

    220 hp6.6 sec 0-60143 mph top2,000,000 built
    Taurus SHO (1989–91) · 3.0 Vulcan V6 · MT-5 2.5 (1986–88) · 3.8 Essex (1988–91) · Wagon
  2. 1989–1991

    Ford Taurus SHO (first generation)

    A family sedan with a Yamaha-built 24-valve V6 that revved to 7,300 rpm, a 5-speed only, and an intake manifold people framed. Ford planned a limited run; 15,519 sold the first year.

    220 hp6.7 sec 0-60143 mph top32,737 built
    1989 · 1990 · 1991 and the Plus package
  3. 1992–1995

    Ford Taurus (second generation)

    America's best-selling car, 1992 through 1996: 409,751 in 1992 alone, the first US mid-size with standard dual airbags — and a Yamaha 3.2 SHO for the connoisseurs.

    140 hp9.8 sec 0-60108 mph top1,400,000 built
    GL 3.0 · 3.8 Essex · SHO 3.2 automatic (1993–95)
  4. 1996–1999

    Ford Taurus (third generation)

    The oval gamble: a $18,000-to-start redesign so radical that buyers flinched, half of 1996's sales went to rental fleets — and the Camry took the crown in 1997.

    235 hp8.0 sec 0-60130 mph top1,497,612 built
    SHO V8 (1996–99) · 3.0 Duratec 24-valve · Wagon
  5. 2000–2007

    Ford Taurus (fourth generation)

    The long goodbye: still selling 382,035 in 2000, then rental lots, then fleets only — until the last one rolled out of Atlanta in 2006, bought by the founder of Chick-fil-A.

    200 hp8.1 sec 0-60115 mph top1,988,651 built
    SES Duratec · 3.0 Vulcan, 155 hp · Wagon (to 2005)
  6. 2008–2009

    Ford Taurus (fifth generation)

    The Five Hundred in witness protection: Alan Mulally revived the Taurus name in 2007, added 60 hp and a 6-speed, and bought the badge 2 transitional years.

    263 hp6.8 sec 0-60120 mph top98,284 built
    Limited · SEL AWD
  7. 2010–2019

    Ford Taurus (sixth generation)

    The flagship finale: a 365-hp, 5.2-second SHO, the cop car that replaced the Crown Vic — and the last Taurus built in Chicago on March 1, 2019.

    365 hp5.2 sec 0-60130 mph top540,692 built
    Taurus SHO (2010–19) · 3.5 V6 · 2.0 EcoBoost (2013–19)
