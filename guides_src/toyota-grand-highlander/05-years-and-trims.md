The Grand Highlander has changed little since it went on sale in the summer of 2023 as a 2024 model. Toyota has added grades rather than redesigning anything, so the main questions for a shopper are which year, which grade and which powertrain. The answer to the first is mostly about recalls and price, and this chapter covers the other 2.

## Year by year

| Model year | What's new | Grades |
|---|---|---|
| 2024 | Launch year. Gas, Hybrid and Hybrid MAX powertrains. Curtain airbag recall and stop-sale in June 2024 | XLE, Limited, Platinum. Hybrid MAX on Limited and Platinum only |
| 2025 | Adds an entry-level LE and a Hybrid Nightshade Edition | LE, XLE, Limited, Hybrid Nightshade, Platinum |
| 2026 | Carries over with few changes. Starts at $41,360 for a gas LE with front-wheel drive | LE, XLE, Limited, Hybrid Nightshade, Platinum |
| 2027 | Adds the Woodland Edition. Starts at $42,560 for a gas LE with front-wheel drive | LE, XLE, Woodland Edition, Limited, Platinum |

## The grades

- **LE (2025 on):** the entry point, with the same body, space and safety equipment as every other Grand Highlander. It's offered with the gas engine and, for 2026, the Hybrid.
- **XLE:** the volume grade and the best value for most buyers. Available with the gas engine or the Hybrid.
- **Hybrid Nightshade (2025–2026):** a darker-styled Hybrid.
- **Limited:** the comfort grade, and the cheaper way into the Hybrid MAX.
- **Platinum:** the top of the range, with every feature Toyota offers. Available with all 3 powertrains.
- **Woodland Edition (2027):** a new, more outdoorsy grade.

## How the drive systems differ

Front-wheel drive is available on the gas and Hybrid models. With all-wheel drive, each powertrain uses a different system. The gas model uses Dynamic Torque Control all-wheel drive, the Hybrid uses Electronic On-Demand all-wheel drive with a rear electric motor, and the Hybrid MAX uses Full-Time Electronic all-wheel drive with its more powerful rear motor.

## What the money buys

For 2026, before Toyota's destination charge, the gas Grand Highlander starts at $41,360, the Hybrid at $44,710 and the Hybrid MAX at $55,190. For 2027, the gas LE with front-wheel drive rises to $42,560 and the Hybrid LE to $45,910. The jump to the Hybrid MAX is the biggest step in the range, which is why it makes sense only if you want its performance or its 2,268-kg (5,000-lb) towing with hybrid economy.

## Which year to buy

- **2024:** the cheapest used, but check that the curtain airbag recall is done. Most 2024 models were included.
- **2025:** adds the LE and Nightshade. Check that the camera and instrument panel software updates are done.
- **2026:** the newest used, but check whether your car was built in April or late June 2026, when the rear axle carrier and steering knuckle recalls apply.
- **2027:** new, with the Woodland Edition.

## The verdict

There's no bad year of Grand Highlander, only recall work to confirm. For most buyers, a 2025 or 2026 XLE, gas or Hybrid, is the sweet spot. Spend more for the Hybrid MAX only if you'll use its speed or towing.
