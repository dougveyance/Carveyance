The Passport is a 5-seat SUV built alongside the Pilot in Lincoln, Alabama, with a V6, a big cargo area and more appetite for dirt roads than most family crossovers. Honda brought the name back for 2019. The original Passport, sold from 1994 to 2002, was an Isuzu Rodeo with Honda badges; this one is Honda's own, a Pilot with the third row left out.

There have been 2 generations since 2019. The 2019–2025 Passport has a 280-hp V6 and a 9-speed automatic, with front-wheel drive or all-wheel drive. It was restyled for 2022, when the first TrailSport arrived, all-wheel drive became standard for 2023, and the TrailSport got all-terrain tires for 2024. The redesigned 2026 Passport is squarer and tougher-looking, with a 285-hp V6, a 10-speed automatic and all-wheel drive on every version, and for 2027 the TrailSport rides higher on a new suspension.

This guide has 3 chapters: the 2019–2025 Passport, the 2026 and later Passport, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2026 or later RTL, with standard all-wheel drive, the 10-speed and the 12.3-inch screen.
- **Best value:** a 2022–2025 EX-L with all-wheel drive, standard from 2023.
- **Best for dirt roads:** a 2024 or later TrailSport, with all-terrain tires, or a 2027 TrailSport with the lifted suspension.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2019–2021 Sport or EX-L with front-wheel drive, if you don't need traction in snow or mud.
- **Think twice if** you need a third row (look at the Pilot) or want the best fuel economy (look at a CR-V Hybrid).
