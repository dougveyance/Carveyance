The Bronco Raptor isn't just a Bronco with bigger tires. Ford Performance built it for high-speed desert running as much as slow rock crawling, and almost everything that touches the ground is different. That makes it spectacularly capable. It also makes it wider, thirstier and much harder to justify as a commuter.

## What the Raptor adds

Ford revealed it in January 2022 as a 4-door only. It has a 3.0-liter twin-turbo V6 rated at 418 hp and 440 lb-ft on premium fuel, with the 10-speed automatic, and a Baja drive mode. The frame is stronger, the axles and drive shafts are heavier-duty, the brakes come from the F-150 Raptor and the cooling was upgraded for desert heat. It rides on FOX shocks and 37-inch tires, with 13.1 inches of ground clearance, 1.6 inches more than a Bronco with the Sasquatch Package.

It's also 85.7 inches wide, so it carries the extra marker lights that the law requires on wide vehicles. Inside, it has orange accents, more heavily bolstered seats, a thicker steering wheel and magnesium paddle shifters.

## Performance

Car and Driver ran a 2022 Bronco Raptor, at 2,600 kg (5,733 lb), to 60 mph in 5.6 seconds, the quickest of the Bronco tests on record. The EPA rates it at 15 mpg combined, every year from 2022 to 2026.

## Towing

[[chart:tow]]

A rear brace raises the Raptor's towing rating to 2,041 kg (4,500 lb), 454 kg (1,000 lb) more than any other Bronco, and the drive-mode selector adds a tow/haul mode. If towing is the main job, though, a pickup will do it better for less.

## What it cost

The 2022 Bronco Raptor started at $68,500 before delivery, or $78,700 in 2026 dollars, and the price climbed over the next 2 years. For 2025, Ford cut it by more than $10,000, to $79,995 ($83,600 in 2026 dollars), and the 2026 Raptor started at the same $79,995.

## Who should buy one

Buy the Raptor for the experience it was built to deliver: fast dirt roads, washboard, sand and whoops. On pavement it's wide, heavy and loud, and its tires cost far more to replace than a standard Bronco's. If your trail driving is mostly slow, a Badlands or a Sasquatch-equipped Bronco gets you most of the crawling ability for much less.

## Raptor checks

On a used Raptor, look hardest at the things the desert punishes: the shocks for leaks, the wheels for bends, the skid plates and the frame for impacts, and the tires for cuts. Run the VIN for the recalls. 2022 Raptors are included in 24V635, the 3.0-liter intake-valve recall, and 23V155 covers internal damage to the steering gear on some 2022 Raptors. The 2021–2026 wiring-harness recall, 26V468, includes the Raptor too.
