By 2017, the second-generation Tundra had already been on sale for a decade, and its rivals had moved on to turbochargers, 10-speed gearboxes and screens the size of laptops. Toyota kept selling a naturally aspirated V8 with a 6-speed automatic. That was either admirably stubborn or badly out of date, depending on how much you spend on gas.

For a used buyer, it's mostly good news. The 5.7-liter V8 is a well-known engine with a long service record, and the truck is simple to work on. Just don't mistake a good reputation for a good truck. The one in front of you still needs checking.

## How it drives

There are 2 engines. The 4.6-liter V8 makes 310 hp and 327 lb-ft, and it was the standard engine on the SR and SR5 through 2019. The 5.7-liter V8 makes 381 hp and 401 lb-ft, and from 2020 it was the only engine. Both use a 6-speed automatic, and 4x4 versions use a part-time system with a low range.

[[chart:power]]

The 5.7 feels relaxed and strong, with a V8 sound that turbo trucks can't match. Car and Driver timed a 2017 CrewMax 4x4 to 60 mph in 6.4 seconds, and a 2020 TRD Pro, weighing 2,721 kg (5,998 lb), in the same 6.4. The steering is slow and the ride is firm and busy with an empty bed, because the rear axle sits on leaf springs.

Properly equipped, a 2017 Tundra with the 5.7 is rated to tow 4,763 kg (10,500 lb), on a 4x2 Regular Cab. For 2020, Toyota quoted 4,627 kg (10,200 lb) under the SAE J2807 towing standard, with a maximum payload of 785 kg (1,730 lb). A 4.6-liter truck tows much less, so check the label on the one you're looking at.

## Living with it

The cabin is big and practical, with a rear seat that adults can use in the CrewMax. Cabs are the Double Cab and the larger CrewMax, plus a Regular Cab through 2018. The best years are the last ones. By 2019, Toyota Safety Sense P, with automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise control, was standard on every grade. For 2020, Apple CarPlay and Android Auto finally arrived.

Fuel is the price of admission. The EPA rates a 5.7 4x4 at 14 mpg combined (13 city, 17 highway) and a 4x2 at 15. At 15,000 miles a year and $4 a gallon, 14 mpg is about $4,286 of fuel. Many trucks have a 38-gallon tank, which helps the range but not the bill.

## What to check before you buy

Get it on a lift. Look at the frame, brake lines and underbody for rust, especially on trucks from states that salt their roads, and check the differential seals, the cooling system and the brakes. Look at the hitch and wiring, and ask what the truck towed and how often. A truck that pulled a heavy trailer every weekend needs records of transmission fluid and differential oil changes.

Start it cold and listen for ticking or exhaust leaks, and make sure 4-high and 4-low engage cleanly. Lifted trucks with big tires aren't automatically bad, but the work should be documented and professional.

Then run the VIN. Certain 2018–2020 Tundras are part of Toyota's fuel pump recall (20V-012 and 20V-682), because the low-pressure pump can fail and stall the engine. Toyota also recalled certain 2007–2021 Tundras because the power steering gear may have been built incorrectly and can leak. Ask for proof the repairs were done. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

The sweet spot is a documented 2020–2021 5.7-liter SR5 or Limited: the same proven V8 with the screen and phone connection the older trucks lack. A 2017–2019 5.7 makes sense when the price reflects the older cabin. Pass on the 4.6 unless the truck is cheap and you won't tow much, and pay TRD Pro money only if you'll use its Fox shocks.

Buy this Tundra if you want a simple, durable V8 truck and can live with the fuel bills. If you want more torque, a better ride and a modern cabin, look at [the 2022–2026 truck](../2022-2026/).

::: curiosity The Window That Drops Into the Wall
Every CrewMax Tundra has a power rear window that slides straight down into the back of the cab, rather than a small center pane that slides sideways. Open it on a warm day and the whole back of the cab opens to the air, and long items can poke through into the bed.
:::
