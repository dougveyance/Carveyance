The Tacoma has been America's best-selling midsize pickup for 2 decades, and its reputation for running forever keeps used prices stubbornly high. For a shopper looking at 2017 to 2026 models, though, there are really 2 different trucks wearing the name. The third generation, sold through 2023, uses a 2.7-liter 4-cylinder or a 3.5-liter V6 and a 6-speed automatic. The fourth generation arrived for 2024 on a new frame shared with the Tundra, with a 2.4-liter turbo, an 8-speed automatic and an optional hybrid called i-FORCE MAX.

This guide has 6 chapters. Start with the generation you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a stock, documented 2020–2023 SR5 V6 or TRD Off-Road. The 2020 update brought Apple CarPlay and Android Auto to the proven V6 truck.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2024–2026 SR5 or TRD Off-Road with the 278-hp turbo. It's quicker than the old V6, uses a little less fuel and rides better on the coil-spring versions.
- **Strongest:** the i-FORCE MAX hybrid, with 465 lb-ft. It's standard on the TRD Pro and Trailhunter.
- **Best for towing:** a 2017–2023 V6 with the Tow Package, rated for up to 3,084 kg (6,800 lb).
- **Think twice if** the back seat has to carry adults every day. The Double Cab's rear seat is tight in both generations, and a Tundra or a 3-row SUV may suit you better.
