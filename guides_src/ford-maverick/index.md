For decades, pickup buyers were offered the same bargain: more size, more engine, more capability and more money. The Maverick, unveiled on June 8, 2021, asked a less fashionable question: what if the truck were only as big as the job? It's a compact, 4-door pickup built on the same unibody platform as the Escape and the Bronco Sport, at the same plant in Hermosillo, Mexico, with a 4.5-foot bed and, at launch, a standard hybrid. It became America's most affordable pickup and, in 2025, a record seller, with 155,051 sold.

Every Maverick so far belongs to the same generation, updated for 2025 with a new face, a 13.2-inch screen and hybrid all-wheel drive. This guide has 4 chapters. Start with the years you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a 2022–2024 hybrid with every recall done, if front-wheel drive suits you.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2025 or later hybrid with all-wheel drive, the combination the original Maverick should have had.
- **Best for towing:** an EcoBoost with all-wheel drive and the 4K Tow Package, or a 2025 or later hybrid with all-wheel drive and the same package.
- **Most fun:** the 2025 Lobo, lowered and quicker. Car and Driver ran one to 60 mph in 5.8 seconds.
- **Think twice if** you tow more than 1,814 kg (4,000 lb), carry long loads or drive rough trails. A Ranger will suit you better.
