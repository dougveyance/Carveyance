The Mazda RX-7 family

3 generations and variants in the Index, 1978–2002. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1978–1985

    Mazda RX-7 (FB)

    The wedge that started it all. Light, simple and powered by a tiny rotary, the first RX-7 went racing straight away and won, taking the top 2 places in its class at Daytona in 1979 and the Spa 24 Hours outright in 1981.

    100 hp9.2 sec 0-60471,018 built
    12A · 13B, GSL-SE
  2. 1985–1992

    Mazda RX-7 (FC)

    The second RX-7 grew up: independent rear suspension, a turbocharged 13B, and a silhouette that critics never stopped comparing to a Porsche. Beneath it sits one of the cleverest passive chassis of the 1980s and a rotary that loves to rev, which makes it the RX-7 that rewards a patient, attentive driver and the most affordable way into the family today.

    202 hp6.5 sec 0-60272,027 built
    Turbo · Non-turbo
  3. 1991–2002

    Mazda RX-7 (FD)

    Mazda's third RX-7 traded comfort for focus: a twin-turbo rotary, a body stripped of every spare kilogram, and a shape that still looks freshly drawn more than 30 years on. It is the purest expression of Mazda's horse-and-rider ideal, and the one hero of Japan's 1990s sports-car boom that never seems to age.

    255 hp5.2 sec 0-6068,589 built
