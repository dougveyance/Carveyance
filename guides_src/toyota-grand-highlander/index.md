For years, the Toyota Highlander had one weakness that families kept running into: its third row was for children, and there was little room for luggage behind it. The Grand Highlander, new for 2024, is Toyota's answer. It's longer, wider and taller than the Highlander, with a third row adults can use and real cargo space behind it, and it comes with 3 very different powertrains.

For a shopper, the choice is mostly about the engine. The gas Grand Highlander uses a 265-hp 2.4-liter turbo. The Hybrid pairs a 2.5-liter engine with electric motors for 245 hp and up to 36 mpg. And the Hybrid MAX combines the turbo with electric motors for 362 hp, making it one of the quickest 3-row SUVs in its class. This guide has 6 chapters: an overview, one for each powertrain, one on the years and trims, and the used-car checklist.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a gas XLE. It has the space and most of the features that make a Grand Highlander worth buying.
- **Lowest running costs:** a Hybrid XLE, rated at 36 mpg combined with front-wheel drive.
- **Quickest:** a Hybrid MAX Limited or Platinum. Car and Driver ran one from 0 to 60 mph in 5.6 seconds.
- **Best for towing:** the gas model or the Hybrid MAX, both rated for 2,268 kg (5,000 lb).
- **Newest:** a 2027 with the new Woodland Edition, or a 2026 with every recall done.
- **Think twice if** you park in a tight garage. At 201.4 inches long, it's bigger than many rivals.
