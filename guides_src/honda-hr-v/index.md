The HR-V is Honda's smallest SUV, sold in the US since the 2016 model year and built in Mexico. Between 2017 and 2026, Honda sold 2 very different versions. The second generation, through 2022, is based on the Fit and shares its Magic Seat, whose rear cushions flip up to carry tall things. The third, from 2023, is a bigger North American model built on the Civic's platform, with more power and a better ride, but without the Magic Seat.

For a used buyer, the choices are the generation, front- or all-wheel drive, and the trim. This guide has 3 chapters: one for each generation and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2023 or later Sport or EX-L. More power, a quieter cabin and a better ride than the older car.
- **Best value:** a 2019–2022 EX, with Honda Sensing standard and the Magic Seat.
- **Most versatile cargo space:** any 2016–2022 HR-V, thanks to the Magic Seat and 58.8 cubic feet with the seat folded.
- **Best for snow:** either generation with all-wheel drive and good tires.
- **Think twice if** you need to tow, or want quick acceleration: neither HR-V is quick, and Honda's specification sheet for the current one lists no tow rating.
