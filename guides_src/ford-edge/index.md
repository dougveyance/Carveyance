The Edge never had a famous year, a scandal, or a redesign anyone argued about — it just sold 85,000 to 142,000 copies annually for 17 straight years while louder nameplates came and went. Ford's first modern 2-row crossover arrived for 2007 on car bones out of Oakville, grew a second generation in 2015, picked up the first ST badge ever worn by a Ford SUV in 2019, and built its last North American example on April 26, 2024 with no direct replacement. About 2.07 million sold.

That arc makes it one of the used market's best quiet buys: enormous supply, 1-owner family histories, simple well-known engines, and no new-model showroom propping up residuals. The 2-row midsize segment the Edge helped invent now belongs to everyone else — but nobody sells you one this cheap.

## Quick picks

- **Best used buy:** a 2019–2024 with the 2.0 EcoBoost and the 8-speed — the refresh brought the better gearbox and Co-Pilot360, and fleet-free examples are everywhere.
- **The sleeper:** an Edge ST — 335 hp and 380 lb-ft of twin-turbo V6 in a shape nobody profiles. High-5s to 60 in period tests, priced like a family hauler.
- **The budget V6:** a 2011–2014 with the 285-hp 3.5 — post-facelift power and the simplest drivetrain of the family.
- **The value Sport:** a 2015–2018 Sport, the ST before the badge — 315 hp, all-wheel drive, forgotten by the market.
- **Approach with eyes open:** 2007–2010 cars — honest but aging, and the early 3.5's water-pump location makes a service history non-negotiable.
