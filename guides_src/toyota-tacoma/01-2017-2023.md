The third-generation Tacoma arrived for 2016 and stayed on sale through 2023 with the same basic recipe: a separate ladder frame, leaf springs at the back and a choice of a 4-cylinder or a V6. It was never the most refined midsize pickup. It was the one people trusted, and that trust is why a clean, high-mileage example still costs more than you think it should.

That reputation is the appeal and the trap. A Tacoma will put up with a lot, but it isn't comfortable for tall rear passengers, it's noisy on the freeway and the V6 has to work hard on long grades. Buy it for what it's good at, which is being a tough, simple truck that holds its value.

## How it drives

There are 2 engines. The 2.7-liter 4-cylinder makes 159 hp and 180 lb-ft, enough for a light-duty work truck and not much more. The 3.5-liter V6 makes 278 hp and 265 lb-ft, and it's the one most buyers want. Both use a 6-speed automatic. A 6-speed manual was offered with the V6, and a 5-speed manual with the 4-cylinder for 2017.

[[chart:power]]

The V6 is strong once it's revving, but its torque peaks high in the range, so the automatic downshifts often on hills and when you ask for a pass. That's normal behavior, not a fault. Car and Driver timed a 2017 TRD Pro to 60 mph in 7.7 seconds, and that truck weighed 2,114 kg (4,661 lb).

The ride is firm and busy with an empty bed, and the seat sits low relative to the floor, so taller drivers end up with their knees up. The Double Cab's back seat works for children and short trips. Test it with your own passengers before you commit.

Every 4x4 uses a part-time system with a low range, and the TRD Off-Road and TRD Pro add a locking rear differential. With the automatic, they also get Crawl Control, a low-speed off-road cruise control. With the V6 and the Tow Package, a properly equipped Tacoma is rated to tow 3,084 kg (6,800 lb). The 4-cylinder tops out at 1,588 kg (3,500 lb).

## Living with it

2020 is the year to know. Toyota added Apple CarPlay, Android Auto and Amazon Alexa, with a 7-inch touchscreen on the SR and an 8-inch screen on every other grade. V6 trucks from the SR5 up gained a 10-way power driver's seat, and the Limited and TRD Pro got a Panoramic View Monitor. Toyota Safety Sense P, with automatic emergency braking and adaptive cruise control, was already standard from 2018.

Fuel economy is worse than the size suggests. The EPA rates a V6 4x4 with the automatic at 20 mpg combined (18 city, 22 highway), and a 4x2 V6 at 21. The 4-cylinder doesn't save much: 20 mpg as a 4x4. The V6 4x4 with the manual is rated at 18. At 15,000 miles a year and $4 a gallon, 20 mpg is about $3,000 of fuel.

## What to check before you buy

Get the truck on a lift. Look at the frame, crossmembers, skid plates and differential seals, and look harder on trucks from states that salt their roads or that have spent time on trails. Shift into 4-high and 4-low, and test the locker if it has one. On a 2019–2023 Double Cab, listen for a whine from the rear axle at speed. It's the subject of a Toyota service bulletin, T-SB-0045-23.

Be wary of lifts, big tires and aftermarket wiring. They change how the truck rides, stops and wears, and they're only as good as whoever fitted them. A stock truck with records is worth more than a built one with stories.

Then run the VIN. Certain 2017–2020 Tacomas are part of Toyota's fuel pump recall (20V-012 and 20V-682), because the low-pressure pump can fail and stall the engine. About 381,000 2022–2023 Tacomas were recalled because welding debris could let the rear axle retaining nuts loosen (24V-152). Ask for proof both repairs were done. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

The sweet spot is a stock, documented 2020–2023 SR5 V6 or TRD Off-Road: the newer screen and phone connection on the proven V6. A 2017–2019 makes sense when the price reflects the older cabin and the inspection comes back clean. Choose the 4-cylinder only for light work at a real discount, and pay TRD Pro money only if you'll use the shocks and skid plate.

Buy this Tacoma if you want a simple, durable midsize truck that holds its value. Look at the newer one in [the next chapter](../2024-2026-turbo/) if you care about ride, torque and a modern cabin.

::: curiosity A Camera Mount From the Factory
The third-generation Tacoma launched with a GoPro camera mount built into every windshield, next to the rearview mirror. It was reported as the first factory GoPro mount on any vehicle, and Toyota's engineers added it after noticing how many owners were sticking cameras to their windshields with suction cups to film their trail runs. The camera itself was never included.
:::
