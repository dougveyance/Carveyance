The Mercedes-Benz W124 specials family

2 generations and variants in the Index, 1986–1995. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1986–1988

    AMG Hammer (W124)

    The 1980s sedan that embarrassed supercars: a midsize Mercedes with AMG's quad-cam V8, good for 60 mph in 5.0 seconds and 178 mph. The Petersen's car is the only wagon.

  2. 1991–1995

    Mercedes-Benz 500 E

    The wolf in sheep's clothing: a W124 rebuilt around the 500 SL's V8 and assembled by hand at Porsche, 18 days per car. Mercedes built 10,479, and only 1,528 came to America.
