Some buyers really need a locking rear differential. Others need the people in the parking lot to think they do. Toyota has sold 4Runners to both groups for years, which says something about the truck and something about the rest of us.

The badges do mean something, but what they mean changes with the generation, the year and the options. Start with the ground you'll actually drive on, then pick the grade.

## The grades at a glance

| Grade | Years | Best for | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| SR5 | 2017–2026 | Most buyers | The essentials. Check whether it's two-wheel drive or a 4x4 |
| SR5 Premium | 2017–2024 | Comfort on a budget | A nicer cabin on the SR5's mechanicals |
| TRD Sport | 2022–2026 | Pavement with attitude | A street look and tuning, not extra trail hardware |
| TRD Off-Road | 2017–2026 | Real trails | Locking rear differential, Crawl Control, Multi-Terrain Select |
| Limited | 2017–2026 | Comfort and winter roads | The plushest cabin short of the Platinum. Full-time 4WD on 2017–2024 4x4s |
| Platinum | 2025–2026 | Luxury | Hybrid only. Head-up display, digital rearview mirror, heated rear seats |
| TRD Pro | 2017–2026 | Serious off-road, factory-built | Fox shocks and a TRD skid plate. Hybrid only from 2025 |
| Trailhunter | 2025–2026 | Overlanding | Hybrid only. Old Man Emu shocks, ARB roof rack, onboard air compressor |

## SR5: buy the bones

For most owners the SR5 has everything that makes a 4Runner a 4Runner: the tall body, the big cargo hold, the truck frame and, on a 4x4, a proper low range. The SR5 Premium of 2017–2024 makes the daily drive less spartan. Both make sensible used buys, because the trucks dressed in TRD badges usually cost noticeably more. One warning: a two-wheel-drive SR5 looks exactly like a 4x4 from the curb. Check the VIN and the shifter before you assume.

## TRD Off-Road: the one to look at first

If your weekends include rocky or slippery trails, start here. Both generations give it a locking rear differential, Crawl Control (which creeps the truck over obstacles while you steer) and Multi-Terrain Select, and the 2025–2026 version adds Bilstein shocks. What matters on a used one is whether all of it is fitted, works and has been looked after.

## TRD Pro: excellent kit, expensive enthusiasm

The TRD Pro's appeal is easy to understand: specialist suspension, protection and a look that Toyota builds and warrants, rather than parts bolted on in someone's driveway. On the 2025–2026 truck that means 2.5-inch Fox shocks, a stabilizer bar you can disconnect for more wheel travel, and the hybrid as standard. It usually commands a premium on the used market, too. Inspect the shocks, ask how hard it has been used, and price replacement parts before you pay extra for one.

## TRD Sport and Limited: different missions

A TRD Sport isn't more capable off-road than a TRD Off-Road. It's tuned and styled for pavement. The Limited is the comfort pick, and on the 2017–2024 truck its full-time four-wheel drive suits drivers who see snow all winter. On the 2025–2026 Limited and Platinum, though, you give up the locking rear differential, Crawl Control and Multi-Terrain Select. If you spend 95 percent of your time on pavement, drive a Limited and an SR5 back to back. Tires and suspension tuning shape the ride more than the letters on the tailgate.

## Platinum and Trailhunter: the new personalities

The 2025 redesign added 2 grades at opposite ends. The Platinum is the luxury 4Runner, hybrid only, with a head-up display, a digital rearview mirror and heated rear seats. The Trailhunter is built for overlanding: Old Man Emu shocks with remote reservoirs, an ARB roof rack, a high-mounted air intake and an onboard air compressor so you can pump your tires back up after letting them down for the trail.

[[photo:ch4-trailhunter]]

Both use the hybrid, and they're aimed at completely different owners. The price gap between a stock SR5 and a fully loaded Trailhunter could pay for a lot of actual trips.

## Special editions

Toyota sprinkled special editions through these years, among them the 2020 Venture Edition and the 2023 40th Anniversary Special Edition. Most are appearance and accessory packages on a regular grade. Judge them by the grade underneath, and pay extra only for parts you'd have bought anyway.

## What to check on a modified 4Runner

Look for frame and body damage hiding behind aftermarket armor, lift kits installed with the wrong geometry, tires that rub at full lock, leaking shocks, amateur wiring and missing emissions parts. Ask whether the axle gear ratios were changed to suit bigger tires. And check the spare: an oversized trail tire on all 4 corners with a stock-size spare underneath is a surprise best discovered in the seller's driveway, not on the trail.
