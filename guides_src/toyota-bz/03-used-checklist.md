A used bZ4X can be one of the cheapest ways into a nearly new electric SUV. Whether it's a good buy depends less on the car than on where you'll charge it and how far you drive. Settle that first, then check the battery, the charging and the recall work.

## Know which car it is

The 2023–2025 bZ4X and the 2026 bZ look alike but are very different cars. Confirm the model year, the battery size, the drive and the grade from the window sticker or the build data, not the listing. A 2026 bZ XLE FWD with the 57.7 kWh battery is rated at 236 miles, while the XLE FWD Plus with the 74.7 kWh battery is rated at 314, so the difference matters. Don't apply the 2026 car's range, power or charging to an earlier one.

## The years at a glance

| Model years | What they are | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 2023 | Launch. 201 hp FWD, 214 hp AWD, 222 to 252 miles | Hub-bolt recall (22V-444). Defroster recall (25V-577). Camera recall on cars with the Panoramic View Monitor (25V-744). Label recall on cars with dealer accessories (24V-548) |
| 2024 | Same power and range as 2023 | Defroster, camera and label recalls |
| 2025 | Up to $6,000 cheaper new. Nightshade Edition. More driver aids on the Limited | Defroster and camera recalls |
| 2026 | Renamed bZ. Bigger batteries, NACS port, 338-hp AWD. bZ Woodland added | Too new for long-term data |

## Test the charging

Charge the car where you'll really charge it. If you'll charge at home, confirm the car and your charger work together, and that you have the charging cable that came with it. If you'll rely on public charging, try a DC fast-charging session. Note the starting charge, the outside temperature, the charging rate the car actually accepts and how it falls as the battery fills. A charger's rating is its maximum, not a promise about your car on a cold morning.

On a 2023–2025 bZ4X, check which DC connector the chargers near you have, and whether you'd need an adapter. On a 2026 bZ, check that the NACS port and Plug & Charge work, and try the battery preconditioning on the way to a fast charger.

## Battery and warranty

Range estimates on the dashboard depend on temperature, tires, speed and how the last driver drove, so don't judge the battery by the number on the screen. Ask a Toyota dealer for a battery health check if one is available, and look for any high-voltage warnings. Toyota covers the battery and electric drive parts of the 2026 bZ for 8 years or 100,000 miles. Get the warranty booklet for the car you're looking at, and count from the date it was first sold, not the model year.

Look under the car for scrapes on the battery pack and its shields, especially on a Woodland or an AWD car that may have been on dirt roads. Inspect the charge port door and the pins, the tires and the wheels.

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **2023:** 22V-444. The hub bolts could loosen until a wheel came off. About 260 US cars. The fix replaced the hub bolts and the wheels. Ask for proof it was done.
- **2023–2025:** 25V-577. The windshield defroster may not clear frost, ice or fog, which limits visibility. About 41,500 cars. Toyota's fix is a climate-control software update.
- **2023–2025 with the Panoramic View Monitor:** 25V-744. The rearview camera image may freeze or not appear. About 13,600 cars.
- **2023–2024 with accessories fitted before sale:** 24V-548. A load-capacity label that may list the wrong accessory weight. The fix is a new label.
- **Every year:** run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Walk away if

The 2023 car's hub-bolt recall wasn't done, the car shows a high-voltage or battery warning, the title is branded, or the charging test fails. Walk away, too, if your weekly driving would need fast charging the car can't reliably deliver in your climate.

## What is it worth?

We don't publish average prices. They go stale quickly, and electric car values have moved a lot. The 2025 price cut and the much better 2026 model both weigh on what earlier cars are worth. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same year, grade, battery, drive and mileage, with the same title history, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what cars actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you. Then compare the total cost: the price, insurance, your charging costs, and a RAV4 Hybrid or a rival electric SUV at the same budget.

Buying from a private seller, or keeping the car past its factory warranty? Service plan providers to compare include [Chaiz](https://www.chaiz.com), [Endurance](https://www.endurancewarranty.com) and [Olive](https://www.olive.com). Carveyance doesn't rank or recommend them. Read what each one covers and excludes, especially for the battery and electric drive, and see the [FTC's guidance on auto service contracts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/what-know-about-auto-service-contracts-extended-warranty-scams) first.

## Our shortlist

- **Best for most people:** a 2026 bZ XLE FWD Plus.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2025 bZ4X XLE FWD with home charging.
- **Most space:** a bZ Woodland, checked underneath.
- **Walk away from:** a 2023 car without proof of the hub-bolt repair.
