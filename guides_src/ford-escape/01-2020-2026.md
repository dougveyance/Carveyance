The 2020 Escape was Ford's landing pad for its own orphaned sedan customers — lower, sleeker and deliberately car-like, because the Focus and Fusion had just been killed and their drivers needed somewhere to go. The rugged-SUV act moved next door to the new Bronco Sport, which shares this platform. In August 2025 Ford called time: US production ended that December, and after the 2026 model year the Escape is gone.

## 4 powertrains, 3 good answers

The volume engine is a 181-hp turbo 3-cylinder — adequate, 28 mpg combined with all-wheel drive, 30 with front drive. The 250-hp 2.0 EcoBoost is the shock: Car and Driver ran a 2020 Titanium to 60 in 5.7 seconds, the quickest Escape ever tested. The Hybrid returned after 8 years away at 40 mpg combined, and the Plug-In Hybrid does about 37 electric miles and 101 MPGe, front-drive only. The honest ranking for most people: Hybrid first, 2.0 second, 1.5 third — the 3-cylinder works, but the Hybrid costs little more to buy and much less to run.

## The 2023 refresh and the 2026 finale

The 2023 refresh brought a new face, a bigger available screen and the ST-Line trim family; hybrid output was restated to 192 hp under new SAE measurement rules, not actually reduced. The 2026 swan-song lineup runs Active ($30,350), ST-Line variants, Platinum ($36,215) and ST-Line Elite ($37,210), with the Plug-In Hybrid continuing. Dealers will deal as the clock runs out — the discount against a RAV4 or CR-V is real, and so is the resale question that comes with a discontinued nameplate. Buy the last Escape because the price is right and the hybrid drivetrain is proven, not because you expect it to hold value like the survivors.

## On a test drive

Check the VIN for completed recalls — a 2022 campaign covered cracked fuel injectors on 1.5-liter cars, shared with the Bronco Sport. The 8-speed should shift cleanly and quietly; the eCVT in hybrids is near-silent and should stay that way. The big screen, cameras and Co-Pilot360 features should all behave, and on all-wheel-drive cars look for 4 evenly worn tires.
