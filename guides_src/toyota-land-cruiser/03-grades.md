The 200 Series was sold in a single, fully loaded version, with the Heritage Edition as its only variation. The 250 Series is simpler still: 2 grades, with the First Edition added for 2024 only. All of them have the same hybrid powertrain, full-time 4WD, low range and locking center and rear differentials. What changes is the comfort, the screens and one important off-road part.

## The grades

| Grade | Years | Headlights | What it adds |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1958 | 2024–2026 | Round | The basics: heated fabric seats, an 8-inch touchscreen, Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 |
| Land Cruiser | 2024–2026 | Rectangular | Disconnecting front anti-roll bar, heated and ventilated SofTex front seats, 12.3-inch touchscreen and gauges, power liftgate, wireless charging |
| First Edition | 2024 only | Round | The Land Cruiser grade with its Premium Package, plus a roof rack, rock rails, a front skid plate, 18-inch wheels and its own badges |

## 1958 or Land Cruiser?

The 1958 is the best value and the purest of the 3, with the same engine, lockers and low range as the others. It's a Land Cruiser for people who like plain cabins and plan to add their own gear.

The Land Cruiser grade is worth the extra money if you'll go off-road often. Its front anti-roll bar disconnects at the push of a button, letting the front wheels move further over rocks and ruts, and the 1958 doesn't offer it. It also brings a much nicer cabin, with ventilated seats, the bigger screens and a power liftgate, and it can be ordered with a Premium Package that adds leather and a JBL sound system, among other things.

The First Edition was a limited run for collectors. It's the Land Cruiser grade, fully equipped, with round headlights and trail accessories from the factory. Pay extra for one only if that matters to you.

## What they cost

[[chart:price]]

In 2026 dollars, a new 2017 Land Cruiser cost about $116,300. A 2024 Land Cruiser 1958 cost about $60,000, and the 2026 1958 started at $57,200, before delivery. Toyota didn't make the Land Cruiser cheaper so much as make a different, simpler Land Cruiser that costs about half as much in real terms.

That gap shows up on the used market too. A clean late 200 Series and a nearly new 250 Series can be listed at similar prices, so compare what you get, not the model year. See [the V8 or hybrid chapter](../v8-or-hybrid/).

## What changed for 2027

The 2027 Land Cruiser went on sale in spring 2026, starting at $57,880 before delivery, with the same 2 grades. New options are a high-mounted air intake and, in the Land Cruiser grade's Premium Package, heated and ventilated outboard rear seats. There's also a new color, Inked. Nothing mechanical changed, so a 2026 is a near-identical truck for a little less.

::: curiosity Why 1958?
The base grade is named for the year the Land Cruiser first went on sale in the US. Toyota calls it its longest-running nameplate, and its 1958 grade wears round headlights and a "TOYOTA" grille spelled out in full, like the trucks of that era.
:::
