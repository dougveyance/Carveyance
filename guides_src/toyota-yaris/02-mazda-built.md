The Yaris iA looked like a tiny economy sedan and wore a Toyota badge, but Mazda designed it and built it in Salamanca, Mexico, as a version of the Mazda2. It started life as the Scion iA for 2016, became the Toyota Yaris iA when Scion closed, and was renamed the Yaris Sedan for 2019. For 2020, Toyota added a Mazda-built 5-door Yaris Hatchback to replace its own. Same badge, same engine size, but a different car from the French-built hatchback in [the previous chapter](../2017-2018-hatchback/).

## How it drives

Every one has a 1.5-liter 4-cylinder with 106 hp and 103 lb-ft. The sedan comes with a 6-speed manual or a 6-speed automatic; the 2020 hatchback is automatic only. Light weight helps: a manual iA weighs just 1,082 kg (2,385 lb), and Car and Driver ran one from 0 to 60 mph in 8.7 seconds. That's no rocket, but it's well ahead of the 10.2 seconds Car and Driver recorded for a 4-speed Toyota-built hatchback.

What sets it apart is the way it drives. The steering is precise, the body is well controlled and the manual shifts cleanly, so an ordinary drive to work feels more engaging than the numbers suggest. The compromises are the usual ones for a car this small: road noise on the freeway, a tight back seat and a 13.5-cubic-foot trunk in the sedan.

The EPA rates the automatic iA at 32 mpg city, 40 highway and 35 combined, and the manual at 30, 39 and 34.

## The names and the years

- **2016:** Scion iA. A single well-equipped version, with a 7-inch screen and low-speed automatic emergency braking as standard.
- **2017–2018:** Yaris iA. The same car with Toyota badges, starting at $15,950 for the manual and $1,100 more for the automatic.
- **2019:** renamed Yaris, sold as the Yaris Sedan, with a honeycomb grille and 3 grades: L, LE and a new XLE with leatherette seats and automatic LED headlights. Prices started $500 lower than the iA's, at $15,450.
- **2020:** the sedan gained standard Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, and the 5-door Yaris Hatchback arrived in LE and XLE grades, automatic only, at $18,705 and $19,705.

Toyota ended the Mazda-built Yarises after 2020, with the last cars for the US built in June 2020.

## Parts and service

Because Mazda designed and built these cars, their parts and repair procedures can differ from other Toyotas'. A good shop will identify the car by its VIN rather than the badge, and won't assume parts from the French-built Yaris hatchback will fit. Toyota dealers service them and handle their recalls.

## What to check before you buy

On a manual, check for a slipping clutch and smooth shifts into every gear. On an automatic, check that it shifts promptly in stop-and-go traffic and on the freeway. Run the VIN for the fuel pump recall on 2019–2020 cars. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

The Mazda-built Yaris is the best of the late Yarises: livelier, more efficient on the freeway and nicer to drive than the Toyota-built hatchback. The 2019–2020 sedan is the pick for its added grades and, for 2020, its phone connection. Choose the 2020 hatchback if you want the extra practicality and can live with the automatic.

The same small sedan was sold as a Scion iA, a Toyota Yaris iA and a Toyota Yaris in just 5 model years, without a redesign. Few cars have changed names twice while staying the same underneath.
