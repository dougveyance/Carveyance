The Ranger was America's best-selling compact pickup from 1987 to 2004, and the last of the old line was built in St. Paul, Minnesota, in December 2011. After 7 years away, Ford brought the name back for 2019 with the midsize Ranger it had been selling around the world, now built in Wayne, Michigan. For a shopper looking at 2019 to 2026 models, there are 2 different trucks under the name. The 2019–2023 truck has a 2.3-liter turbo 4-cylinder and comes as a SuperCab or a SuperCrew. The 2024 redesign added a twin-turbo V6, a Raptor and a modern cabin, but in America it comes only as a SuperCrew.

This guide has 4 chapters. Start with the generation you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a documented 2019–2023 XLT, rear-drive or 4x4, with its recalls done.
- **Best for long loads:** a 2019–2023 SuperCab, the last Ranger sold with a 6-foot bed.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2024 or later XLT or Lariat. The 2.7-liter V6 is quicker, and the cabin is a decade newer.
- **Fastest off-road:** the Ranger Raptor. Car and Driver ran a 2024 to 60 mph in 5.3 seconds.
- **Think twice if** you tow near the limit every weekend or need a big back seat. An F-150 will do both better.
