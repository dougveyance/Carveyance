The eleventh-generation Civic arrived for 2022 looking as if Honda had taken an eraser to the old one. The creases, fake vents and fussy tail are gone, replaced by a longer hood, a lower, cleaner body and a cabin with a strip of honeycomb mesh running across the dashboard to hide the air vents. It feels more expensive than the car it replaced, and it still steers like a Civic.

## The engines, by year

For 2022–2024, the LX and Sport have a 2.0-liter 4-cylinder with 158 hp and 138 lb-ft, and the EX and Touring sedans, and the EX-L and Sport Touring hatchbacks, have a turbocharged 1.5-liter with 180 hp and 177 lb-ft. Every one of these uses a CVT except the hatchback with the turbo, which could also be ordered with a 6-speed manual. The new car is no quicker than the old one, because it gained weight: Edmunds ran a 2022 Hatchback Sport Touring from 0 to 60 mph in 7.9 seconds, 0.6 second slower than the 2017 version.

[[chart:mpg]]

The 2025 refresh changed the lineup. The turbo 1.5-liter and the manual hatchback were dropped from the regular Civic. The LX and Sport moved to a revised 2.0-liter with 150 hp and 133 lb-ft, which the EPA rates at 36 mpg combined in the LX sedan, and the new [Civic Hybrid](../hybrid/) took over the top of the range. So a 2025 Sport Touring is a hybrid, while a 2022 Sport Touring hatchback has the turbo, and the 2 are very different cars.

## Sedan or hatchback

The sedan has a large trunk for the class and the most choice of trims. The hatchback, the first Civic hatchback built in the US, at Honda's plant in Greensburg, Indiana, has the more useful cargo area and was the only regular Civic in this generation with a manual. Both have the same comfortable front seats, a rear seat that suits adults and the same safety equipment: Honda Sensing is standard on every one.

## What to check before you buy

The most important recall is 24V744, which covers 2022–2025 Civic sedans and hatchbacks, as well as Type R and Hybrid models. Friction inside the steering gearbox can make the steering feel heavy or sticky, and the fix is a new worm gear spring and fresh grease. A related recall, 23V704, covers 2022–2024 cars that received a replacement steering rack. Some 2023–2024 cars may have a loose driver's seat cushion frame (24V859), the stability control unit on some 2023 cars can leak brake fluid (23V430), and the high-pressure fuel pump on some 2025 cars may crack and leak fuel (24V763). The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2022–2024 EX sedan or EX-L hatchback, with the 180-hp turbo, is the best-value eleventh-generation Civic: quicker than the 2.0-liter and rated at up to 36 mpg. A turbo hatchback with the manual is the enthusiast's choice short of an Si. For 2025 and later, skip the 2.0-liter if you can stretch to [the Hybrid](../hybrid/), which is quicker and more frugal.

::: curiosity Built in Indiana
When the 2022 Civic Hatchback went into production at Greensburg, Indiana, it was the first time Honda had built a Civic hatchback in the US. The previous one came from England.
:::
