Ask the seller not to start the truck before you arrive. Most of what an F-150 will confess, it confesses cold.

## Engine, cold start

- **Cam-phaser rattle (3.5 EcoBoost, mainly 2017–2021):** listen for a 1–2 second rattle at cold start. Ford extended coverage and issued a repair for many model years; a truck with the rattle and no paperwork is a negotiation, not a dealbreaker, but price the job in thousands.
- **Timing and turbos (any EcoBoost):** smooth idle once warm, no blue smoke on a hard pull, no coolant smell. Short-tripped EcoBoosts can show oil dilution; check the dipstick's smell as much as its level.
- **5.0 V8:** the low-drama choice; look mainly for oil-consumption history on 2018–2020 and tune evidence on any truck that sounds aftermarket.
- **PowerBoost:** confirm clean gas-electric handoffs in a parking lot, test Pro Power outlets under real load, and have the hybrid battery scanned with the rest of the truck.

## Driveline

- **10-speed (2017 on):** warm, it should shift without clunks or shudder. Harsh 1–2 engagements, slipping, or a delay into reverse means a transmission scan before any money moves. Confirm the big **2026 park-system recall**, over 741,000 trucks, is closed by VIN.
- **4x4:** engage 4A, 4H and 4L on the test drive, listening for grinding at the transfer case. On Raptors, assume off-road use and inspect accordingly.

## Body and bed

- **Aluminum body (2015 on):** no rust, but repairs demand certified shops. Sight down each panel in low light for filler waves, check panel gaps at the doors and bed, and look underneath the bed floor for impact dents. Cheap crash repair is this generation's version of rot.
- **Steel frame:** in salt states, inspect the frame, not the body, for corrosion, especially 2015–2017 trucks that lived outside.

## Paper

- **Recalls closed, by VIN,** at the dealer or NHTSA's site: seat belts and brake master cylinders on 2015–2017, tailgate latches, the 2026 transmission park-system campaign.
- **Towing history in writing** if the hitch shows wear: what was towed, how far, how often. An honest answer tells you about the seller as well as the truck.
- **Service intervals:** EcoBoosts forgive little about neglected oil. 2 missing years of records outweigh 20 careful answers.

If the seller balks at any of this, there are, by the end of 2025 alone, 828,832 newer F-Series trucks to choose from. Walk.
