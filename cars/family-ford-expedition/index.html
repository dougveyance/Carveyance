The Ford Expedition family

5 generations and variants in the Index, 1997–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1997–2002

    Ford Expedition (first generation)

    Ford retired the Bronco, stretched an F-150 into 4 doors and 3 rows, and the Expedition invented the modern full-size family SUV — 1.27 million sold in 6 years.

    260 hp9.5 sec 0-6098 mph top1,274,308 built
    5.4 4WD · 4.6 V8 · 1997 XLT 5.4 4WD, as tested · Eddie Bauer
  2. 2003–2006

    Ford Expedition (second generation)

    A hydroformed frame and the first independent rear suspension in a full-size SUV — the 2003 Expedition rode like no truck before it, and the 3rd row finally folded flat into the floor.

    260 hp9.9 sec 0-6098 mph top542,733 built
    5.4 4WD (2003–04) · 4.6 V8 (2003–04) · 5.4 3-valve (2005–06)
  3. 2007–2017

    Ford Expedition (third generation)

    11 years, one body: the 3rd-generation Expedition rode out $4 gas and the financial crisis, gained a foot-longer EL, and finally got the 365-hp EcoBoost that made it quick.

    365 hp6.5 sec 0-60105 mph top529,105 built
    3.5 EcoBoost (2015–17) · 5.4 (2007–08) · 5.4 flex-fuel (2009–14) · Expedition EL
  4. 2018–2024

    Ford Expedition (fourth generation)

    Aluminum body, 10-speed, up to 440 hp: the 2018–24 Expedition went from family hauler to a 2,601-kg (5,735-lb) truck that hits 60 in 4.9 seconds.

    440 hp5.1 sec 0-60110 mph top514,347 built
    Timberline (2022–24) · 3.5 EcoBoost, 375 hp (2018–21) · Platinum, 400 hp · Limited Stealth, 440 hp (2022–24) · Expedition Max
  5. 2025–present

    Ford Expedition (fifth generation)

    Split Gate tailgate, a 440-hp option, a Tremor on 33s — and a tested 4.9 to 60: the 2025 Expedition is the quickest, strangest-tailgated one yet.

    440 hp4.9 sec 0-60124 mph top85,921 built
