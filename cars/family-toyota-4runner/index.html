The Toyota 4Runner family

6 generations and variants in the Index, 1984–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1984–1989

    Toyota 4Runner (N60)

    The first 4Runner, 1984–1989: a Toyota pickup with a rear seat under a removable fiberglass top, Toyota's answer to the Bronco and Blazer.

    116 hp14.5 sec 0-6078,999 built
    2.4-liter · 3.0-liter V6
  2. 1990–1995

    Toyota 4Runner (N120/N130)

    The second 4Runner, 1990–1995: a proper all-steel SUV body on Hilux underpinnings, with the old roll-down tailgate glass.

    150 hp15.7 sec 0-60329,814 built
    3.0-liter V6 · 2.4-liter
  3. 1996–2002

    Toyota 4Runner (N180)

    The third 4Runner, 1996–2002: a clean-sheet redesign on the Land Cruiser Prado's chassis, with coil springs all around and a 183-hp V6.

    183 hp9.5 sec 0-60749,871 built
    3.4-liter V6 · 2.7-liter
  4. 2003–2009

    Toyota 4Runner (N210)

    The fourth 4Runner, 2003–2009: built on the Land Cruiser Prado 120, with a 245-hp V6 or, for the first time, a V8.

    235 hp8.0 sec 0-60585,707 built
    4.7-liter V8 · 4.0-liter V6
  5. 2010–2024

    Toyota 4Runner (N280)

    Toyota's old-school 4Runner: a 4.0-liter V6, a 5-speed automatic and a ladder frame, sold for 15 model years with barely a change. Slow and thirsty by modern standards, and hugely popular anyway, because it goes almost anywhere and keeps going.

    270 hp7.7 sec 0-60114 mph top1,480,346 built
  6. 2025–present

    Toyota 4Runner (N500)

    The 4Runner reinvented for 2025: the same body-on-frame toughness and roll-down rear window, but a turbocharged 4-cylinder or a 465 lb-ft hybrid in place of the old V6, 8 gears and a cabin from this decade.

    278 hp7.5 sec 0-60217,414 built
    2.4-liter turbo · i-FORCE MAX hybrid
