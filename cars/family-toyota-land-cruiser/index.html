The Toyota Land Cruiser family

11 generations and variants in the Index, 1951–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1951–1955

    Toyota Land Cruiser (BJ and FJ)

    The Toyota Jeep BJ, 1951–1955: the military-style 4x4 that climbed to the sixth stage of Mount Fuji and became the Land Cruiser in 1954.

    84 hp874 built
  2. 1955–1960

    Toyota Land Cruiser (20 Series)

    The 20 Series Land Cruiser, 1955–1960: a more civilian Land Cruiser with a 3.9-liter 6-cylinder, the first Toyota built outside Japan and the first Japanese vehicle regularly exported to Australia.

    133 hp15.0 sec 0-60
  3. 1960–1984

    Toyota Land Cruiser (40 Series)

    The 40 Series Land Cruiser, 1960–1984: the classic short, boxy FJ40, finally with low-range gearing, and for a time Toyota's best-selling vehicle in America.

    135 hp14.0 sec 0-601,100,000 built
  4. 1967–1980

    Toyota Land Cruiser (55 Series)

    The 55 Series Land Cruiser, 1967–1980: the "Moose," the first true Land Cruiser station wagon, built mainly for North America and Australia.

    135 hp16.0 sec 0-60113,000 built
  5. 1980–1990

    Toyota Land Cruiser (60 Series)

    The 60 Series Land Cruiser, 1980–1990: a bigger, more comfortable wagon with air conditioning, and from 1987 the fuel-injected FJ62.

    155 hp14.2 sec 0-6045,489 built
    4.0-liter (FJ62) · 4.2-liter (FJ60)
  6. 1984–present

    Toyota Land Cruiser (70 Series)

    The 70 Series Land Cruiser, 1984 to today: the heavy-duty workhorse that replaced the 40, still in production more than 40 years later.

    202 hp14.6 sec 0-60
  7. 1990–1997

    Toyota Land Cruiser (80 Series)

    The 80 Series Land Cruiser, 1990–1997: full-time 4-wheel drive, available locking differentials front and rear, and a 212-hp 4.5-liter from 1993.

    155 hp14.1 sec 0-6074,796 built
    4.0-liter · 4.5-liter
  8. 1998–2007

    Toyota Land Cruiser (100 Series)

    The 100 Series Land Cruiser, 1998–2007: the first Land Cruiser with independent front suspension and the first with a V8, which was the only engine in America.

    230 hp9.4 sec 0-6087,727 built
  9. 2008–2021

    Toyota Land Cruiser (200 Series)

    The last V8 Land Cruiser sold in America: a 381-hp, 8-seat luxury 4x4 with full-time 4WD, KDSS suspension and an 8,100-lb (3,674-kg) tow rating. A 2017 started at $84,775, about $116,300 in 2026 dollars.

    381 hp6.7 sec 0-60114 mph top41,834 built
  10. 2021–present

    Toyota Land Cruiser (300 Series)

    The 300 Series Land Cruiser, 2021 to today: the global flagship that replaced the 200, with twin-turbo V6 engines and about 200 kg less weight, sold almost everywhere but the US and Canada.

    409 hp6.3 sec 0-60105,700 built
  11. 2024–present

    Toyota Land Cruiser (250 Series)

    The Land Cruiser's US comeback: a boxy 5-seat hybrid 4x4 with full-time 4WD, center and rear lockers and 23 mpg combined, starting at $55,950 for 2024, about $60,000 in 2026 dollars, against about $106,300 for the last V8.

    326 hp7.7 sec 0-60100,880 built
