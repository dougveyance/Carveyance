Put a 2021 next to a 2025 and it's tempting to call the new one an upgrade. It isn't, quite. They share a name and real off-road ability, but they sit at different points between luxury and utility, and neither wins just because it has more cylinders or more technology.

## Where each one wins

| | 2017–2021 200 Series | 2024–2026 250 Series |
|---|---|---|
| Power | **381 hp** | 326 hp |
| Torque | 401 lb-ft | **465 lb-ft** |
| 0–60 mph, Car and Driver | **6.7 seconds** | 7.7 seconds |
| Seats | **Up to 8** | 5 |
| Tows up to | **3,674 kg (8,100 lb)** | 2,722 kg (6,000 lb) |
| EPA combined | 14–15 mpg | **23 mpg** |
| Front anti-roll bar | KDSS, adjusts itself | **Disconnects at a button**, Land Cruiser grade |
| Locking differentials | Center | **Center and rear** |
| Apple CarPlay and Android Auto | No | **Yes** |

## Fuel

[[chart:mpg]]

[[chart:fuelcost]]

At 15,000 miles a year and $4 a gallon, the hybrid saves about $1,677 a year. That's a real difference, but buy the 250 Series for the whole package, not for the fuel alone. Towing, roof racks, big tires and cold weather lower both figures.

## Towing

[[chart:tow]]

The V8 is rated for 952 kg (2,100 lb) more. If your trailer is heavier than 2,722 kg (6,000 lb), the choice is made for you. If it's lighter, both will do, and payload is usually the limit. Check the door-jamb label of the truck you're buying.

## Living with the V8

The 200 Series is effortless, quiet and roomy, with a cabin made for long days and a third row for bigger families. The trade is the fuel bill and the cost of age: suspension, 4WD parts and the KDSS system can all be expensive to put right on a neglected truck. Condition and records matter far more than the badge.

## Living with the hybrid

The 250 Series is easier to park, stronger off the line and better equipped for hard trails, with its rear locker and disconnecting anti-roll bar. It's also narrower in purpose: 5 seats, less towing and a newer, more complex powertrain with recalls still being worked through. Anyone expecting the old luxury cruiser may find it more utilitarian.

## The decision

| If you need... | Buy |
|---|---|
| 6 to 8 seats | 2017–2021 200 Series |
| To tow more than 2,722 kg (6,000 lb) | 2017–2021 200 Series |
| The most capable trail truck | 2024–2026 Land Cruiser grade |
| Lower fuel bills and modern tech | 2024–2026 250 Series |
| The lowest price for a nearly new one | 2025–2026 Land Cruiser 1958 |

If you like the 250 Series but need a third row, look at the [Sequoia](/fitdrive/guides/toyota-sequoia/) or a Lexus GX 550. If you like the 200 Series but want something newer, the Lexus LX 600 is the closest thing on sale.

::: curiosity A Name, Not a Platform
The 2024 Land Cruiser isn't a smaller-engined 200 Series. It's the 250 Series, sold as the Land Cruiser Prado in many markets, while the 200 Series' real successor, the 300 Series, never came to America at all. Lexus sells it here as the LX 600. That one fact explains more of the difference between old and new than any brochure.
:::
