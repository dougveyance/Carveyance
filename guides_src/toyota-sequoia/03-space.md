This is the chapter for anyone whose third row is full and whose cargo area is full at the same time. On that measure, the old Sequoia wins clearly, even though it's the older design.

## The numbers

[[chart:cargo]]

With every seat up, the 2017–2022 Sequoia has 18.9 cu ft behind the third row. The 2023–2026 model has between 11.5 and 22.3 cu ft, depending on where you slide the third row. Fold the third row and the old one has 66.6 cu ft against 49.0. Fold both rear rows and it's 120.1 against 86.9. The new Sequoia gives up about 28% of its maximum space, and the third row no longer folds into a flat floor.

The seats changed too. The old Sequoia's third row has 35.3 inches of legroom. The new one has between 28.1 and 33.7 inches, depending on the slide, and second-row legroom dropped from 40.9 to 39.2 inches.

## Why it shrank

Reviewers point to 2 causes. The new Sequoia uses a solid rear axle instead of the old one's independent rear suspension, and a solid axle needs room to move up and down under the cargo floor. And the hybrid's battery takes space of its own. The result is a cargo floor that sits higher, so the seats can't fold flat into it.

Toyota's answer is an adjustable cargo shelf that creates a level load surface over the folded third row, or splits the space into 2 levels. It helps, but the shelf sits high, so lifting heavy items in is harder, and the total volume is still smaller.

## The sliding third row

The new third row slides fore and aft by 6 inches, and the seatbacks fold 60/40 with power on every grade from 2026. Slide it forward and there's 22.3 cu ft behind it, enough for several carry-on bags. Slide it back for taller passengers and the space drops to 11.5. It's a clever way to share limited room between knees and suitcases, and it's a problem the old Sequoia mostly didn't have.

## How to choose

- **Seats full and lots of luggage:** the 2017–2022 Sequoia, or a Chevrolet Suburban or Ford Expedition Max.
- **Towing a big trailer with a smaller crew:** the 2023–2026 Sequoia.
- **Adults in the third row every day:** test both with your own passengers. The old one has more legroom, and the new one's slide helps only if you give up cargo space.
- **Strollers, wheelchairs or dog crates:** bring them to the test drive. Load height and a flat floor may matter more than the cubic feet.

::: curiosity A Third Row That Slides
J.D. Power describes the 2023 Sequoia's sliding third row as an industry exclusive. Most rivals let you slide the second row to make room in the third. The Sequoia moves the third row itself, which is the only way to buy back cargo space without folding a seat.
:::
