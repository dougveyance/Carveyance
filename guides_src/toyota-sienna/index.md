The Sienna is Toyota's minivan, built in Princeton, Indiana, and it covers 2 very different generations in this guide. The 2017–2020 cars are the end of the third generation, with a 296-hp V6, an 8-speed automatic and, for years, the only all-wheel drive in the minivan class. The 2021 redesign made every Sienna a hybrid: 245 hp, all-wheel drive from a rear electric motor, and an EPA rating of 36 mpg combined, against 21 or 22 for the V6.

For a used buyer, the choice of generation is mostly a choice between power and fuel economy, with seating and recalls close behind. This guide has 4 chapters: one for each generation, one on choosing between the V6 and the hybrid, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2021 or later Sienna XLE, rated at 36 mpg combined with front-wheel drive.
- **Best value:** a documented 2019–2020 Sienna LE or XLE, with Apple CarPlay as standard and the newer V6.
- **Most luxury:** a 2021 or later Limited or Platinum, or a 2025 or later Platinum with the built-in vacuum and fridge.
- **Best in snow:** any all-wheel-drive Sienna. The hybrid's system costs less fuel than the V6's.
- **Most flexible cargo space:** a 2017–2020 car, whose second-row seats come out.
- **Think twice if** you need to remove the second row. On the hybrid, the captain's chairs have built-in airbags and stay put.

## At a glance

|  | 2017–2020 (third generation) | 2021–2026 (fourth generation) |
|---|---|---|
| Powertrain | 3.5-liter V6, 296 hp, 8-speed automatic | 2.5-liter 4-cylinder hybrid, 245 hp, CVT |
| All-wheel drive | Mechanical, on LE, XLE and Limited; SE from 2019 | Electric rear motor, on every grade |
| EPA combined | 22 mpg for 2017, then 21; AWD 20 | 36 mpg; AWD 35 |
| 0–60 mph (Car and Driver test) | 6.8 seconds | 7.7 seconds |
| Second-row seats | Removable | Outboard seats fixed; middle seat removable on 8-seaters |
| Apple CarPlay | From 2019 | Standard |
| Base price new | L $29,750 for 2017 ($40,800 in 2026 dollars) | LE $34,460 for 2021 ($42,800 in 2026 dollars) |
