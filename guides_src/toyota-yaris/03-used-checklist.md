Small, cheap cars often live hard lives: city commuting, curb strikes, delivery work and oil changes only when something goes wrong. A low asking price can disappear fast once you replace 4 tires, the brakes and worn suspension parts. A well-kept car with higher mileage can be a safer buy than a neglected one with low miles.

## First, know which Yaris it is

Listings often just say "Yaris," so check the VIN and the badge. A 2017–2018 hatchback is the Toyota-designed car built in France. A 2017–2018 Yaris iA, a 2019–2020 Yaris Sedan and a 2020 Yaris Hatchback are Mazda-designed cars built in Mexico. They don't share engines, transmissions or most parts, so make sure the parts quotes and service records match the actual car.

## The years at a glance

| Model years | What it is | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 2017 | Toyota-built hatchback. Mazda-built Yaris iA sedan | Side-airbag wiring recall on the hatchback (19V-319). Spare-tire recall on some hatchbacks (17V-295) |
| 2018 | Hatchback renamed Liftback, restyled. Yaris iA carries on | Run the VIN |
| 2019 | Hatchback dropped. The iA renamed Yaris Sedan, with an XLE grade | Fuel pump recall (21V-617). Load-label recall (19V-244) |
| 2020 | Final year. Mazda-built Yaris Hatchback added | Fuel pump recall (21V-617) |

## The test drive

Drive it on the freeway as well as in town, and merge at full throttle to hear how it copes. Then park it in the kinds of spaces you use every day. On a manual, check the clutch for slipping and the gears for grinding. On an automatic, check for prompt, smooth shifts. Test the air conditioning on a hot day, the heater, every window and lock, and the backup camera.

## Look for hard use

Check the wheels and tires for curb damage, and the tires for uneven wear, which points to alignment or suspension problems. Look for repainted panels and uneven gaps that suggest accident repairs. Check the hatch struts on a hatchback, the seats and the controls for wear, and the service records for regular oil changes. Ask whether the car was used for ride-hailing or deliveries.

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **2017 hatchback:** 19V-319. The wiring for the side-airbag sensors in the front doors can crack and corrode, so the side and curtain airbags may not deploy properly. About 43,200 cars from 2015–2017. The fix is a new door wiring harness.
- **2017:** 17V-295. Some cars sold through Gulf States Toyota had a spare tire inflated below the pressure on the placard.
- **2019–2020 Mazda-built cars:** 21V-617. The low-pressure fuel pump can fail, and the engine can stall while driving. Mazda traced it to exposure to high temperatures. About 31,300 cars. The fix is a new pump.
- **2019:** 19V-244. The load-capacity label on some cars can become unreadable, so a driver may unknowingly overload the car.
- **Every year:** run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls), and don't rely on recalls reported for another Yaris generation or a Yaris sold in another country.

## Walk away if

The car has a branded title, signs of flood damage or a slipping clutch the seller won't discount. Walk away, too, if the price only looks good because it ignores the tires, brakes and service the car needs now.

## What is it worth?

We don't publish average prices. They go stale quickly, and an average that mixes a 2017 hatchback with a 2020 sedan tells you very little. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same year, body, transmission and mileage, with the same title history, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what cars actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you. Price a Honda Fit and a Corolla with the same mileage too, and take the cost of tires, brakes and overdue service off the price.

Buying from a private seller? Service plan providers to compare include [Chaiz](https://www.chaiz.com), [Endurance](https://www.endurancewarranty.com) and [Olive](https://www.olive.com). Carveyance doesn't rank or recommend them. Read what each one covers and excludes, and see the [FTC's guidance on auto service contracts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/what-know-about-auto-service-contracts-extended-warranty-scams) first.

## Our shortlist

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2019–2020 Yaris Sedan with records and its fuel pump recall done.
- **Most fun:** a Yaris iA or Yaris Sedan with the 6-speed manual.
- **Most practical:** a 2020 Yaris Hatchback, or a 2017–2018 Toyota-built 5-door.
- **Walk away from:** any Yaris whose service history is a mystery and whose price doesn't reflect it.
