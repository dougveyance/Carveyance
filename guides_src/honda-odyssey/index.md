The Odyssey is Honda's minivan, sold in North America since 1994, and since the 1999 model it has been a big American van with a V6, 2 sliding doors and a third row that folds into the floor. It's built in Lincoln, Alabama, and it drives more like a large car than most of its rivals. Between 2017 and 2026, Honda sold 2 generations: the last year of the 2011–2017 van, with a 248-hp V6 and a 6-speed automatic, and the fifth generation from 2018, with a 280-hp V6, a 9- or 10-speed automatic and Magic Slide second-row seats.

For a used buyer, the choices are the generation, the gearbox on 2018–2019 vans, and the trim, which decides the seats and the family-entertainment equipment. This guide has 3 chapters: the 2017 van, the 2018–2026 van, and the checklist to use before you buy.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2021 or later EX-L, with Honda Sensing standard, the 10-speed and Magic Slide seats.
- **Best value:** a 2018–2020 EX or EX-L with its door and seat recalls done.
- **Best for long trips with children:** a Touring or Elite, with CabinWatch, CabinTalk and rear entertainment.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2017 EX or SE, the last of the old van, with its second-row seat bracket fitted.
- **Think twice if** the sliding doors stick, the seller can't show the recall repairs, or the timing belt is overdue.
