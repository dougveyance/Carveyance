The Ford Super Duty family

5 generations and variants in the Index, 1999–present. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

    Ford Super Duty (first generation)

    The 1999 split that created the modern heavy-duty truck: its own frame, its own face, the 7.3 Power Stroke — and 1 million built inside 3 years.

    Ford Super Duty (second generation)
