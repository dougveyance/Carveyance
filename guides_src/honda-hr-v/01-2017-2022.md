The second-generation HR-V reached the US in May 2015 as a 2016 model, and it's essentially a taller, more rugged-looking Honda Fit. That's a compliment. It shares the Fit's platform and its Magic Seat, which lets the rear seat work 2 ways: fold the backs down for a flat load floor, or flip the cushions up to leave a floor-to-roof space behind the front seats for a bicycle, a tall plant or a box that won't lie down. It's easy to park, easy to see out of and cheap to run.

## The engine and gearboxes

[[chart:power]]

Every one has a 1.8-liter 4-cylinder with 141 hp and 127 lb-ft. Most have a CVT, but front-drive LX and EX models could have a 6-speed manual through 2018. All-wheel drive was available on every trim with the CVT. It isn't quick: Car and Driver ran a front-drive EX-L Navi to 60 mph in 8.6 seconds, a manual EX in 8.4, and an all-wheel-drive 2019 Touring needed 10.2. The EPA rated a 2017 front-drive CVT at 31 mpg combined and an all-wheel-drive one at 29; the manual is rated at 28.

## Space

Behind the rear seat there's 24.3 cubic feet with front-wheel drive, and with it folded, 58.8 in the LX and a little less in the higher trims. Those are big numbers for a car only 169.1 inches long. The rear seat is roomy enough for adults, and the cargo floor is low.

## The years

The 2016 and 2017 HR-V came as an LX, EX or EX-L Navi, and Honda's LaneWatch camera was on the EX and EX-L Navi. The 2019 refresh brought a new grille, headlights and bumpers, more sound deadening, a new touchscreen with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto on the Sport and above, and 2 new trims: the Sport, between the LX and EX, and the Touring at the top, with all-wheel drive, LED headlights and navigation. It also made Honda Sensing standard on the EX and above and dropped the manual. The Touring was dropped for 2022, the last year of this generation.

## What to check before you buy

Some 2016 and 2018–2019 HR-Vs are covered by a brake caliper recall (18V777). On 2019–2022 cars, the rearview camera image may not appear when the car is started with a key, fixed first with new software (23V046) and then again for cars that still had the fault (24V384). Fuel pump campaigns cover some 2018–2022 cars, and 2019–2022 cars have passenger seat weight sensor recalls. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

A 2019–2022 EX is the sweet spot: Honda Sensing, the newer touchscreen and the Magic Seat, for less than an EX-L. If you want the cheapest HR-V and can drive a manual, a 2016–2018 LX or EX with the 6-speed is the most fun version and the quickest one Car and Driver tested. Skip all-wheel drive unless you need it; it costs fuel and makes the HR-V slower still.

::: curiosity A Seat That Folds Up as Well as Down
The Magic Seat came from the Fit, Honda's subcompact hatchback, and the HR-V kept it for its whole second generation. The 2023 model, built on a different platform, dropped it.
:::
