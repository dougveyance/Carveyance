The Mustang Mach-E is Ford's electric crossover, built in Cuautitlán Izcalli, Mexico, and on sale in the US since the 2021 model year. It shares a name and some styling cues with the Mustang coupe, but it's a 5-seat, 5-door crossover with a hatch and a front trunk. In 2025 it sold 51,620 in the US, nearly matching the record of 51,745 set in 2024.

What makes a Mach-E hard to shop is that the name covers many different cars. There are standard-range and extended-range batteries, rear-wheel drive and all-wheel drive, and the higher-performance GT and Rally. A listing that just says "Mach-E Premium" doesn't tell you enough. Confirm the battery, the motor layout and the EPA rating for that exact configuration before you compare prices or range. This guide has 4 chapters.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a 2023–2024 extended-range Premium with rear-wheel drive and its recalls done. The EPA rates the 2024 version at 320 miles.
- **Best for cold climates:** a 2025 or later Mach-E. Every one has a vapor-injection heat pump.
- **Quickest:** the GT, or the Rally from 2024. Car and Driver ran a 2024 Rally to 60 mph in 3.3 seconds.
- **Lowest price new:** the 2026 Select, from $37,795.
- **Think twice if** you can't charge at home, or your car is a 2021–2022 with an extended-range battery and no record of its junction-box recall.
