The Porsche 944 / 968 family

2 generations and variants in the Index, 1982–1995. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1982–1991

    Porsche 944

    The front-engined Porsche that sold best until the Boxster: balanced, practical and quick, with its own Porsche-built engine. Porsche made 163,192.

  2. 1993–1995

    Porsche 968 Club Sport

    A lighter, lowered, stripped-out 968 for drivers who wanted the drive and nothing else, with a 3.0-liter four, a rear transaxle and near-perfect balance. Walter Röhrl is widely quoted calling it the best-handling Porsche he ever drove, and not one was sold new in America.
