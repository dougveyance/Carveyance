The C-HR name covers a gas crossover that's now 4 to 9 years old and a nearly new electric one, so check the right things for the car in front of you. Either way, judge it by its records and an inspection, and confirm the recalls by VIN.

## First, know which one it is

A gas C-HR is a 2018–2022 model, with a tailpipe and a fuel door. An electric C-HR is a 2026 or later model, with a NACS charging port and no tailpipe. There were no 2023–2025 C-HRs in the US. On a gas car, confirm the grade: the 2018 came as XLE or XLE Premium, and 2019–2022 cars as LE, XLE or Limited, with a Nightshade Edition from 2021.

## The years at a glance

| Model years | What they are | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| 2018 | Gas C-HR launches: XLE, XLE Premium | Electronic parking brake (17V-717) |
| 2019 | LE, XLE, Limited. Apple CarPlay standard | Rear hub bolts on some cars (18V-710). Rear center seat belt (19V-877). Load label (19V-244) |
| 2020 | Refresh: LED headlights, Android Auto | Rear center seat belt (19V-877) |
| 2021 | Nightshade Edition | Forward collision radar (22V-107) |
| 2022 | Last gas C-HR in the US | Run the VIN |
| 2026 | Electric C-HR: 338 hp, AWD | Battery software, loss of drive power (26V-570) |

## Gas checks

Ask for the oil-change history, and look for leaks and uneven tire wear. Apply and release the electronic parking brake several times; a warning that reads "EPB Malfunction" points to the 2018 recall. Drive it on the freeway to make sure the CVT pulls smoothly, with no shudder, slipping or whine. A 2018 has no Apple CarPlay or Android Auto, and a 2019 has CarPlay only, so budget for that if your phone matters.

## Electric checks

Don't judge the battery by the range shown on the dashboard, which depends on temperature, speed and the last driver. Ask a Toyota dealer for a battery health check if one is available, and look for any high-voltage warnings. Charge the car during the inspection, on a DC fast charger if you can, and make sure the charging cable comes with it. Toyota covers the battery and electric drive components for 8 years or 100,000 miles from the date the car was first sold, so every electric C-HR is still within the time limit.

## Recalls: check by VIN

- **2018:** 17V-717. An oxide film on the electronic parking brake motor can make the system read a fault. The brake may then not release, or in some cases not apply. The fix is a software update.
- **2019:** 18V-710. On some cars, the rear hub bearing bolts may not have been tightened enough at the factory. They can loosen, damage the rear brakes or let a rear wheel come off. Dealers inspect the bolts and replace the hub and carrier if needed. 19V-244: the load label can become unreadable.
- **2019–2020:** 19V-877. A locking mechanism in the rear center seat belt may have been assembled incorrectly, so it may not restrain a passenger properly. Dealers replace affected belts.
- **2021:** 22V-107. The forward collision radar on some cars may not work as designed. Run the VIN for the details and the fix.
- **2026:** 26V-570. A software error can let regenerative braking overcharge a full or nearly full battery, which shuts down the electric drive and cuts power while driving. The fix is a software update.
- **Every year:** run the VIN at [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Walk away if

The car has a branded title or signs of flood damage, which is especially serious on an electric car. Walk away from parking brake or high-voltage warnings the seller can't explain, an electric C-HR that won't charge, and any seller who won't allow an inspection.

## What is it worth?

We don't publish average prices. They go stale quickly, and an average that mixes a 2018 gas XLE with a 2026 electric XSE tells you very little. Compare 20 to 30 listings for the same year, grade and mileage, with the same title history, and keep dealer asking prices separate from what cars actually sold for. [FitDrive's deal check](/fitdrive/) does the first part for you. Cross-shop a gas C-HR against a Corolla Cross and other small crossovers, and an electric one against Toyota's bZ and other compact EVs, and take the cost of tires, brakes and overdue service off the price.

Buying from a private seller, or keeping the C-HR past its factory warranty? Service plan providers to compare include [Chaiz](https://www.chaiz.com), [Endurance](https://www.endurancewarranty.com) and [Olive](https://www.olive.com). Carveyance doesn't rank or recommend them. Read what each one covers and excludes, especially for the CVT on a gas car and the battery and electric drive on an electric one, and see the [FTC's guidance on auto service contracts](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2023/03/what-know-about-auto-service-contracts-extended-warranty-scams) first.

## Our shortlist

- **Best gas C-HR:** a 2020–2022 XLE or Limited with its recalls done.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2019 LE with records.
- **Best electric C-HR:** a 2026 SE with 26V-570 done.
