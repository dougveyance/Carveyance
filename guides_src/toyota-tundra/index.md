Toyota builds every Tundra in San Antonio, Texas, and for most of the last 2 decades its pitch was simple: a big V8, a 6-speed automatic and a reputation for running forever. That truck lasted through 2021. For 2022, Toyota replaced it with an all-new Tundra on a new frame, with a twin-turbo V6, a 10-speed automatic, coil springs at the back and an optional hybrid called i-FORCE MAX.

The new truck is stronger, more efficient and far more modern. It also comes with a complication the old one never had: a series of recalls for machining debris inside the gas engine. This guide has 6 chapters, including one on that recall. Start with the generation you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a documented 2020–2021 5.7-liter V8. Simple, strong and finally fitted with Apple CarPlay and Android Auto.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2025–2026 SR5 or Limited with the twin-turbo V6, or a 2022–2024 with the engine recall work documented.
- **Strongest:** the i-FORCE MAX hybrid, with 583 lb-ft. It's standard on the TRD Pro and Capstone.
- **Best for towing:** a 2022–2026 gas V6, rated for up to 5,443 kg (12,000 lb) when properly equipped.
- **Think twice if** you rarely tow or haul. A Tundra is a lot of truck to park and fuel, and a Tacoma or a 3-row SUV may do the job for less.

## At a glance

|  | 2017–2021 V8 | 2022–2026 twin-turbo V6 | 2022–2026 hybrid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine | 4.6-liter V8 (through 2019) or 5.7-liter V8 | 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 | 3.4-liter twin-turbo V6 with an electric motor |
| Transmission | 6-speed automatic | 10-speed automatic | 10-speed automatic |
| Power | 310 hp or 381 hp | 389 hp (SR: 348 hp at launch) | 437 hp |
| Torque | 327 or 401 lb-ft | 479 lb-ft (SR: 405) | 583 lb-ft |
| Rear suspension | Leaf springs | Coil springs, air springs available | Coil springs, air springs available |
| EPA combined, 4x4 | 14 mpg (5.7) | 19 mpg | 20 mpg |
| Tows up to | 4,763 kg (10,500 lb) | 5,443 kg (12,000 lb) | Varies by grade. A 2022 Capstone: 4,690 kg (10,340 lb) |
