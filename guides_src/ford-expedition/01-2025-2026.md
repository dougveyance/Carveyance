Revealed October 3, 2024 and launched at $63,695, the 5th-generation Expedition leads with the Split Gate: a 2-piece tailgate whose lower half folds down like a pickup's, with an available package of cushions and cupholders aimed straight at the stadium parking lot. The old XL, XLT and Limited trims are gone. For 2026 the ladder runs Active at $62,700, Platinum at $74,250, the off-road Tremor at $81,700 and King Ranch at $84,360, each in standard or long-body Max form, rear- or 4-wheel drive.

## 2 engines, 1 decision

Every truck runs a 3.5 EcoBoost twin-turbo V6 and the 10-speed. The standard tune makes 400 hp; the high-output version makes 440 and is standard on the Tremor, optional on the Platinum. Car and Driver ran a Max Platinum with the 440 to 60 mph in 4.9 seconds — the quickest Expedition ever tested, in the longest, plushest body. EPA combined is 18 mpg with 4-wheel drive either way, and towing peaks at 4,354 kg (9,600 lb) with the heavy-duty package, 3,175 kg (7,000 lb) standard.

The honest advice: the 400 is plenty, and the $62,700 Active with the tow package is the value play of the range. Edmunds' pick is the Platinum, which gets you the big interior upgrades and the option of the 440. The Tremor — 33-inch all-terrains, steel front bumper, standard high-output — is the first factory off-road Expedition, and it reads as Ford's answer to the Tahoe Z71 with twice the horsepower of the idea.

## What to know before ordering

The cabin moves to a 24-inch panoramic display up high and a lower touchscreen, a layout shared with the Lincoln lineup — try it before committing, because it reorganizes every control. BlueCruise hands-free highway driving is on the options sheet, and the Max body adds real cargo depth behind the 3rd row for the usual $3,000-ish walk. First-year builds of any new generation deserve a VIN check for open campaigns; the truck is built alongside the Super Duty at the Kentucky Truck Plant in Louisville. Ford sold 85,921 Expeditions in changeover-year 2025, so used examples of the new body are already appearing with light miles.
