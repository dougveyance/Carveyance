The Toyota Supra family

5 generations and variants in the Index, 1979–2026. Oldest first; every card opens the full page, with its own versions, figures and story.

  1. 1979–1981

    Toyota Celica Supra (A40)

    The first Celica Supra, 1979–1981: a Celica with a longer nose and an inline-6, built because Toyota's American dealers wanted a rival to the Datsun 280Z.

    110 hp11.2 sec 0-6063,895 built
  2. 1982–1986

    Toyota Celica Supra (A60)

    The second Celica Supra, 1982–1986: pop-up headlights, a twin-cam inline-6 and suspension tuned with help from Lotus, in P-type and L-type forms.

    145 hp8.8 sec 0-60114,459 built
  3. 1986–1993

    Toyota Supra (A70)

    The third Supra, 1986–1993: no longer a Celica, with rear-wheel drive, double wishbones at all 4 corners and, from 1987, a 230-hp turbo.

    200 hp8.0 sec 0-60108,565 built
    3.0-liter · Turbo
  4. 1993–2002

    Toyota Supra (A80)

    The fourth Supra, 1993–2002: a sequential twin-turbo inline-6 with 320 hp and a Getrag 6-speed, and a 4.6-second run to 60 in Car and Driver's hands.

    220 hp6.2 sec 0-6012,059 built
    3.0-liter · Turbo
  5. 2020–2026

    Toyota GR Supra (A90)

    The Supra name's return, built in Austria alongside the BMW Z4 with a BMW turbocharged straight-six. Small, quick and serious, with a 382-hp 3.0 from 2021, a lighter 2.0 for 4 years and, from 2023, a manual.

    382 hp3.9 sec 0-60155 mph top31,228 built
