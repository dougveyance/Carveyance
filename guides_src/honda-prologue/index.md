The Prologue is a 5-seat electric SUV that Honda developed with General Motors. It sits on GM's Ultium platform, shares its battery and motors with the Chevrolet Blazer EV and is built by GM in Ramos Arizpe, Mexico, but it was designed, inside and out, at Honda's design studio in Los Angeles. It's roomy and quiet, with an 85-kWh battery, front-wheel drive or dual-motor all-wheel drive, and an EPA range of up to 308 miles.

It was sold for 3 model years. The 2024 went on sale in March 2024, the 2025 got more power and range at the same price, and in April 2026 Honda cut the price of the 2026 by $7,500. In July 2026, Honda confirmed that production would end with the 2026 model year, so the only new ones left are 2026 models in dealer stock, and Honda says owners will keep getting service, parts and warranty support through its dealers.

This guide has 3 chapters: the 2024 Prologue, the 2025–2026 Prologue, and the checklist to use before you buy, with what to ask about the battery and the charging.

## Quick picks

- **Best all-rounder:** a 2025 or 2026 EX or Touring with front-wheel drive, for the longest range, 308 miles.
- **Best value:** a 2026 EX bought at or near the April 2026 price, $39,900, or a used one priced against it.
- **Best in snow:** a 2025 or 2026 EX or Touring with all-wheel drive, rated at 294 miles.
- **Cheapest way in:** a 2024 with its recalls done and a battery report, priced well below a 2025.
- **Think twice if** you can't charge at home, or you want the quickest charging; the Prologue's peak DC rate is 150 kW.
