The Corolla Cross is the answer to a question a lot of shoppers ask: can I have a Corolla, but as an SUV? Toyota's answer arrived for 2022, built on the Corolla's platform, sized between the C-HR and the RAV4 and pitched as Toyota's entry-level SUV.

It's a sensible, efficient, unexciting small crossover, and that's fine. What matters most is which version you buy. The gas model is cheap and slow. The hybrid, added for 2023, is quicker, more frugal and comes with all-wheel drive as standard. This guide has 4 chapters. Start with the review, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best overall:** a 2023–2026 Corolla Cross Hybrid SE. Quicker, more efficient and all-wheel drive as standard.
- **Best value:** a 2022–2025 gas LE with all-wheel drive, if the price gap to a hybrid is large.
- **Newest look:** a 2026 Corolla Cross, with the new front end and the available 10.5-inch screen.
- **Think twice if** you regularly carry adults in the back or need lots of cargo space. The rear seat is tight, and a RAV4 is the bigger option.
