Accept the premise before you shop: used Transits are fleet vans, cargo examples almost without exception. Mileage runs high, interiors run scuffed, and none of it matters as much as maintenance cadence — a 150,000-mile van on a fleet service contract routinely beats a 60,000-mile van an owner-operator ran on vibes. Ask for the service printout first; fleets keep them, and a seller who can't produce one has told you something. City-delivery vans (doors slammed 200 times a day, curbs, idling) wear harder than highway shuttles at the same odometer reading, and the body tells the story: check the sliding-door tracks and rollers, the rear-door hinges, the load floor and the lower body seams where upfit racks were screwed through paint.

Mechanical checks sort by era. On 2015–19 vans, feel for 6-speed harshness, listen to the 3.7 for timing-chain rattle at cold start, and remember the 3.2 diesel is an orphan — fine when sorted, expensive when not, with emissions-system repairs the usual sore spot. EcoBoosts want evidence of oil changes (turbos punish neglect) and a look for exhaust-manifold tick. The 2020-on vans are the sweet spot: the 10-speed, available AWD and driver aids, plus the simpler port-and-direct 3.5 base engine. On any year, confirm the recall history is closed out at a dealer — a high-volume commercial vehicle accumulates campaigns — and on high-roof vans glance at the roof seams and any upfit penetrations for sealed-over leaks.

## The deal

Price follows configuration, not condition, so decide your spec and hunt it specifically. Low-roof 150s in passenger trim hold value best; high-roof extended 350s are plentiful and discount hardest; AWD and EcoBoost each carry a real premium because the camper crowd wants both. A 2015 base van listed at $29,565 new — about $41,900 in 2026 dollars — and early examples now trade in the low-to-mid teens, which is a lot of capability per dollar if the service file is thick. Buy the records, buy the right roof, and let somebody else's depreciation furnish your business — or your van life.
