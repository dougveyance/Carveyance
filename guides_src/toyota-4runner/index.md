Toyota has sold the 4Runner in America since 1984, and its reputation for going anywhere and lasting forever was earned the hard way. For anyone shopping a 2017 to 2026 model, the story splits cleanly in 2. The fifth generation ran through 2024 with a 4.0-liter V6 and a 5-speed automatic. The sixth arrived for 2025 on a new frame, with a turbocharged 4-cylinder, an optional hybrid and 8 gears. Same name, same square shoulders, very different trucks underneath.

This guide has 6 chapters. Start with the generation you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a stock, documented 2020–2024 SR5 Premium or TRD Off-Road. The 2020 update brought Apple CarPlay, Android Auto and standard Toyota Safety Sense to the proven V6 truck.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2025–2026 turbo SR5 or TRD Off-Road. More torque, better mileage and a cabin from this decade.
- **Strongest:** the i-FORCE MAX hybrid, standard on the TRD Pro, Trailhunter and Platinum.
- **Think twice if** you mostly commute on freeways or need a real 3-row family SUV. A Highlander or Grand Highlander will suit you better.
