The Bronco Sport isn't a shrunken version of the removable-door Bronco, and it's no worse for that. It's an upright, 5-seat compact crossover built on the same unibody platform as the Ford Escape, with 4-wheel drive standard and more trail ability than its neighbors in the mall parking lot. Ford builds it in Hermosillo, Mexico, and has sold it since late 2020 as a 2021 model.

Every Bronco Sport so far belongs to the same generation, refreshed for 2025 with a new face and much bigger screens. This guide has 4 chapters. Start with the years you're shopping, or go straight to the checklist if you're about to look at one.

## Quick picks

- **Best value:** a documented 2021–2024 Big Bend with the 1.5-liter, with every fuel-injector recall done.
- **Best all-rounder:** a 2025 or later Big Bend or Outer Banks, for the 13.2-inch screen and SYNC 4.
- **Best for trails:** a Badlands. Its 2.0-liter and twin-clutch rear drive unit make it a different vehicle underneath.
- **Quickest:** also the Badlands. Car and Driver ran a 2021 Badlands to 60 mph in 5.9 seconds.
- **Think twice if** you need a big back seat or plan to tow more than 1,225 kg (2,700 lb). A Bronco, an Explorer or a midsize truck will suit you better.
