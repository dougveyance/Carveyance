Announced in November 2020 and delivered from February 2022, the E-Transit was Ford's most sensible electric bet: the standard Transit body — same racks, same shelving, same upfits — with a 68-kWh usable battery under the floor and a 266-hp, 317-lb-ft motor at the back axle. The launch range of 126 EPA miles drew scoffs from people who don't run vans and more than 10,000 pre-orders from 300 fleets who do, because the average American delivery route is a fraction of that and depot charging makes every morning a full tank. The 2024 update grew the pack to 89 kWh usable and the range to 159 miles, and the biggest fleet of all wears blue: the US Postal Service put thousands of E-Transits into mail service as the first big wave of its electric fleet.

It drives better than any work van has a right to. Edmunds clocked 0–60 in 8.4 seconds with a 3.7-second 0–30 that makes it the quickest thing between stoplights with a ladder rack, the floor-mounted battery settles the unloaded ride that makes empty cargo vans skittish, and Pro Power Onboard turns the pack into a jobsite generator. Every E-Transit is a single series — call it a T-350 at 9,500 lb GVWR — in the same roof and length choices as the gas van, rear-drive only.

## Buying one used

The math is the whole story. Off-lease 2022–23 vans with the 126-mile battery have depreciated hard — fleet EVs always do — and if your duty cycle is 60 or 80 miles a day with overnight charging, they are the cheapest cost-per-mile Transits ever sold: no oil changes, no plugs, regenerative braking that makes pads last forever. Check the battery's state of health through a Ford dealer or an OBD report, confirm the 11.3-kW AC onboard charger suits your depot or home circuit (DC fast charging peaks at a modest 115 kW — this is not a road-trip vehicle), and treat a van that spent its life DC-charging to full as worth a harder look at the health report.

Skip it if your routes are regional, your winters are brutal and uncharged, or you tow heavy — cold and trailers both eat range the 68-kWh van can't spare. For everyone else, the discount does the deciding.
