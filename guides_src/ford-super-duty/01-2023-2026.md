The 2023 redesign kept the recipe — steel frame, aluminum body, V8s only — and turned every dial to the stop. There is no 6-cylinder anything in a Super Duty: the lineup runs from a 405-hp 6.8-liter gas V8 to the High Output 6.7 Power Stroke, 500 hp and 1,200 lb-ft, every one through the 10-speed. For 2026 the retail ladder is XL, XLT, Lariat, King Ranch and Platinum, with an F-250 XL SuperCrew opening at $48,890 and a loaded High Output Platinum listing within sight of $100,000.

## The 4 engines

The **6.8 gas** (405 hp) is the fleet-spec value play, a destroked version of the 7.3 and completely adequate for plows, service bodies and sub-4,536-kg (10,000-lb) trailers. The **7.3 "Godzilla"** (430 hp, 485 lb-ft) is the enthusiast's gas engine, pushrod-simple, DEF-free and happy on regular. The **standard 6.7 Power Stroke** (475 hp, 1,050 lb-ft) is the default for anyone who tows weekly. The **High Output 6.7** (500 hp, 1,200 lb-ft) exists for max-tow work and bragging rights, and does both without strain. For 2026, Ford widened the standard diesel's availability to the F-250 Tremor and brought back the 6.75-foot bed for SuperCabs.

## Which truck, which axle

An F-250 handles what most private owners actually tow. The F-350 single-rear-wheel adds payload for heavy pin weights; the dually and the diesel-only F-450 are for fifth-wheel and gooseneck work where the trailer is the business. Max-tow numbers — up to 18,144 kg (40,000 lb) by gooseneck on a correctly equipped F-450 — depend entirely on configuration, so spec from the trailer backward: loaded trailer weight, pin or tongue weight, then the axle, wheel and package that carry it with margin. The door-jamb payload label outranks every brochure.

## The clever hardware

This generation civilized heavy towing. Onboard Scales read payload through the suspension and show it in the taillights, Smart Hitch coaches tongue weight, Pro Trailer Hitch Assist backs the ball under the coupler by itself, and the camera suite sees down both sides of a 40-foot trailer. Tremor packages bring 35-inch rubber and off-road hardware to a 3-quarter-ton, and Pro Power Onboard turns the bed into a generator. None of it replaces knowing your weights; all of it lowers the stress of hauling them.
