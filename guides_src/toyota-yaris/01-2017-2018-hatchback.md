For its last 2 years in the US, the Toyota-designed Yaris hatchback was an old car doing a simple job well. It's tiny, at 155.5 inches long, easy to park and easy to see out of, with a 3-door or 5-door body and a hatch that swallows more than a small sedan's trunk. Toyota built it in France for the US market, and for 2018 renamed it the Yaris Liftback to tell it apart from the Mazda-built Yaris iA sedan. It wasn't sold here for 2019.

## How it drives

The engine is a 1.5-liter 4-cylinder with 106 hp and 103 lb-ft, the same output as the Mazda-built Yarises, but the gearboxes are older. You get a 5-speed manual or a 4-speed automatic, which was standard on the LE. The automatic is the weak point: with only 4 gears, the engine works hard on the freeway and the car feels slow. Car and Driver ran a 2016 Yaris LE 5-door automatic from 0 to 60 mph in 10.2 seconds.

[[chart:mpg]]

Around town it's light on its feet and pleasant to drive. On long freeway runs it's noisy and busy, and passing takes planning. That's the trade for a car this small and this cheap.

## Living with it

For 2017, Toyota made its Toyota Safety Sense C suite standard on every Yaris: automatic emergency braking, lane departure alert and automatic high beams. Toyota said it was the first entry-level subcompact to make those features standard across the range. The 2018 update added new front and rear styling, upgraded equipment and 9 airbags, including front-passenger seat-cushion airbags.

The cargo area holds 15.3 cubic feet in the 3-door and 15.6 in the 5-door, and the rear seats fold. The back seat is fine for children and short trips, tight for adults. The infotainment is basic, with no Apple CarPlay or Android Auto.

The 2018 hatchback started at $15,635 for an L 3-door manual, about $20,900 in 2026 dollars, and topped out at $19,060 for an SE 5-door automatic, about $25,500.

## What to check before you buy

These cars are simple, but cheap cars are often neglected. Ask for the oil-change history, check the tires, brakes and suspension for wear, and look for repaired curb damage. On a manual, check the clutch for slipping. On an automatic, make sure the shifts are prompt and smooth. Run the VIN for the side-airbag wiring recall on 2017 cars. The full list is in [the used checklist](../used-checklist/).

## The verdict

The Toyota-built Yaris hatchback is a sensible, cheap city car with standard emergency braking and a useful hatch. Choose the manual if you can, because the 4-speed automatic is its biggest weakness. If you drive mostly on the freeway, or want a more modern feel, the Mazda-built cars in [the next chapter](../mazda-built/) are the better buy.

::: curiosity Made in France
The Yaris hatchbacks sold in the US in these years came from Toyota's factory in France, and Toyota's US press materials made a point of it, under the heading "Euro Designed, Euro Built."
:::
