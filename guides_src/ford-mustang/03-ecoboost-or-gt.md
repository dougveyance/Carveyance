Every Mustang in this guide is a front-engine, rear-wheel-drive car, and the choice that shapes it most is under the hood. The 2.3-liter EcoBoost is a turbocharged 4-cylinder. The GT has Ford's 5.0-liter V8, a naturally aspirated engine that revs past 7,000 rpm. They make very different cars, and the right one depends less on numbers than on how you'll use it.

## Power, side by side

[[chart:power]]

The gap has stayed wide in both generations. A 2018–2023 EcoBoost makes 310 hp and a GT 460 hp; on the 2024–2026 car it's 315 and 480. But the EcoBoost's torque arrives low in the rev range, so it feels stronger in everyday driving than the gap suggests. Car and Driver ran a 2018 EcoBoost with the manual to 60 mph in 5.1 seconds, which would have been V8 territory a generation earlier.

## What each one is like to live with

The EcoBoost is the lighter-feeling, more economical car, and the 2020–2023 High Performance Package raised it to 330 hp with a sharper chassis. Its sound is the weak point; it's a 4-cylinder, and no amount of active exhaust makes it sound like an 8. The GT is louder and thirstier, and it usually costs more to insure, so get quotes for both before you decide. Its throttle response and soundtrack are why many buyers want a Mustang in the first place.

On the 2024–2026 car there's another difference: the EcoBoost comes only with the 10-speed automatic. If you want a manual Mustang built after 2023, it has to be a GT or a Dark Horse.

## Running costs

[[chart:fuelcost]]

At 15,000 miles a year and $4 a gallon, a 2024–2026 EcoBoost uses about $2,300 of fuel, against about $3,200 for a GT automatic and $3,500 for a Dark Horse. Fuel isn't the only difference. V8 cars tend to wear wider, more expensive tires, and a Performance Pack adds bigger brakes that cost more to service.

## Which to choose

| If you… | Choose |
|---|---|
| Commute most days and want the look | EcoBoost |
| Want a manual on a 2024 or later car | GT or Dark Horse |
| Plan track days | GT with the Performance Pack, or a Mach 1 or Dark Horse |
| Want the classic Mustang sound | GT |
| Need the lowest running costs | EcoBoost |
