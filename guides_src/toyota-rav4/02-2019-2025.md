For 2019, Toyota threw out the soft, rounded RAV4 and replaced it with something that looked like a small truck. The fifth-generation RAV4 has square wheel arches, an upright nose and a much tougher stance, and it sold in enormous numbers. Underneath, it's built on Toyota's TNGA-K platform, and the body is 57% stiffer than before. This chapter covers the gas model. The hybrid and the plug-in Prime have chapters of their own.

## What changed

The gas engine is a new 2.5-liter 4-cylinder with 203 hp and 184 lb-ft of torque, and it drives through an 8-speed automatic in place of the old 6-speed. Front-wheel drive is standard and all-wheel drive is optional. Some grades use Toyota's Dynamic Torque Vectoring all-wheel drive, which can send power to whichever rear wheel has more grip and disconnects the rear driveline on the highway to save fuel.

The 2019 RAV4 started at $25,500 for an LE with front-wheel drive, about $33,600 in 2026 dollars, and all-wheel drive added $1,400. Toyota Safety Sense 2.0 was standard, and Apple CarPlay arrived at last. The wheelbase grew by 1.2 inches to 105.9 inches, most of it going to rear legroom.

The gas RAV4 launched as an LE, XLE, XLE Premium, Adventure and Limited. The Adventure has its own grille, a little more ground clearance and torque vectoring all-wheel drive. For 2020, Toyota added the TRD Off-Road, with a TRD-tuned suspension and torque vectoring all-wheel drive as standard, and Android Auto joined CarPlay across the range. For 2023, every RAV4 got Toyota's newer Audio Multimedia system, with an 8-inch screen as standard and a 10.5-inch screen on the TRD Off-Road and Limited, plus an available 12.3-inch digital gauge cluster.

The 2025 RAV4 was the last one sold with a gas engine only.

## The numbers

[[chart:fuelcost]]

The EPA rated the front-drive LE at 30 mpg combined, a clear step up from the old car's 26. All-wheel drive costs little: most AWD versions are rated at 29 or 30 mpg. Most gas RAV4s can tow 680 kg (1,500 lb). The Adventure and TRD Off-Road are rated for 1,588 kg (3,500 lb), which makes them the gas RAV4s to buy if you pull a small trailer.

## How it drives

The fifth-generation RAV4 feels more solid than the old one, and it rides and handles better. The steering is light but accurate, and the body doesn't wallow. The new engine is stronger, though it's also coarse and loud when you rev it, and the 8-speed automatic can be slow to kick down. Testers generally found the hybrid quicker and quieter, which is why it's worth considering even if you don't care about fuel.

The cabin is practical, with big knobs for the climate controls, lots of storage and a large cargo area. Early cars use a smaller screen and older software. The 2023 update is a real improvement if you use your phone through the car a lot.

## What to check

- **Roof rail leaks (program 22TE05):** On 858,200 2019–2021 RAV4s, plus hybrids and early Primes, the seals on the roof rail mounting clips can deteriorate and let water in. Toyota set up a customer support program to cover the repair. Check the headliner, the front footwells and under the carpets for water stains or damp, and ask whether the work was done.
- **Front passenger airbag sensor (23V-865):** About 249,800 2020–2021 RAV4s were part of a December 2023 recall. A sensor in the front passenger seat can short-circuit, so the system may not detect an adult passenger and the passenger airbag may not deploy. Dealers inspect and replace the sensor as needed.
- **Instrument panel software (25V-595):** Certain 2023–2025 RAV4s were part of a September 2025 Toyota and Lexus recall. At startup, the instrument panel may fail to show the speed and some warning lights. The fix is a free software update.
- **Rearview camera software (25V-744):** Certain 2023–2025 RAV4s with Toyota's Panoramic View Monitor were part of an October 2025 recall. The rear camera image can freeze or go blank when you shift into reverse. The fix is a free software update.

On any car you're considering, run the VIN on [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) and ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Which one to buy

- **LE or XLE:** the value picks. An XLE adds the features most people want.
- **XLE Premium:** a well-equipped middle choice.
- **Adventure or TRD Off-Road:** the ones to buy if you tow or drive on dirt roads, with 1,588 kg (3,500 lb) of towing and torque vectoring all-wheel drive.
- **Limited:** the most comfortable gas RAV4.
- **2023–2025:** worth paying a little more for if you want the newer screen and software.

## The verdict

The gas RAV4 is a good car that's overshadowed by its hybrid sibling. If you find a well-priced Adventure or TRD Off-Road, or you simply want the simplest RAV4, it's a solid buy. If not, the hybrid costs a little more and gives you more of everything.
