For 2026, Toyota redesigned the RAV4 from the ground up and made a decision that would have seemed bold a few years ago: no more gas-only version. Every sixth-generation RAV4 is either a hybrid or a plug-in hybrid. The good news for buyers is that the base hybrid costs little more than the last gas-only RAV4 did. The 2026 RAV4 Hybrid LE starts at $31,900 before the $1,450 destination fee.

## What changed

The RAV4 Hybrid moves to Toyota's fifth-generation hybrid system. It makes 226 hp with front-wheel drive, which is offered on the hybrid for the first time, and 236 hp with all-wheel drive. The plug-in, called the RAV4 Plug-in Hybrid now that the Prime name is gone, uses Toyota's sixth-generation plug-in system, with a 22.7-kWh battery, all-wheel drive as standard and 324 hp.

Toyota now groups the grades into 3 styles. The Core models are the LE, XLE Premium and Limited. The Rugged model is the Woodland. The Sport models are the SE, XSE and the first-ever RAV4 GR Sport, which comes only as a plug-in, with GR-tuned suspension and steering. Every RAV4 gets a new multimedia system, and the body and chassis are stiffer than before.

Prices for the hybrid, before destination: LE $31,900, SE $34,700, XLE Premium $36,100, Woodland $39,900, XSE $41,300 and Limited $43,300. The Plug-in Hybrid costs $41,500 as an SE, $45,300 as a Woodland, $47,200 as an XSE and $48,500 as a GR Sport. The hybrids are built in Kentucky and in Woodstock and Cambridge, Ontario, and the plug-ins in Japan.

For 2027, the RAV4 carries over with small price increases. The Hybrid LE with front-wheel drive starts at $32,300 and the Plug-in Hybrid SE at $42,100.

## The numbers

[[chart:mpg26]]

The front-drive LE, SE and XLE Premium are rated at 43 mpg combined, and the all-wheel-drive LE and SE at 42. The XLE Premium AWD, XSE and Limited are rated at 41, and the Woodland at 38. All of them beat the 2019–2025 hybrid.

The Plug-in Hybrid's electric range jumps. Toyota first estimated 52 miles for the SE and XSE, 49 for the Woodland and 48 for the GR Sport, and toyota.com now lists EPA estimates of 54 miles for the SE and 49 for the GR Sport. Chart 4 compares it with the old Prime. Edmunds timed a GR Sport from 0 to 60 mph in 5.6 seconds and a front-drive Hybrid XLE Premium in 7.8 seconds.

Towing depends on the version. The LE with all-wheel drive is rated for 794 kg (1,750 lb), and the XLE Premium, SE and Woodland with all-wheel drive for 1,588 kg (3,500 lb).

## How it drives

The new RAV4 is quieter and more refined than the last one, and the hybrid feels stronger, especially from a stop. Reviewers still note some engine drone when you accelerate hard, a trait of Toyota's hybrids, and light steering. The plug-in is genuinely quick, and the GR Sport adds tighter handling to go with it.

Front-wheel drive is the efficiency pick for drivers in mild climates. All-wheel drive costs about 1 mpg and adds traction in rain and snow. The Woodland is the one to choose for gravel roads and trailheads, though, like every RAV4, it's not built for serious off-roading.

## What to check

The 2026 RAV4 is new enough that most examples are still under full factory warranty: 3 years or 36,000 miles basic, 5 years or 60,000 miles powertrain, 8 years or 100,000 miles for hybrid components and 10 years or 150,000 miles for the hybrid battery, which transfers to the next owner.

- **Plug-in Hybrid power loss (26V-659):** In October 2026, Toyota recalled 30,978 2026 RAV4 Plug-in Hybrids built between July 22, 2025, and August 19, 2026. The motor generator control unit may not finish its startup routine correctly, and at higher speeds the plug-in hybrid system can reduce power or shut down, cutting drive power. The fix is a free software update.
- **Everything else:** It's too early for long-term data. Run the VIN on [NHTSA's recall lookup](https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls) before you buy, since new recalls can appear at any time, and ask for proof that each repair was done.

## Which one to buy

- **Hybrid LE FWD:** the cheapest and thriftiest RAV4, at 43 mpg.
- **Hybrid XLE Premium:** the sweet spot for equipment, with 1,588 kg (3,500 lb) of towing when you choose AWD.
- **Woodland:** for gravel roads and campsites, with 1,588 kg (3,500 lb) of towing.
- **Plug-in Hybrid SE:** the best value if you can charge at home, with more than 50 miles of electric range.
- **GR Sport:** the quickest and most fun RAV4 ever, and the most expensive.

## The verdict

The 2026 RAV4 is the best RAV4 yet, and it doesn't ask you to pay much for the privilege. The base hybrid costs little more than the old gas model, uses far less fuel and drives better. If you can plug in, the Plug-in Hybrid is the better car still. Check that the software recall is done on any plug-in.

::: curiosity A RAV4 You Can't Buy With Gas Alone
For most of its life, the RAV4 was a gas-engine car first, with a hybrid added in 2016 and a plug-in in 2021. For 2026, it reversed the order completely. The base engine is now a hybrid, and front-wheel drive, which the hybrid never had in the US, is the new way to save money.
:::
