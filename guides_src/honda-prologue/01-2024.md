Honda needed an electric SUV for America before its own were ready, so it bought the hardware from General Motors. The Prologue uses GM's Ultium platform and shares its 85-kWh battery and motors with the Chevrolet Blazer EV, and GM builds it in Ramos Arizpe, Mexico. Production began in January 2024, and the first cars reached Honda dealers in March. The styling, inside and out, was done at Honda's design studio in Los Angeles.

## The drivetrain

[[chart:power]]

The front-wheel-drive Prologue has a single motor, rated at 212 hp and 236 lb-ft. All-wheel drive adds a second motor at the rear, for 288 hp and 333 lb-ft. The EPA rates the front-drive EX and Touring at 296 miles, the all-wheel-drive EX and Touring at 281, and the Elite, on 21-inch wheels, at 273. Car and Driver ran a 2024 Elite to 60 mph in 5.9 seconds. It's a heavy car, at 2,237 kg (4,932 lb) for a front-drive EX and 2,392 kg (5,273 lb) for the Elite, and towing is limited to 680 kg (1,500 lb).

## Charging

The Prologue takes up to 150 kW at a DC fast charger, enough, Honda says, to add about 65 miles in 10 minutes or go from 20 to 80% in about 35 minutes, in good conditions. On a 240-volt Level 2 charger at home it adds about 34 miles of range per hour. It has a CCS fast-charge port; with Honda's NACS-to-CCS adapter it can also use Tesla Superchargers.

## The trims

The range was EX, Touring and Elite. The EX cost $47,400 ($50,800 in 2026 dollars), the Touring $51,700 ($55,400), and all-wheel drive added $3,000 ($3,200) to either. The Elite, all-wheel drive only, cost $57,900 ($62,100). Every Prologue has wireless Apple CarPlay and Android Auto, Google built-in, an 11-inch driver display and an 11.3-inch touchscreen, and the wheelbase, at 121.8 inches, is longer than an Odyssey's, so the rear seat is roomy. The cargo area holds 25.2 cubic feet behind the rear seat and 57.7 with it folded. Most 2024s qualified for the federal $7,500 tax credit when new.

## What to check before you buy

Some 2024 Prologues were recalled for a right front lower control arm that could fracture (24V540). Software faults could leave the instrument panel or the rearview camera blank (26V112), and on 2024–2025 cars the rearview camera can show a distorted or blank image; Honda replaces the camera (26V306). Ask for proof that each was done, and see [the used checklist](../used-checklist/) for the battery and charging checks.

## The verdict

A 2024 Prologue makes sense if it's priced well below a 2025, because the 2025 has more range and power for the same original price. Pick front-wheel drive for range, all-wheel drive for snow, and skip the Elite unless you want its 21-inch wheels and extra equipment, which cost range and tire money.
