No cheap car ever got a better performance arc. The **SVT Focus** (2002–04) was America's first proper modern hot hatch: 170 hand-tuned Zetec horsepower, a 6-speed Getrag, real brakes and a verified 6.9 to 60. The **Focus ST** (2013–18) raised it to 252 turbocharged horsepower, manual only, 6 seconds flat and 155 mph. And the **Focus RS** (2016–18) ended the story at 350 hp with torque-vectoring all-wheel drive, a 4.6-second 0–60 and a button marked Drift Mode — the first RS Ford ever sold here, after 40 years of watching Europe keep them.

## Shopping each one

**SVT:** rare now, cheap still, and climbing. Most led hard lives; pay up for stock, documented cars and treat ratty ones as parts. The Getrag gearbox and unique suspension bits are the hunt items.

**ST:** the value sweet spot. Thousands exist, all manual, most in the low-to-mid teens. Check for clutch abuse, curbed wheels and tasteless tunes — a stock, adult-owned ST is one of the best driver's cars $15,000 buys. Recaro seats split opinions; sit before you buy.

**RS:** the keeper. Early 2016–17 engines suffered documented head-gasket failures that Ford repaired under warranty — insist on the service history showing the fix or a later build. The rear drive unit should engage silently, Drift Mode is best demonstrated by the seller on video rather than your test drive, and Nitrous Blue carries a real premium. Clean stock cars have stopped depreciating; this is the one to buy right, not cheap.
